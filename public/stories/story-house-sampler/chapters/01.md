# The First Shelf

Quill ran one hoof along the blue shelves and smiled. Every book had arrived in a different way. Some were only a few pages long. Others were thick enough to need two hooves and a very comfortable chair.

<!-- block:empty-space -->
There was still an empty space at the end of the shelf.

“That one,” Quill said, “is for the next story.”

<!-- block:catalogue-promise -->
For now, the little book simply proves that Story House can find a story, open one chapter, and remember stable places inside it without loading the rest of the library.
