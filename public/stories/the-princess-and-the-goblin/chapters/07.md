# Curdie's Clue

Curdie returned underground.

This time he was even more careful.

He followed goblin voices through twisting tunnels and listened whenever he could.

He learned that the goblins had strange animals living with them.

Some had too many legs.

Some seemed made from mixtures of different creatures.

Curdie decided he did not want to meet any of them in the dark.

He also discovered that the goblins were digging.

Not randomly.

They were making new tunnels.

<!-- block:what-the-goblins-want -->
Curdie heard enough conversations to piece together part of the plan.

Prince Harelip wanted Princess Irene.

The goblins intended to break into the King's house from below and carry her away.

There was more.

Another group was digging towards the human mines.

Curdie did not yet understand why.

He needed to tell someone.

Before he could leave, the goblins discovered him.

<!-- block:curdie-captured -->
Curdie ran through the tunnels.

He sang rhymes.

He stamped on goblin feet when they came too close.

For a while, it worked.

Then he was surrounded.

The goblins took his lamp and tied him up.

Curdie was left alone underground.

He tried to loosen the ropes.

No luck.

He shouted.

Nobody answered.

The mine above was too far away.

For the first time since he had begun spying on the goblins, Curdie wondered whether he had been too confident.

Then, somewhere in the darkness, something touched his hand.

It felt like a thread.
