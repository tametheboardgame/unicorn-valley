# The Goblins Attack

The goblins finally moved.

They entered the King's house through hidden passages beneath the cellars.

Guards heard noises.

Servants ran through corridors.

Doors slammed.

Curdie arrived and helped organise the defence.

He knew the goblins' weakness.

Their feet.

The defenders stamped, struck at their shoes and drove them back whenever they could.

But in the confusion, Irene disappeared.

Curdie searched her room.

Empty.

His heart sank.

He feared the goblins had taken her.

<!-- block:curdies-guide -->
Then something brushed his hand.

A thread.

Curdie could not see it.

But he could feel it.

He froze.

For the first time he understood what Irene had meant.

He followed.

The invisible thread led him away from the house.

Up the mountain.

Towards his own home.

Curdie ran.

Inside the cottage, his mother sat beside the fire.

Princess Irene was asleep in her arms.

The thread had led Irene to safety before the goblins reached her.

<!-- block:believing-late -->
Curdie stood silently.

He had doubted Irene because he could not see what she saw.

Now he was following the same invisible guide.

When Irene woke, Curdie apologised properly.

This time he did not add explanations.

He simply said he had been wrong.

Irene forgave him.

But the danger was not finished.

Below them, the goblins had begun the second part of their plan.
