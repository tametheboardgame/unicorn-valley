# Irene's Clue

The mine tunnels were black.

Irene had no miner's lamp.

She could not see the silver thread.

She could only feel it.

One step at a time, she followed.

The passage narrowed.

Sometimes Irene had to crawl.

Sometimes she was sure the thread was leading her deeper rather than out.

She wanted to cry.

She continued anyway.

<!-- block:behind-the-rock -->
At last the thread stopped at a wall of stone.

Irene felt around.

Nothing.

She pulled gently.

The thread seemed to pass straight through the rock.

“That cannot be right.”

Then she heard a faint sound on the other side.

A voice.

Curdie.

Irene began moving loose stones.

Curdie heard her and helped from his side.

Together they opened a gap.

Irene squeezed through.

Curdie stared at her.

Of all the people he expected to find in the goblin caves, Princess Irene was nowhere on the list.

<!-- block:curdie-does-not-believe -->
Irene explained about the thread.

Curdie could not see it.

He thought she had simply found him by luck.

Irene was hurt.

She had risked everything to follow something invisible, and now Curdie did not believe her.

Still, there was no time to argue.

The goblins could return.

Irene touched the thread again.

It was leading onward.

“Come with me.”

Curdie hesitated.

Then he followed the princess who had come into the mountain to rescue him.
