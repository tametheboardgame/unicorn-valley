# Princess Irene

Princess Irene was eight years old and lived in a great old house on the side of a mountain.

Her father was the King, but he did not live there all the time. The mountain country was wild and beautiful, and Irene stayed in the house with her nurse Lootie and the other servants.

There was one thing nobody liked to discuss after dark.

Goblins.

Long ago, strange people had gone underground and stayed there. Their children and grandchildren had changed over the years until they became goblins.

They disliked the people who lived above ground.

They especially disliked the King.

Lootie told Irene never to stay outside after sunset.

Irene listened.

Usually.

<!-- block:lost-in-the-passages -->
One rainy day Irene could not go outside at all.

She became bored with her toys and wandered through a door she had hardly noticed before.

Behind it was an old staircase.

Irene climbed.

At the top she found corridors full of doors.

She turned one corner, then another.

Soon she had no idea where the stairs were.

The house became very quiet.

Irene tried to be brave.

Then she became frightened enough to cry.

After a while she heard a sound.

A soft humming noise.

Someone was singing somewhere beyond the doors.

Irene followed it.

At the end of a passage she found a room she had never seen before.

Inside sat a beautiful old lady with long white hair, spinning thread at a wheel.

The lady looked up.

“I have been waiting for you, Irene.”
