# The Goblin Plan

While Curdie was trapped, the goblins continued preparing.

Their plan had two parts.

First, they would enter the King's house through underground passages and take Princess Irene.

Second, they would break barriers holding back great amounts of underground water.

The water would flood the human mines.

The goblins believed they could destroy their enemies below and retreat safely into higher passages.

It was a cruel plan.

It was also complicated.

Complicated plans have many places where things can go wrong.

<!-- block:above-ground -->
Above ground, Irene knew nothing of this.

She did know that Curdie had not been seen.

She remembered the boy who had protected her from the goblins.

She remembered the ring.

That night Irene placed it beneath her pillow.

She reached for the invisible thread.

There it was.

She followed.

<!-- block:where-the-thread-leads -->
The thread led out of her room.

Down corridors.

Out of the house.

Across the dark mountainside.

Irene became frightened.

Still she followed.

Then the thread led towards the mine.

That seemed impossible.

Her grandmother would surely not send her underground.

Irene nearly turned back.

The thread tightened gently against her finger.

She remembered the promise she had made.

Not necessarily where you think safety is.

Irene stepped into the darkness.
