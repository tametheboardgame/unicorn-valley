# The Wicked Witch of the West

The Wicked Witch of the West had only one eye, but it could see an astonishing distance.

She saw Dorothy and her friends approaching.

The Witch sent creatures to stop them.

Again and again the travellers protected one another and continued.

At last the Witch used her most powerful tool: a golden cap that summoned the Winged Monkeys.

The monkeys scattered the group and carried Dorothy, Toto and the Lion to the Witch's castle.

<!-- block:at-the-castle -->
The Witch could not harm Dorothy while the girl wore the silver shoes.

So instead she ordered Dorothy to work in the castle and waited for a chance to steal them.

The Lion was kept nearby, but he refused to obey the Witch.

Dorothy secretly brought him food.

One day the Witch tricked Dorothy and managed to take one silver shoe.

Dorothy was furious.

She grabbed a bucket of water and threw it.

The water splashed across the Witch.

<!-- block:the-witch-melts -->
The Witch screamed.

She began to shrink.

Dorothy stared in astonishment.

The Wicked Witch of the West could not bear water.

Within moments she had melted away completely.

Dorothy had not known what the water would do.

She stood silently for a moment.

Then she rescued the Lion and found the Tin Woodman and Scarecrow.

The people of the Witch's country were free.

They helped repair Dorothy's friends and gave them supplies for the journey.

Dorothy placed the golden cap on her head.

She did not yet know how important it would become.

Then the friends turned east towards the Emerald City.
