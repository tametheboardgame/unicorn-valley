# The Emerald City

At last the travellers saw a green glow on the horizon.

The Emerald City.

Its walls shone in the sunlight.

At the gate, a Guardian fitted each visitor with green spectacles and locked them in place.

“Everything in the city is green,” he explained.

Inside, the streets glittered.

The houses were green.

The clothes were green.

Even the sweets in the shop windows were green.

<!-- block:green-everywhere -->
Dorothy and her friends were taken to the palace.

The Wizard would see them, but only one at a time.

When Dorothy entered, she saw a gigantic floating head.

The Scarecrow saw a beautiful lady.

The Tin Woodman saw a terrible beast.

The Lion saw a ball of fire.

Each visitor heard the same answer.

The Wizard would grant their wishes only if they first defeated the Wicked Witch of the West.

<!-- block:an-impossible-task -->
The friends met again outside.

Nobody wanted to fight a wicked witch.

But Dorothy wanted to return to Kansas.

The Scarecrow wanted brains.

The Tin Woodman wanted a heart.

The Lion wanted courage.

So they left the safety of the Emerald City and travelled west.

“I don't like this,” said the Lion.

“Neither do I,” said Dorothy.

The Scarecrow adjusted his hat.

“Perhaps being brave doesn't mean liking the dangerous part.”

The Lion thought about that as they walked.
