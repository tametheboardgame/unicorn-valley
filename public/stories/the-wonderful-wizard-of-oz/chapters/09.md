# South to Glinda

The people of the Emerald City told Dorothy about Glinda, the Good Witch of the South.

Perhaps Glinda would know how to send her home.

So Dorothy, Toto, the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman and Lion set off again.

The journey south took them through places stranger than anything they had seen before.

They crossed a country where tiny people and buildings seemed to be made of delicate china.

They passed through a forest where enormous trees tried to block the road.

They climbed steep hills guarded by odd creatures who did not want travellers passing through.

Each time, the friends found a way forward together.

<!-- block:glinda-the-good -->
At last they reached Glinda's palace.

Glinda listened carefully as Dorothy told the whole story.

“The cyclone, the yellow road, the Wizard, the Witch, the balloon. Everything.”

Glinda smiled.

“You have had the power to return home for some time.”

Dorothy stared at her.

“How?”

“The silver shoes.”

They could carry their wearer anywhere in only a few steps.

Dorothy looked down at them.

“All this time?”

“All this time.”

Dorothy was happy, but then she looked at her friends.

The Scarecrow would return to rule the Emerald City.

The Tin Woodman had friends waiting in the west.

<!-- block:lion-king -->
The Lion had found creatures who wanted him as their king.

They all had places in Oz now.

Dorothy hugged each of them.

She had found wonderful friends on the road.

But she knew where she belonged.
