# Gifts from Oz

Oz understood that knowing you already possessed something was not always enough.

So he prepared special gifts.

For the Scarecrow he made a mixture of bran, pins and needles and placed it inside his head.

“Now you have plenty of bran-new brains.”

The Scarecrow was delighted.

<!-- block:the-tin-mans-heart -->
For the Tin Woodman, Oz made a beautiful silk heart stuffed with sawdust.

He placed it carefully inside the Tin Woodman's chest.

“Is it a kind heart?” asked the Tin Woodman.

“An excellent one.”

The Tin Woodman smiled.

<!-- block:the-lions-courage -->
For the Lion, Oz poured a green drink.

“What is it?”

“Courage.”

The Lion swallowed it.

He immediately stood taller.

Dorothy knew that none of these gifts had created the qualities her friends wanted.

But somehow having the symbols helped them believe what their journey had already proved.

<!-- block:the-balloon -->
Oz planned to take Dorothy home in the same way he had arrived: by balloon.

The people of the Emerald City helped build one.

On the day of departure, Dorothy climbed into the basket.

Then Toto spotted a kitten and jumped out.

Dorothy ran after him.

At that exact moment the ropes holding the balloon snapped.

Oz floated into the sky.

“Come back!” Dorothy cried.

“I can't!” Oz called.

The balloon disappeared.

Dorothy sat down and hugged Toto.

They had come so far.

And she was still in Oz.
