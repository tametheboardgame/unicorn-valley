# The Wizard Revealed

The travellers returned to the Emerald City expecting the Wizard to keep his promise.

Instead, he avoided them.

They waited.

And waited.

Finally the Lion roared so loudly that Toto jumped backwards into a screen.

The screen fell over.

Behind it stood a small old man.

Everyone stared.

“Who are you?” Dorothy asked.

The man looked embarrassed.

“I am Oz.”

“But where is the great Head?”

“That was me.”

“The beautiful lady?”

“Me.”

“The terrible beast?”

“Also me.”

“The ball of fire?”

The old man sighed.

“Me again.”

Oz admitted that he was not a powerful wizard at all.

Long ago he had arrived in Oz by accident in a balloon.

The people assumed he was a great magician.

He had been too frightened to tell them otherwise.

The Scarecrow was devastated.

“How will I ever get my brains?”

“You don't need them,” Oz said. “You learn something new every day.”

The Tin Woodman asked about his heart.

“You show kindness constantly.”

The Lion asked about courage.

“You face danger even while you are frightened.”

Dorothy folded her arms.

“What about Kansas?”

Oz looked thoughtful.

“That problem,” he said, “may actually require some work.”
