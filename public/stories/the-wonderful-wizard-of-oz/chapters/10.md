# Home to Kansas

Dorothy picked up Toto.

Glinda explained what to do.

“Knock the heels of the silver shoes together three times and tell them where you wish to go.”

Dorothy hugged the Scarecrow.

Then the Tin Woodman.

Then the Lion.

“I don't know how I would have reached the Emerald City without you.”

The Scarecrow smiled.

“We didn't exactly take the shortest route.”

The Tin Woodman wiped his eyes, although tears were not especially good for his joints.

The Lion tried to say goodbye without crying and failed.

Dorothy held Toto tightly.

Then she knocked the heels of the silver shoes together.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

“Take me home to Aunt Em.”

<!-- block:across-the-air -->
The world spun.

Dorothy felt herself rushing through the air.

The silver shoes slipped from her feet somewhere along the way.

Then suddenly she was standing on the Kansas prairie.

The bright colours of Oz were gone.

The yellow road was gone.

Toto barked.

Ahead stood a newly built farmhouse.

Aunt Em came running outside.

<!-- block:home -->
Dorothy ran into her arms.

“My darling child! Where in the world have you been?”

Dorothy looked across the familiar grey prairie.

She thought about Munchkins and witches.

A Scarecrow who believed he had no brains.

A Tin Woodman who believed he had no heart.

A Lion who believed he had no courage.

A Wizard who was not really a wizard.

And a pair of silver shoes that had known the way home all along.

Dorothy smiled.

“I've been in the Land of Oz.”

Aunt Em looked at her.

Dorothy hugged her again.

“Oh, Aunt Em,” she said. “I'm so glad to be home.”
