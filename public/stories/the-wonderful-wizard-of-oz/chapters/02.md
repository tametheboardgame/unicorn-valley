# The Yellow Brick Road

The yellow brick road wound through the beautiful country of the Munchkins.

Dorothy passed tidy houses painted blue and fields filled with corn.

People waved as she went by.

That evening she was welcomed into a farmhouse where everyone wanted to hear about Kansas and the house that had fallen from the sky.

The next morning Dorothy and Toto continued towards the Emerald City.

<!-- block:meeting-the-scarecrow -->
Beside a cornfield stood a Scarecrow fixed to a pole.

As Dorothy passed, one of its painted eyes winked.

Dorothy stopped.

“Did you just wink at me?”

“Certainly,” said the Scarecrow. “Would you mind helping me down?”

Dorothy lifted him from the pole.

The Scarecrow stretched his straw-filled arms and thanked her.

He explained that he had only recently been made.

“I don't mind being stuffed with straw,” he said. “But I have no brains.”

Dorothy told him about the Wizard of Oz.

“Perhaps he could give you some.”

The Scarecrow brightened.

He joined Dorothy and Toto on the yellow brick road.

<!-- block:what-brains-are-for -->
As they walked, Dorothy noticed that the Scarecrow was actually rather good at solving problems.

When they reached a ditch, he found a safe way across.

When the road became difficult, he thought carefully about which direction to take.

Dorothy wondered whether someone without brains should really be able to think of so many useful things.

The Scarecrow, however, remained certain.

“If I had brains,” he said, “I could be clever properly.”

Dorothy did not argue.

She was beginning to think that people in Oz might not always recognise what they already had.
