# Home Again

Peter stumbled through the doorway.

His mother stared at him.

He was muddy.

He was wet.

He had no shoes.

He had no jacket.

And his stomach still hurt from eating far too many vegetables.

Mrs Rabbit put down her basket.

“Peter.”

Peter's ears drooped.

“I went into Mr McGregor's garden.”

“I had guessed.”

<!-- block:a-quiet-evening -->
Mrs Rabbit dried him, wrapped him up warmly and put him to bed.

She made him a cup of camomile tea.

Peter drank it without complaining.

His sisters had bread, milk and blackberries for supper.

From his bed Peter could hear them talking and laughing.

He thought about the garden.

The vegetables had been delicious.

The chase had not.

Losing his clothes had definitely not been worth a few radishes.

<!-- block:tomorrow -->
Before he fell asleep, Peter decided that tomorrow he would go blackberry picking with Flopsy, Mopsy and Cotton-tail.

At least, that was what he decided tonight.

Mrs Rabbit looked at her sleeping son and smiled.

She knew Peter very well.

Tomorrow, she suspected, might require another conversation.
