# Lost and Found

Peter scrambled beneath flowerpots and around gardening tools.

He passed a cat sitting beside the pond.

The cat looked extremely interested in him.

Peter decided not to stop and introduce himself.

He kept running.

Soon he reached a wall.

Then another hedge.

Then rows of plants he did not recognise.

Everything looked different from the way it had when he entered.

Peter's heart sank.

He could not remember where the gate was.

<!-- block:asking-the-sparrows -->
A group of sparrows fluttered down nearby.

Peter told them what had happened.

The sparrows chirped and hopped excitedly.

One flew towards the far end of the garden.

Peter followed.

There, beyond a row of currant bushes, he saw it.

The gate.

<!-- block:finding-the-gate -->
Mr McGregor saw it too.

Peter ran.

Mr McGregor ran.

Peter reached the gate first.

He squeezed beneath it so quickly that his whiskers brushed the wood.

Then he was through.

He did not stop.

He raced across the field, down the lane and all the way back to the great fir tree.

Only when the burrow entrance appeared ahead did Peter finally slow down.
