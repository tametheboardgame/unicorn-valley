# Mrs Rabbit's Warning

Once upon a time there were four little rabbits called Flopsy, Mopsy, Cotton-tail and Peter.

They lived with their mother beneath the roots of a very large fir tree.

One morning Mrs Rabbit tied on her basket.

“I have to go out,” she told them. “You may play in the fields or down the lane.”

Then she looked particularly at Peter.

“But do not go into Mr McGregor's garden.”

Flopsy, Mopsy and Cotton-tail nodded at once.

Peter tried to look as though he had not been thinking about Mr McGregor's garden at all.

Mrs Rabbit explained that the garden was not a safe place for rabbits.

Mr McGregor grew rows and rows of vegetables there, and he did not appreciate unexpected visitors eating them.

“So stay out,” she said firmly.

“Yes, Mother,” said the four rabbits.

Mrs Rabbit set off through the wood.

Flopsy, Mopsy and Cotton-tail went down the lane to gather blackberries.

Peter watched them disappear.

Then he looked in the other direction.

Beyond the hedge stood Mr McGregor's garden.

Peter's ears lifted.

He had already made up his mind.
