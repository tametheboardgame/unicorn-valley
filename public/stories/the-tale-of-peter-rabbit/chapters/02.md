# Into the Garden

Peter ran straight to Mr McGregor's garden.

He squeezed beneath the gate and landed between two neat rows of vegetables.

For a moment he simply stared.

There were lettuces.

French beans.

Radishes.

Parsley.

Carrots.

Everything looked greener, crunchier and more delicious than anything Peter had ever seen.

<!-- block:among-the-vegetables -->
He ate some lettuce.

Then some beans.

Then several radishes.

Then a little more lettuce because he had forgotten how much he had already eaten.

Before long Peter was so full that he began to feel slightly uncomfortable.

“Perhaps,” he thought, “I should have stopped before the fourth radish.”

He wandered towards a patch of parsley, hoping it might settle his stomach.

That was when he heard footsteps.

Heavy footsteps.

Peter froze.

At the end of the row stood Mr McGregor.

He was holding a gardening tool.

He had also spotted Peter.
