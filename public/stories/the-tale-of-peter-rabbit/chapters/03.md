# Mr McGregor

“Stop!” shouted Mr McGregor.

Peter did the exact opposite.

He raced between the vegetable rows.

Mr McGregor ran after him.

Peter turned left.

Then right.

Then left again.

He lost one shoe among the cabbages.

The other disappeared near the potatoes.

Without them he could run faster, but now he was frightened and completely lost.

<!-- block:the-gooseberry-net -->
Peter dashed beneath a gooseberry bush.

His blue jacket caught in a net.

He pulled.

The buttons held fast.

Mr McGregor's footsteps came closer.

Peter wriggled out of the jacket and left it behind.

Now he had lost his shoes and his coat.

But at least he was still moving.

<!-- block:the-watering-can -->
Peter rushed into a tool shed and jumped into a large watering can.

It seemed like an excellent hiding place.

Unfortunately, there was water in the bottom.

Peter crouched there, soaked and shivering, while Mr McGregor searched nearby.

He tried not to sneeze.

His nose twitched.

He held his breath.

Then came an enormous:

“ATCHOO!”

Peter sprang out of the watering can and ran again.
