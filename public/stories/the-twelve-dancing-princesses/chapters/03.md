# Beneath the Palace

The staircase twisted deep beneath the palace.

The traveller stayed close behind the youngest princess.

A little too close.

At one sharp turn she accidentally stepped on the hem of the princess’s dress.

The youngest spun around.

“Someone tugged my skirt!”

“Nonsense,” said the eldest. “It caught on the railing.”

The traveller waited until they had gone ahead before continuing.

At the bottom of the stairs stood a forest unlike any she had ever seen.

<!-- block:the-silver-wood -->
Every leaf on every tree shone like silver.

When the princesses passed beneath the branches, the whole forest whispered and chimed.

The traveller carefully broke off one small silver twig.

Crack!

The youngest princess jumped.

“There! Did you hear that?”

“A branch broke,” said another sister. “Forests do that.”

Beyond the silver forest was another wood.

This one glittered gold.

The traveller took a tiny golden branch.

Crack!

The youngest princess stopped again.

“I know I heard something.”

Her sisters laughed and pulled her onward.

<!-- block:the-diamond-wood -->
Then came a third forest.

Its leaves sparkled like diamonds under a sky filled with strange blue stars.

The traveller had never seen anything so beautiful.

She almost forgot why she was there.

At last the trees opened onto the shore of a great underground lake.

Twelve little boats waited beside the water.

And standing in each boat was a finely dressed dancer.

The princesses climbed aboard.

The traveller slipped quietly into the boat with the youngest sister.

The dancer picked up his oars.

“This boat feels unusually heavy tonight,” he said.

The youngest princess looked over her shoulder.

The traveller held her breath beneath the invisible cloak.
