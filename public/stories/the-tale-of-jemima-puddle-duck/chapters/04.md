# Kep Asks Questions

The next afternoon Jemima collected the herbs from the garden.

She found two onions in the farmhouse kitchen.

As she waddled outside carrying everything, Kep the farm's collie dog spotted her.

Kep was good at noticing things.

Especially unusual things involving animals carrying onions.

“Where are you going?” he asked.

“To my nest.”

“What nest?”

“My secret one.”

Kep sat down.

Jemima realised immediately that she had said too much.

Kep asked where the nest was.

Jemima told him about the wood.

He asked who had shown it to her.

Jemima described the polite gentleman.

“Black ears?”

“Yes.”

“Long tail?”

“Yes.”

“Sandy whiskers?”

“Yes.”

Kep's ears stood up.

“And he asked you to bring herbs and onions?”

“Yes. For dinner.”

Kep looked at the bag.

Then at Jemima.

Then back at the bag.

“I think,” he said carefully, “I should come with you.”

“Oh, there is no need,” said Jemima. “He is extremely polite.”

“That,” said Kep, “is not what I am worried about.”

He asked Jemima for exact directions.

Then he ran down the lane to find help.

Jemima continued towards the wood, though she was beginning to feel less certain than before.
