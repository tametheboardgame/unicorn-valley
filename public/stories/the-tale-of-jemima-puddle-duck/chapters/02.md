# The Gentleman in the Wood

Near a tree stump sat a very elegant gentleman.

He had pointed black ears, sandy-coloured whiskers and a long bushy tail.

He was holding a newspaper.

Jemima tilted her head.

“Quack?”

The gentleman lowered the paper.

“What a pleasant surprise,” he said.

His voice was extremely polite.

Jemima explained that she was searching for somewhere quiet to make a nest.

The gentleman smiled.

“As it happens, I know exactly the place.”

Behind the trees stood a small tumble-down shed.

The gentleman opened the door.

Inside was a deep layer of soft feathers.

Jemima looked around.

There were rather a lot of feathers.

More feathers than she would have expected to find in an empty shed in the middle of a wood.

But the nest looked comfortable.

“This will do beautifully,” she said.

“I thought you might like it,” said the gentleman.

Jemima arranged the feathers carefully.

When she left for the evening, the gentleman promised to watch the nest until she returned.

He was so helpful.

So charming.

So very interested in ducks.

Jemima flew home feeling pleased with herself.

The gentleman watched her disappear over the trees.

Then he folded his newspaper and smiled.
