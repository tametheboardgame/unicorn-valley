# The Lobster Quadrille

The Gryphon and the Mock Turtle offered to show Alice a dance called the Lobster Quadrille.

They arranged themselves carefully.

“You begin by forming a line along the seashore,” explained the Mock Turtle.

“Then you advance twice.”

“Each with a lobster as a partner,” added the Gryphon.

“And then,” said the Mock Turtle, “you throw the lobsters out to sea.”

Alice thought this seemed rather unfair on the lobsters.

<!-- block:songs-and-stories -->
The two creatures danced without any actual lobsters.

Then the Mock Turtle sang a song about a whiting and a snail.

Alice tried to describe some of her own adventures.

She explained that she kept changing size and could no longer recite familiar poems correctly.

The Gryphon demanded a demonstration.

Alice tried.

The words came out completely wrong.

“That is certainly unusual,” said the Mock Turtle.

Before Alice could defend herself, a distant shout echoed across the garden.

“The trial is beginning!”

The Gryphon jumped up.

“Come on!”

Alice had no idea what trial they meant.

But she had learned something else in Wonderland.

When everyone suddenly runs towards something strange, following them usually leads to an interesting story.
