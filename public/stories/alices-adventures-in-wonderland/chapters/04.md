# The Rabbit Sends in a Little Bill

The White Rabbit hurried towards Alice.

He did not seem to recognise her.

“Mary Ann!” he cried. “Why are you standing there? Run home and fetch my gloves and fan!”

Alice was so surprised that she obeyed.

She found a little house with the Rabbit's name on the door.

Inside, she spotted another bottle.

Alice knew by now that mysterious bottles in Wonderland usually caused trouble.

She drank from it anyway.

<!-- block:too-big-for-the-house -->
Alice grew until one arm stuck out of a window and one foot pushed up the chimney.

The White Rabbit returned and found his house occupied by something enormous.

Soon a crowd gathered outside.

They sent a lizard called Bill down the chimney.

Alice kicked gently.

Bill shot out again like a cork from a bottle.

Next, little cakes came flying through the window.

Alice ate one and shrank.

She darted through the door before anyone could stop her.

<!-- block:back-in-the-wood -->
She ran deep into the wood.

A huge puppy bounded towards her.

At her present size, even a playful puppy was dangerous.

Alice distracted it with a stick and escaped behind a thistle.

When the puppy finally bounded away, Alice rested against a mushroom.

Something was sitting on top of it.

A large blue Caterpillar.

It was calmly smoking a hookah and looking down at her.
