# The Queen's Croquet-Ground

In the garden Alice found three gardeners shaped like playing cards.

They were hurriedly painting white roses red.

“Why are you doing that?” Alice asked.

They explained that they had planted the wrong colour by mistake.

If the Queen discovered it, they feared she would be furious.

Then a great procession arrived.

Soldiers.

Courtiers.

Royal children.

The White Rabbit.

The King of Hearts.

And finally the Queen.

<!-- block:the-queen-arrives -->
The Queen of Hearts stared at Alice.

“Who is this?”

“My name is Alice.”

The Queen noticed the gardeners.

Her face turned red.

“Off with their heads!”

Alice quietly hid the gardeners behind a flowerpot.

She was beginning to realise that the Queen shouted about chopping off heads much more often than anyone actually did it.

Soon everyone was playing croquet.

The balls were hedgehogs.

The mallets were flamingos.

The hoops were soldiers bent double.

Nothing stayed where it was supposed to.

The Queen cheated constantly and became angry whenever anyone disagreed with her.

<!-- block:the-cat-returns -->
The Cheshire Cat's grin appeared above the croquet ground.

The King disliked the way the Cat looked at him.

The Queen ordered its head chopped off.

This caused a long argument because only the Cat's head had appeared.

The executioner said he could not chop a head off if there was no body to chop it off from.

While they argued, the Cat slowly vanished.

Alice decided Wonderland had finally produced a problem too silly even for Wonderland.
