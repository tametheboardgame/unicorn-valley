# A Caucus-Race

Everyone climbed out of the pool completely soaked.

The Mouse tried to dry them by giving a very serious speech.

Nobody became any drier.

The Dodo announced that what they needed was a Caucus-Race.

“What is a Caucus-Race?” Alice asked.

“The best way to explain it,” said the Dodo, “is to do it.”

The creatures began running in every direction around a rough circle.

There was no starting line.

There was no finish line.

Nobody counted laps.

After a while the Dodo shouted, “The race is over!”

“Who won?” everybody asked.

The Dodo thought for a long time.

“Everybody has won,” he declared, “and all must have prizes.”

This caused a problem because nobody had brought prizes.

At last everyone looked at Alice.

She searched her pockets and found a box of sweets.

There was exactly one sweet for each creature.

They cheered.

Alice received a thimble as her own prize, even though it had come from her pocket in the first place.

She decided not to argue.

Then she mentioned her cat Dinah.

Alice loved Dinah and began explaining what an excellent mouse-catcher she was.

The Mouse looked horrified.

The birds began backing away.

Within moments everyone had left.

Alice sat alone.

“Why do I always say the wrong thing?” she wondered.

A rustling sound came from the trees.

The White Rabbit was coming back.
