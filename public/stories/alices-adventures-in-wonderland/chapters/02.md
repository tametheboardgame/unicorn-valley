# The Pool of Tears

Alice searched the hall and found a little bottle labelled DRINK ME.

She checked carefully that it did not say anything alarming.

Then she tasted it.

It was delicious.

Almost at once she began to shrink.

Soon she was small enough for the little door.

Unfortunately, the golden key was still on top of the glass table.

Now Alice was too short to reach it.

“This is becoming ridiculous,” she said.

Then she found a small cake marked EAT ME.

After one bite, she began to grow.

Up and up she went until her head nearly touched the ceiling.

<!-- block:far-too-tall -->
Alice was now far too large for the little door.

She became so frustrated that she started to cry.

Her tears fell in enormous drops and spread across the floor.

Then the White Rabbit appeared again.

When he saw giant Alice, he squeaked, dropped his fan and gloves, and ran away.

Alice picked up the fan.

As she waved it, she began shrinking again.

Smaller.

Smaller.

Too small.

She slipped into the great puddle made by her own tears.

<!-- block:swimming-in-tears -->
Alice found herself swimming beside a Mouse.

Soon a Duck, a Dodo, a Lory, an Eaglet and several other wet creatures joined them.

Alice tried to make conversation.

Unfortunately, almost everything she said seemed to offend somebody.

Eventually the strange group paddled towards the shore.

Alice had been in Wonderland for only a short time.

Already she was beginning to suspect that ordinary manners might not be enough here.
