# The Mock Turtle's Story

The Queen sent Alice away with a Gryphon.

“Take her to see the Mock Turtle,” she ordered.

The Gryphon led Alice to a lonely creature sitting on a rock and sighing heavily.

The Mock Turtle seemed terribly sad.

Alice waited to hear what terrible thing had happened to him.

At last he began.

“Once,” he said, “I was a real Turtle.”

Alice was not quite sure what that meant.

<!-- block:school-under-the-sea -->
The Mock Turtle told Alice about school beneath the sea.

They had lessons in Reeling and Writhing.

Their arithmetic included Ambition, Distraction, Uglification and Derision.

Their teacher was an old Turtle they called Tortoise.

“Why did you call him Tortoise if he wasn't one?” Alice asked.

“We called him Tortoise because he taught us,” said the Mock Turtle.

Alice decided not to ask any more sensible questions for a while.

The Gryphon and Mock Turtle described their lessons, dances and games.

Somehow every explanation created three new questions.

Alice listened politely.

By now she was learning that in Wonderland it was sometimes easier to let nonsense finish speaking before trying to understand it.
