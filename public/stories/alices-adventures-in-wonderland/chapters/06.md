# Pig and Pepper

Outside the little house, a Fish-Footman delivered an invitation to a Frog-Footman.

Inside, everything was chaos.

A cook was throwing plates.

Pepper filled the air.

A Duchess sat holding a crying baby.

And a large cat grinned from ear to ear.

Alice had never seen a cat grin like that.

The Duchess suddenly pushed the baby into Alice's arms and hurried away.

Alice carried it outside.

The baby snorted.

Its nose turned upward.

Its hands seemed more like little trotters.

Before long, Alice was holding a pig.

She carefully set it down and watched it trot into the woods.

The Cheshire Cat appeared on a branch above her.

It was still grinning.

“Would you tell me which way I ought to go?” Alice asked.

“That depends on where you want to get to,” said the Cat.

Alice admitted she did not much care.

“Then it doesn't much matter which way you go.”

The Cat told her that a Hatter lived one way and a March Hare the other.

“They're both mad,” it said.

“But I don't want to go among mad people.”

“Oh, you can't help that. We're all mad here.”

The Cat vanished.

Then its grin vanished.

Then the grin appeared again by itself.

Alice stared at it.

Of all the strange things she had seen that day, a grin without a cat was definitely near the top of the list.
