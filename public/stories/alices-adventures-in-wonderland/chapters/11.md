# Who Stole the Tarts?

The court was packed.

The King and Queen of Hearts sat on their thrones.

The White Rabbit stood nearby holding a trumpet and a scroll.

A plate of tarts sat in the middle of the room.

Alice immediately understood why the trial mattered.

The tarts looked excellent.

The Knave of Hearts stood accused.

The White Rabbit unrolled the scroll and read the charge:

The Queen had made some tarts.

The Knave had supposedly stolen them.

Alice waited for evidence.

Wonderland, unfortunately, seemed less interested in evidence than in making speeches.

<!-- block:very-strange-witnesses -->
The first witness was the Hatter.

He arrived carrying his teacup because he had not finished his tea.

The King asked confusing questions.

The Queen glared at everyone.

The Hatter became so frightened that his answers made even less sense than usual.

Next came the Duchess's cook.

She answered almost every question with “Pepper.”

Then Alice noticed something.

She was growing again.

Slowly at first.

Then enough that the creatures beside her complained she was taking up too much room.

Alice did not care.

For the first time since arriving in Wonderland, being larger made her feel braver.

Then the White Rabbit called the next witness.

“Alice!”
