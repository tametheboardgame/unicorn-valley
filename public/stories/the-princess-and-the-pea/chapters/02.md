# Twenty Mattresses

While the visitor warmed herself by the fire, the queen hurried upstairs.

She had an idea.

A very strange idea.

She went into the guest bedroom and removed all the bedding.

Then she placed one tiny dried pea in the middle of the bed frame.

On top of it she stacked a mattress.

Then another.

Then another.

Soon the pile was taller than the queen.

But she did not stop.

<!-- block:the-tallest-bed -->
By the time she finished, there were twenty mattresses piled one on top of another.

On top of those went twenty soft feather beds.

The guest room now contained what looked less like a bed and more like a very comfortable tower.

The queen called for a ladder.

The young woman climbed carefully to the top.

“This is certainly the tallest bed I have ever slept in,” she said.

“I hope you rest well,” said the queen.

The visitor thanked her.

The queen closed the door.

Then she tiptoed away, looking extremely pleased with herself.

Downstairs the prince asked what she was doing.

“Nothing at all,” said the queen.

The prince knew this usually meant she was doing something very odd indeed.
