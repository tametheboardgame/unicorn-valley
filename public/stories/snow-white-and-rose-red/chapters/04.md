# The Forest Secret

The sisters met the dwarf again beside the river.

This time his beard had become tangled in a fishing line.

A large fish was pulling the line towards the water.

The dwarf was sliding after it.

“Help!” he shouted.

Rose-Red grabbed his coat.

Snow-White cut the tangled thread.

Once again the dwarf was safe.

Once again he complained about his beard.

And once again he hurried away carrying something valuable.

This time it was a bag of pearls.

<!-- block:the-eagle -->
A few days later they heard him shouting from a rocky hillside.

A great bird had caught hold of his coat and was trying to carry him away.

Snow-White and Rose-Red grabbed his boots.

They pulled together.

The bird let go.

The dwarf landed in a bush.

“You stretched my coat!” he cried.

Rose-Red folded her arms.

“You’re welcome.”

The dwarf gathered up a box that had fallen beside him.

Gold coins spilled from it.

He hurriedly pushed them back inside.

<!-- block:following-the-trail -->
By now the sisters were certain something strange was happening.

The dwarf always appeared where treasure was hidden.

Their bear friend had said he was protecting something in the forest.

And every time the sisters helped the dwarf, he became more worried about being followed.

So when they saw him carrying another heavy sack towards the deepest part of the woods, they quietly followed.

The trail led to a rocky clearing.

There the dwarf emptied his treasures onto the ground.

Gold.

Pearls.

Bright jewels.

Silver cups.

Objects that looked far too grand to belong to him.

Then a branch cracked behind the sisters.

A familiar black bear stepped into the clearing.
