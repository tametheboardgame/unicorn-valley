# The Bear's Secret

The dwarf spun around.

“You!” he shouted at the bear.

The bear moved between the dwarf and the sisters.

His voice was calm.

“You have hidden these things long enough.”

“They’re mine!”

“They were never yours.”

The dwarf looked from the bear to Snow-White and Rose-Red.

For the first time, he did not look angry.

He looked frightened.

He grabbed a handful of jewels and tried to run.

But a strange golden light flashed through the clearing.

The dwarf dropped the jewels.

The forest seemed to hold its breath.

<!-- block:the-spell-breaks -->
The bear began to glow.

His dark fur shimmered.

Then it fell away like a heavy winter cloak.

Where the bear had stood was a young prince dressed in gold.

Snow-White and Rose-Red stared.

Rose-Red was the first to speak.

“I knew there was something odd about your fur.”

The prince laughed.

He explained that the dwarf had stolen treasure from his family and used magic to trap him in the shape of a bear.

The spell could only last while the dwarf kept hold of the stolen treasure.

Now that it had been found and returned, the magic was broken.

As for the dwarf, he fled into the forest without his gold and was never able to trouble them again.

<!-- block:back-to-the-cottage -->
The prince returned the treasure to everyone it belonged to.

But before going home, he visited the cottage one more time.

Their mother opened the door.

She looked at him.

Then she looked behind him.

“Where is the bear?”

The prince smiled.

Snow-White and Rose-Red burst out laughing.

It took quite a while to explain.

The prince remained their friend, and from then on the road between the palace and the little cottage was travelled often.

Years passed.

The sisters grew older, but they never forgot the promise they had made beside the two rose trees.

Whatever one had, she shared with the other.

And every spring, outside the cottage window, one tree bloomed white and the other bloomed red.
