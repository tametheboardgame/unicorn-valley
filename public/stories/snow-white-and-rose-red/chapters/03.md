# The Dwarf in Trouble

Some weeks later Snow-White and Rose-Red went into the forest to gather fallen branches.

Near an old tree they heard a furious little voice.

“Pull! Push! Do something!”

A tiny dwarf was jumping up and down beside a split log.

His long white beard was trapped firmly in a crack in the wood.

“We’ll help,” said Snow-White.

“I do not need help!” snapped the dwarf.

Rose-Red looked at his beard.

“It looks rather like you do.”

They pulled at the log.

They pushed at the branches.

Nothing worked.

At last Snow-White took a tiny pair of scissors from her sewing basket and snipped off the very end of the trapped beard.

The dwarf was free.

Instead of thanking them, he stared at the shortened end of his beard.

“My beautiful beard!” he shouted.

“We did just rescue you,” said Rose-Red.

The dwarf grabbed a sack hidden beneath the tree roots.

For a moment the girls saw bright stones glittering inside it.

Then he dragged the sack into the bushes.

“Keep away from my treasure!”

And he vanished.

Rose-Red stared after him.

“Well,” she said, “he was cheerful.”

Snow-White laughed.

But as they walked home, she thought about the sparkling stones.

And about the bear who had said there was something in the forest he needed to protect.
