# The Quiet Clearing

In the centre of the clearing stood another lantern. It was smaller than the one at the village edge and much older. Moss curled around its base. Its glass was cloudy, and inside there was no candle at all.

Rowan nudged the little door with his nose. It swung open.

Inside lay a single mirror.

<!-- block:reflected-light -->
Rowan peered into it and saw his own face, the glowmoths behind him and, far between the trees, a tiny line of village lights.

The mirror caught those distant lights and sent a soft gleam into the empty lantern.

The lantern began to shine.

Rowan shifted to one side and the light faded. He stepped back into place and it returned.

"Oh," he whispered. The lantern was not waiting for a flame. It was waiting for light from somewhere else.

<!-- block:one-light-not-enough -->
Rowan tried every angle he could think of. The glow grew stronger, but never enough to fill the clearing.

At last he sat down. He had come all this way expecting to solve the mystery alone. That seemed important when he left the village. Now it seemed rather silly.

The glowmoths gathered on the lantern roof.

"All right," Rowan said. "I think I know what I was supposed to learn."

He turned towards home.

This time, the path did not move at all.
