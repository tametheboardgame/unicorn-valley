# The Last Lantern

Every evening, the lanterns of the little hill village were lit from the fountain outward. Gold lights appeared beside doorways, along garden walls and at each turn of the winding path. By the time the first star showed itself, the whole village seemed to be wearing a necklace of warm fire.

Rowan liked to watch from the bakery step. He could always tell where the lamplighter had reached by following the glow. But there was one lantern that never shone. It stood beyond the final cottage, where the neat path narrowed towards the woods.

<!-- block:question-for-quill -->
Rowan asked everyone why. The baker said the lantern was old. The gardener said it belonged to the woods. A pair of racing foals said it was probably haunted, then galloped away before Rowan could ask whether they actually knew that.

At last he asked Quill.

Quill looked over the top of a book and said, "Some lights are meant to show you where to go. Some are meant to show you when you are ready."

"Ready for what?"

"If I told you," said Quill, "it would stop being that sort of story."

<!-- block:first-glimmer -->
That evening Rowan walked to the edge of the woods. He carried no torch and had no plan beyond looking at the unlit lamp from very close up.

For a while nothing happened.

Then, somewhere between two dark trunks, a green-gold light blinked once. The old lantern answered with the faintest silver glimmer.

Rowan took one step into the woods.

The glimmer brightened.
