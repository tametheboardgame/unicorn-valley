# The Path That Moved

At first the path was easy to follow. Pale stones curved between ferns, and little glowmoths hovered above them like drifting sparks.

Then Rowan looked back. The village had vanished behind the trees.

He turned forward again and found that the pale stones now curved the other way.

"That is extremely rude for a path," Rowan told it.

The path did not apologise.

<!-- block:chasing-light -->
Rowan hurried after the brightest glowmoth. Whenever he sped up, it darted farther ahead. Soon he was cross, scratched and completely uncertain which direction was home.

He stopped.

The glowmoth stopped too.

Rowan took one slow breath. Then another. The branches were not moving. The roots had been there all along. He had simply been looking too far ahead to see them.

When he walked again, carefully this time, the glowmoth floated beside him instead of racing away.

<!-- block:shared-pace -->
More glowmoths joined it. One by one they settled along the path, and the pale stones became visible again.

Rowan began to understand. The woods had not been trying to lose him. They had been refusing to be hurried.

At the next fork he waited until the glowmoths chose a branch. At a fallen log he looked for a safe way around instead of leaping blindly over it.

By the time the trees opened into a round clearing, Rowan no longer felt alone.
