# The White Birds

Before winter had begun, the duckling had once seen a flock of magnificent white birds flying overhead.

They had long curved necks and enormous wings.

He had watched them until they disappeared.

He did not know what they were.

He only knew that seeing them had made something inside him ache.

Now spring had returned.

The duckling wandered towards a lake surrounded by fresh green trees.

There, gliding across the water, were three of those same white birds.

Swans.

<!-- block:going-towards-them -->
The duckling stopped at the edge of the lake.

He wanted to hide.

Beautiful creatures had never been kind to him before.

Then he thought about the farmyard.

The marsh.

The little house.

The long winter.

He was tired of deciding what other creatures would think before they had even met him.

“If they chase me away,” he thought, “at least I will know.”

He stepped into the water.

The swans noticed him.

They began swimming towards him.

The duckling lowered his head.

He waited.
