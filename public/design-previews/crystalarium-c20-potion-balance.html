Campaign-wide balancing evidence

Across the actual 50 authored resonance patterns, building each recipe independently from basic crystals requires an estimated 950 basic shards, 812 successful merges and 50 completed orders, including the extra crystals required to craft 14 harmonies. This is an analytical comparison, not a timed playtest or the exact inventory of a saved game.

Assumes −1 per successful basic shard, no optional refined shortcuts and no re-use of leftover crystals. Actual potion gains are lower when a full bottle clips rewards. The first proposal nearly cancels the cost of growing 950 shards, so we should explicitly decide whether the potion meter is meant to require occasional refilling or simply show a gentle reward cycle.

Review before connecting to real gameplay

The final design must settle the capacity, refill method, normal and refined growth costs, merge/Offer rewards, old-save migration, and what the reservoir shows at zero. Costs and rewards will only apply to successful, saved actions. Blocked growth, a full board, failed saves, and cancelled gestures must never spend potion.

The visual deliberately uses existing approved C17 artwork. This page is independent of the game controller and never calls localStorage, sessionStorage, any API or the save service.
