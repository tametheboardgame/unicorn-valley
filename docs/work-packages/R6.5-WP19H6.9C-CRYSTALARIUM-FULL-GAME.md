# H6.9C — Crystalarium: substantial replayable merge game (design proposal)

**Status:** proposed for David's review, not yet an approved implementation or merge decision. H6.9B's small musical entrance is accepted in principle on 23 October 2026. It must remain unchanged as the approximately 30–60-second first entry. The desired expansion is informed by the long-form merge/crafting progression of *Flambé: Merge & Cook*, not its energy economy.

## Product goal

Make Crystalarium a proper, long-lived optional game that rewards making elaborate crystals, discovering harmonies and restoring a visible crystal nursery in Crystal Brook. A player should enjoy a few minutes or a long voluntary session. All main-story Grotto access is earned solely by the original three-note entrance round. The expanded game must never make the player repeat or grind that unlock.

## One game, two experiences

- Preserve game ID `crystalarium`, the existing `CrystalariumActivityScene`, shared merge primitive, catalogue definition, SceneManifest loading, normal MiniGameLauncher and outcome isolation.
- `opening-melody`: unchanged deterministic 4×3, three matching pairs, world only; first unlock permanent, legacy saves still valid.
- `resonance-patterns`: the larger optional game, playable from an authored Brook-side Crystalarium nursery and from Just Games. Neither entry spawns a separate gameplay implementation.
- Reuse the same UI/engine components for piece representation, selection, merging, optional sound and return; introduce independent rules/controller/state modules for the deep variant rather than growing a monolithic Phaser scene.

## Intended full-game loop

1. Choose a **Resonance Pattern** from the pattern book. Initially one clear request, later up to three visible requests with a relaxed choice of which to pursue.
2. Tap a crystal vein/source to generate a low-tier piece in a free slot. **No energy cost, recharge timer, daily limit or payment**. Later sources add more crystal families.
3. Drag/tap two equal-family, equal-tier pieces to merge them, advancing shard → polished shard → crystal → cluster → prism → rare formation. The result is spatially and musically satisfying; no adjacency requirement.
4. When a requested formation exists, explicitly **offer** it to the pattern. The target consumes only the confirmed pieces, not accidental matches.
5. Some later patterns use a **polishing basin** to refine a piece or a **harmony altar** to combine selected different voices through a separate authored recipe. Cross-family recipes do not silently change the core equal-family merge rule.
6. Completing a small sequence of patterns brings a visible, optional nursery restoration or musical composition milestone. Continue, take a break or return to the Brook. Progress resumes where the player left off.

## Initial scale target, subject to playtest

- **Board:** 7 columns × 6 rows (42 cells), with responsive layout and minimum practical touch targets; adapt viewport composition rather than shrinking controls illegibly.
- **Piece growth:** six existing tiers and the three established low/bright/bell voices at launch. Add one or two clearly distinguished family chains later only if they improve choices.
- **Campaign:** propose five themed chapters of around 10–12 hand-authored patterns each (roughly 50–60 objectives), with a gradual mechanical reveal and optional free-play after introduction. The count is a content target for playtesting, not a requirement to force grinding.
- **Progression examples:** First Notes → River Nursery → Singing Pools → Prism Workshop → Great Harmonic Garden. These are *Brook nursery/game* identities, not unauthorised changes to the Crystal Grotto interior owned by H7.
- **Pacing:** early patterns complete in a handful of moves; later patterns involve planning, choice and satisfying combinations, but not deliberately astronomical merge counts.

## UX and child-first safeguards

- Design an approachable full-game layout with board, readable pattern goals, source affordances, reserve/shelf, notes/status and large touchable return/reset/help controls. No cluttered customer-order or popup stacks.
- Distinguish crystals by silhouette/glyph/pattern as well as colour; show the musical relationship visually, with sound always optional and existing mute/volume settings honoured.
- Every legal merge and special transformation has readable preview/feedback; offer a reversible mistake recovery where practical and a clear tutorial. Keyboard parity and accessible focus are required.
- Board-space safety is guaranteed, not a reward trap: reserve storage plus free **Brook Wash** return/recycle can recover space, including generator-blocked and no-legal-merge states. No permanent dead board.
- Resetting a *session* never accidentally discards earned chapter progress. Provide Continue, Start fresh board (with confirmation) and leave/resume.
- No real-time waiting, energy, lives, adverts, paywalls, loot boxes, streaks, daily prizes, time-limited events or real-world FOMO.

## Persistence and isolation design gate

The large game needs genuine **save/resume and durable chapter progress**, unlike H6.9B's disposable tutorial board. Before source implementation, design and explicitly approve the versioned persistence contract and any schema change:

- In-world Crystalarium progress may be stored only through the canonical versioned save/service, with backwards-compatible migration and bounded optional Brook-local cosmetic effects through the normal authorised world gateway.
- Just Games must use an **independent, namespaced, versioned sandbox progress store** if persistent Just Games play is approved; it must not mutate adventure quests, flags, inventory, Shimmer, discoveries or normal world Crystalarium state.
- Decide whether to keep separate independent chapter tracks (recommended) or to make Just Games session-only. Do not silently share progress between world and sandbox.
- Reset, corrupted storage, reload, old saves and source-switching all need tests.

## Creative progression

Pattern completion restores the **physical Crystalarium nursery beside Crystal Brook** (light, faceted plants, singing formations and gentle ambient notes), providing visible consequences beyond scores. Optional Echo/Ripple dialogue may acknowledge milestones. Do not change Crystal Grotto H7 interior composition or move established world quest/reward gates without a separate dependency review.

## Bounded implementation sequence once approved

- **H6.9C1 — deep-game pure engine and persistence design:** model 42-cell board, families, six tiers, generators, move/offer rules, versioned isolated state, migration and invariants; prove basic solvability and recovery.
- **H6.9C2 — satisfying playable board:** implement generation/merges/drag/tap/keyboard, reachable pattern goals, tray and presentation with a draft preview and child-first feedback.
- **H6.9C3 — depth:** polishing and harmonies, tier discoveries, authored chapter objectives, progression pacing and optional free play.
- **H6.9C4 — world purpose:** add the separate Brook nursery interaction, approved optional cosmetic restoration, save-resume, distinct world and sandbox progress; do not interfere with the Grotto entrance.
- **H6.9C5 — final playtest and handoff:** no-softlock deterministic/property tests, performance/loading budget, accessibility, touch/mobile, long-play balancing and new/legacy save regressions. H6.9D retains integration and final human acceptance.

## Explicit approval questions for the next design gate

- Is the target a substantial **persistent chapter-based merge game** with restorative cosmetic rewards, rather than a series of short discrete puzzles?
- Should Just Games retain its own independent, persistent campaign progress (recommended), or reset at exit?
- Is the proposed five-chapter scale appropriate for the first full release, subject to child playtesting?

Until these design decisions are accepted, this file is planning evidence only. **Do not implement H6.9C as part of H6.9B or weaken H6.9B's merge gate to expedite the expansion.**
