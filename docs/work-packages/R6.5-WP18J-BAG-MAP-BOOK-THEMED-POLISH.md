# R6.5-WP18J - Bag, Map & Wonderbook Themed Presentation Polish

## Outcome

**Complete and human-approved on 2026-09-08.**

Final approved game-code head: `e4d64c0fa258bd91eb29579321e7da6b0968f71e`.

Final approved immutable preview: `https://8292d7b9.unicorn-valley.pages.dev`.

WP18J began as a bounded Bag/Map/Wonderbook polish pass and was explicitly extended during human review to finish the responsive exploration presentation across desktop/laptop, tablet landscape, phone landscape and phone portrait.

## Final Bag result

- magical satchel/saddlebag identity retained;
- category/pocket row spacing and alignment corrected;
- nested border/shadow clutter reduced;
- item/detail areas remain readable and touch-friendly;
- close control is visually only `×`, with the generous invisible touch target retained;
- the Bag `Visit the Shop` shortcut was deliberately removed: shops are visited physically in the world;
- inventory, Food use, Shimmer, quantity and scrolling semantics are otherwise preserved.

## Final Map result

- magical parchment/adventure-map identity retained;
- click/touch drag panning added without changing topology or routes;
- North marker is fixed and not part of draggable geography;
- moving geography is rendered by a dedicated camera viewport so it hard-clips beneath the parchment opening in WebGL;
- header, footer, compass, frame and drag hint stay fixed above/beside the moving content;
- close control is visually only `×`, with its large invisible hit target retained;
- existing current-location, route, homeward and nearby guidance semantics are preserved.

## Final Wonderbook result

- enchanted open-book/storybook identity retained;
- section controls moved to page-edge filing/index tabs;
- no tab crosses the book spine;
- Friends / Places / Races / Goals / Discoveries behaviour, filters, pagination and progress semantics remain unchanged.

## Responsive and HUD result

- legacy landscape UI fallback deleted rather than hidden;
- desktop/laptop, tablet landscape and phone landscape use the canonical concept shell;
- phone portrait uses the approved dock/control composition below gameplay;
- top HUD is rendered in `ExplorationHudOverlayScene`, independent of the world-follow camera, eliminating the observed Bag/Book/Shimmer text jitter;
- opening/closing Bag, Map or Book no longer leaves top navigation icon geometry in a pressed/misaligned state;
- old standalone Sound launcher/mini-panel is deleted; Sound remains available through Settings.

## Responsive acceptance rule

Future presentation work is incomplete unless these four display classes are audited together:

1. desktop/laptop;
2. tablet landscape;
3. phone landscape;
4. phone portrait.

Landscape phone should be treated as a smaller tablet composition, not as a reason to resurrect a legacy HUD.
