# R6.5-WP12 - Race Expansion and Rainbow Cup

## Status

COMPLETE - merged through PR #141 as `610216ae13b96772d3a7b7e95696c695ddcd1870` after exact-head validation passed the operating contract, formatting, lint, type-check, unit tests, production build, the unchanged 520 KiB app-entry budget, static smoke, Chromium/Firefox/WebKit compatibility and the full Chromium playtest.

## Goal

Turn racing into a broad, repeatable valley activity before R7 by expanding the regular course roster from two to five and completing the friendly multi-race Rainbow Cup structure already staged in Rainbow Meadow.

## Delivered

- Five regular courses now exist: Sunrise Sprint, Crystal Cascade, Petal Parade, Mooncap Trail and Shoreline Surge.
- The three new courses reuse the shared `RaceScene`, movement, NPC competition, touch controls and assistance system while providing distinct themes and obstacle/collectable rhythms.
- Mooncap Trail includes the Root Hop alternate-route shortcut through the existing shortcut architecture.
- The Rainbow Cup covers all five events and completes through participation rather than requiring first place.
- New course finishes award registered finisher ribbons, podium rosettes and Wonderbook discoveries; the Cup awards its one-time Pennant.
- New keepsakes integrate with Cottage display profiles.
- Meadow, Woods and Beach expose coherent race entry points; direct regional races return to their origin and Cup races return to the Meadow hub.
- Shoreline Surge uses the existing Skipper route-ready progression hook.
- Starlight Beach remains intentionally lazy-registered; the final browser acceptance follows the real Woods-to-Beach gateway rather than defeating that loading contract.
- Existing save files remain valid; no save-schema change was introduced.
