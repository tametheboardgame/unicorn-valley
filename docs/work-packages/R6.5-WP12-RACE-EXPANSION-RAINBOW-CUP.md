# R6.5-WP12 - Race Expansion and Rainbow Cup

## Goal

Turn racing into a broad, repeatable valley activity before R7 by expanding the regular course roster from two to five and completing the friendly multi-race Rainbow Cup structure already staged in Rainbow Meadow.

## Delivery contract

- Keep Sunrise Sprint and Crystal Cascade as established regular courses.
- Add three recognisably different regular course experiences:
  - Petal Parade - bright Meadow flower-course rhythm;
  - Mooncap Trail - winding Whispering Woods route;
  - Shoreline Surge - Starlight Beach shoreline/tide course rooted in Skipper's route story.
- Reuse the existing `RaceScene`, race movement, NPC competition, assistance controls, touch controls, save records and reward authorities rather than creating a parallel racing system.
- Give at least one new course a meaningful shortcut/route distinction using the existing race shortcut architecture.
- Add a five-event Rainbow Cup view at the Meadow race hub.
- Cup completion is participation-friendly: every event must be finished, but first place is never required.
- New course finishes produce their own registered ribbons/rosettes/discoveries and connect back to the Cottage/Wonderbook.
- Direct regional race entries return to their originating region; Cup-launched races return to the Cup hub.
- Starlight Beach's existing Skipper route-board flag becomes the Beach-course unlock/entry hook.
- Existing mobile race controls and race assistance remain available on every new course.

## Acceptance

1. There are exactly five regular courses exposed by the shared race-course contract, excluding Nova's tutorial run.
2. The three new courses have distinct obstacle/boost/collectable rhythms and distinct presentation themes.
3. At least one new course has a usable alternate route/shortcut mechanic.
4. The Rainbow Cup shows five events, saved completion/best-time state and useful lock clues.
5. Finishing all five events completes the Cup regardless of placement and grants its reward once.
6. New course finisher/podium rewards use the existing race-result/save authorities and registered content IDs.
7. The Beach route board launches the shoreline race once Skipper's route is ready; Woods and Meadow have coherent course access.
8. All new courses retain keyboard, touch and assistance behaviour.
9. Existing save files remain valid; no save-schema change is introduced.
10. Formatting, lint, type-check, unit tests, production build, static smoke, browser compatibility and full browser playtest pass with the 520 KiB app-entry budget unchanged.

## Guardrails

- No production deployment in this package without explicit approval.
- Do not weaken the 520 KiB app-entry performance gate.
- Do not require perfect wins, podiums or grinding to finish the Cup.
- Prefer code-split/lazy world integration so new race breadth does not inflate the entry bundle unnecessarily.
- Preserve accepted WP7-WP11 content, world-state restoration, geometry and graphics fixes.
