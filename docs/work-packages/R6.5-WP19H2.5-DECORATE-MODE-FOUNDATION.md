# R6.5-WP19H2.5 – Decorate Mode Foundation

**Status:** In progress

**Parent package:** R6.5-WP19H2 – Moonflower Cottage & Home Customisation

**Previous checkpoint:** H2.4 Bed & Sleep, human-approved 17 September 2026 after removal of the competing legacy bed interaction.

## Purpose

Separate normal cottage play from home editing. Decoration slots must no longer exist as permanent visual or interaction clutter. The player deliberately enters Decorate mode, edits intentional blue magical placement spots, then explicitly returns to normal play.

## Implementation decisions

- `CottageInteriorScene` is the canonical owner of whether the cottage is in normal or Decorate mode.
- Normal play contains only normal cottage interactions. Decoration-slot targets are not registered in the scene interaction set.
- `CottageDepthWorldManager` observes the canonical scene mode flag and withdraws its normal tactile targets while decorating so shared and scene-local targets cannot compete.
- Empty decoration markers are removed from normal home rendering at source.
- Decorate mode reveals small blue magical slot markers, including the H2.4-deferred bedside slot.
- Inside the cottage, the normal Gallop action is replaced by a blue `Decorate` control in the same lower-right action position; it becomes `Done` while editing. Back/Escape also exits Decorate mode before leaving the cottage.
- Approaching the cottage doorway while decorating opens `Are you finished decorating?`; choosing Yes exits Decorate mode without forcing an immediate scene transition, while No keeps editing active.
- The existing `HomeDecorationService` and existing decoration picker remain the persistence/selection foundation.
- Returning from the existing picker restores Decorate mode so the player can continue editing rather than falling back into normal play.

## Acceptance criteria

- Normal cottage play shows no empty decoration-slot stars/halos.
- Normal cottage interaction selection does not include decoration slots.
- A visible blue `Decorate` button replaces Gallop inside the cottage and enters editing mode.
- Editing mode reveals only subtle blue magical placement markers for editable slots.
- Normal sleep, exit, Wonderbook, visitor and tactile room interactions do not compete with decoration targets while editing.
- Selecting a decoration slot opens the existing picker.
- Returning from the picker restores editing mode.
- `Done` or Back/Escape returns to clean normal play and restores normal cottage interactions.
- Entering the doorway while editing asks whether decorating is finished; Yes exits editing so the normal exit can be used, No leaves Decorate mode active.
- Existing placed-decoration persistence remains handled by `HomeDecorationService`.

## Human gate

Enter the cottage in normal play and confirm it is visually clean. Enter Decorate mode, walk around several blue placement spots, open at least one picker, return to the room, then press `Done`. Confirm the markers disappear and ordinary cottage interactions work again.
