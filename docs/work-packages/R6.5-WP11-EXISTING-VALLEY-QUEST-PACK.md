# R6.5-WP11 - Existing Valley Quest Pack A

## Status

COMPLETE - reconciled onto the accepted WP7/WP8 mainline, full automated acceptance passed, and merged through PR #140 as `af532922d6a071adf86b345e5c94a81143f9832d`.

## Goal

Increase story density across the established valley after the WP4-WP8 region-depth passes, using familiar landmarks and existing quest, relationship, discovery, inventory and economy authorities.

## Substantive story threads

### Willow and the Moonflowers After Dark

- prerequisite: Willow's Moonflowers completed;
- discovered through the planted Glade garden after dark rather than handed out by a new quest marker;
- reveals the Moonflowers' new night-light pattern;
- returns to Willow for payoff;
- awards a Moonflower Night Memory, friendship and standard substantive-quest Shimmer;
- leaves persistent glowing garden evidence.

### Nova, Clover and a Race With No Finish Line

- prerequisite: Nova's First Race completed;
- begins from Clover's hand-drawn Meadow route card;
- uses Nova plus three existing Meadow landmarks: Rainbow Pond, Picnic Hill and Windmill Lookout;
- deliberately has no timer, ranking or formal RaceScene;
- awards a Friendship Route Pennant, Nova/Clover friendship and standard substantive-quest Shimmer;
- leaves the three untimed route flags visible afterwards.

### Pebble and the Stone That Does Not Match

- discovered by exploration in Crystal Brook;
- asks Pebble about the unusual stone;
- deliberately crosses Crystal Brook -> Story House -> Crystal Brook;
- resolves the pattern at the Reflection Pool rather than through a collection quantity;
- awards an Odd Stone Bookend, Pebble friendship and standard substantive-quest Shimmer;
- leaves a small matching-stone cairn visible in the Brook.

## Supporting-resident microstories

### Juniper's Butterfly Count

- becomes available after the Hidden Flower Circle is known;
- uses a tiny observation/counting game rather than a fetch objective;
- awards Wonderbook discovery and Juniper friendship;
- intentionally does not mint Shimmer.

### Maple's Perfect Picnic Spot

- prerequisite: Maple and the Wobbly Cake Plan completed;
- tests a bun-safe spot on Picnic Hill;
- awards Wonderbook discovery and Maple friendship;
- leaves a small picnic marker visible afterwards;
- intentionally does not mint Shimmer.
