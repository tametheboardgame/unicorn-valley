# R6.5-WP18H - Full Human Tablet Replay and Return to WP17

## Objective

Run a fresh substantially unguided human playthrough on the reference landscape tablet after all WP18 remediation is complete. Confirm that the actual child-facing experience now meets the intended standard before returning to the unresolved WP17 R7-readiness decision.

## Reference environment

Primary human device:

- Samsung Galaxy Tab S8;
- current Android version available for that device;
- Chrome;
- landscape orientation.

## Testing principle

The child should receive no more guidance than would be normal for a player using the finished game.

Observe what she independently:

- notices;
- understands;
- presses;
- ignores;
- chooses to do;
- seeks out;
- enjoys;
- avoids;
- gets stuck on;
- misinterprets;
- asks an adult to explain.

Do not coach around interface defects merely to continue the test.

## Mandatory replay areas

The play session should naturally or deliberately cover enough of the following to validate the remediated systems:

### Controls and shell

- normal exploration using tap-to-move;
- directional controls;
- Gallop;
- contextual action button;
- direct NPC tapping;
- Bag;
- Map;
- Wonderbook;
- Settings/help;
- reward/currency feedback.

### Racing

At least one real race on the Galaxy Tab S8, ideally enough to confirm:

- no Chrome long-press interference;
- controls do not obscure upcoming obstacles;
- actions are comfortable and understandable;
- race is genuinely playable rather than technically startable.

### Previously frozen surfaces

Revisit:

- Tree Nook;
- Twinkle and Thread;
- Sunlit Beach Bag open/close.

No freeze or world-disappearance regression is acceptable.

### Functional fixes

Where progression allows, verify:

- Echo can be spoken to;
- Maple can be found/understood through the relevant quest state;
- cottage back wall is not walkable through;
- decoration placement/removal state is understandable;
- purchases visibly succeed;
- inventory beyond six items remains accessible;
- food can be meaningfully used;
- characters are not obviously duplicated across impossible simultaneous states.

### Visual/readability improvements

Observe reactions to:

- shell clarity/noise;
- creator layout;
- Shell Cove;
- Crystal areas/entrance;
- shop presentation;
- unicorn character proportions/neck/mane;
- NPC body/icon consistency;
- retained positive areas such as Rainbow Meadow, Whispering Woods and Tree Nook.

## Human questions after play

Use simple child-friendly questions rather than leading technical prompts. Useful areas:

1. Could you work out what to do?
2. Were any controls annoying or difficult?
3. Was anything hard to read or understand?
4. Could you find the people/places you wanted?
5. What did you spend most of your time doing?
6. What did you enjoy most?
7. Was anything boring, ugly or confusing?
8. Did anything stop working or freeze?
9. Would you play again?
10. What would you most like added or changed next?

## Acceptance evidence

WP18H itself is complete when the replay has happened and the evidence has been captured faithfully.

The replay should demonstrate, at minimum:

- no hard freeze;
- no major landscape-tablet control blocker;
- race playable by touch;
- no major unreadable/overlapping shell state;
- primary interactions understandable;
- Bag/Map/Creator usable;
- no known progression blocker from the WP17 set;
- enough uninterrupted free play to judge the valley rather than the interface.

If major blockers remain, create bounded additional R6.5 remediation rather than pretending WP17 is released.

## Important gate rule

**WP18H does not release R7.**

After the replay, return to `R6.5-WP17 - Full Human Playthrough and R7 Readiness Gate` and ask for the user's explicit readiness decision using the new evidence.

Only explicit user acceptance of WP17 may unblock R7.

## Exit

- If blockers remain: keep WP17 unreleased and plan bounded remediation.
- If the user explicitly approves readiness: close WP17, update durable project state and begin the bounded R7-WP7.1 planning path.
