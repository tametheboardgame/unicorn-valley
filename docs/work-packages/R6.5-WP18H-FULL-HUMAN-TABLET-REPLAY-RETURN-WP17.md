# R6.5-WP18H - Full Human Tablet Replay and Return to WP17

## Objective

Run a fresh, substantially unguided daughter playthrough on the reference Galaxy Tab S8 after WP18K architecture cleanup and after known open readiness blockers have been accounted for. Capture the real child-facing evidence, then return to WP17 for the explicit R7-readiness decision.

WP18H itself does **not** release R7.

## Preconditions

- WP18I/J accepted UI remains visually intact after WP18K;
- WP18K technical validation is reviewed;
- every `OPEN` item in `docs/HUMAN-PLAYTEST-FEEDBACK-LEDGER.md` is either resolved or explicitly accepted/bounded before the readiness decision;
- the known Moonflower Cottage/window/back-wall collision is not silently ignored.

## Reference environment

- Samsung Galaxy Tab S8;
- current supported Android;
- Chrome;
- landscape orientation.

## Replay principle

Provide no more guidance than a normal player would receive. Observe what the child independently understands, presses, ignores, seeks out, enjoys, avoids, misinterprets or asks an adult to explain. Do not coach around defects merely to continue the test.

## Mandatory replay coverage

Use `docs/HUMAN-PLAYTEST-FEEDBACK-LEDGER.md` as the checklist. At minimum cover:

- tap-to-move, directional movement, Gallop and contextual actions;
- direct NPC interaction;
- Bag, Map, Wonderbook and Settings/help;
- at least one real touch race, including long-press/browser-gesture behaviour;
- Tree Nook, Twinkle and Thread, and Sunlit Beach Bag open/close freeze regressions;
- Echo, Maple, purchases/reward clarity, >6 Bag items, Food use, decoration state and character-presence consistency where progression permits;
- cottage collision honestly;
- creator/readability and world visual improvements;
- the positive areas that must be preserved: Rainbow Meadow, Whispering Woods, Tree Nook/mushrooms, Firefly activity, reward/currency loop, questing/unlocks and tap movement.

## Child-facing questions

1. Could you work out what to do?
2. Were any controls annoying or difficult?
3. Was anything hard to read or understand?
4. Could you find the people/places you wanted?
5. What did you spend most of your time doing?
6. What did you enjoy most?
7. Was anything boring, ugly or confusing?
8. Did anything stop working or freeze?
9. Would you play again?
10. What would you most like added or changed next?

## Exit

Capture evidence faithfully, update the feedback ledger, then return to R6.5-WP17. Only an explicit user readiness decision at WP17 can unblock R7.
