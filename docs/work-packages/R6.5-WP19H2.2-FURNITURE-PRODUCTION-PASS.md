# R6.5-WP19H2.2 – Furniture Production Pass

## Purpose

Replace Moonflower Cottage's temporary H2.1 furniture blocks with permanent, deliberately styled room furnishings before collision and spatial layering are rebuilt in H2.3.

The user approved H2.1 on 16 September 2026 and then asked to start H2.3. The roadmap makes H2.3 depend on the final furniture silhouettes from H2.2, so H2.2 is being completed first rather than building collision against temporary geometry and immediately discarding it.

## Scope

- substantially redesign the fireplace;
- substantially redesign the bed;
- substantially redesign the tea table and seating;
- substantially redesign the sofa/seating area;
- improve windows and curtains;
- improve the treasure shelf/display area;
- give the Wonderbook a deliberate reading nook rather than leaving it on an otherwise empty patch of floor;
- preserve the H2.1 open architectural exit instead of restoring a brown door panel;
- remove obsolete primitive furniture rendering from `CottageInteriorScene.ts` as the production renderer becomes canonical;
- preserve H2.1 furniture positions, interaction anchors and collision geometry for this visual-only checkpoint.

## Implementation ownership

- `CottageInteriorMap.ts` continues to own furniture positions and current broad collision geometry.
- `CottageFurnitureRenderer.ts` owns permanent-furniture presentation for H2.2 onward.
- `CottageInteriorScene.ts` owns scene composition and calls the dedicated furniture renderer rather than carrying individual primitive furniture methods.
- `WonderbookWorldProp.ts` remains the canonical Wonderbook prop renderer; H2.2 only adds its surrounding nook.

## Deliberately deferred

H2.2 does not attempt the physicality pass. Broad H2.1 colliders and fixed presentation depths remain until H2.3, where they can be rebuilt against these final visual silhouettes. Bed entry/sleep remains H2.4 and Decorate-mode cleanup remains H2.5.

## Acceptance criteria

- permanent furnishings no longer read as plain prototype rectangles/ellipses;
- fireplace reads clearly as a detailed hearth with mantel, stonework and fire;
- bed reads as a furnished, styled bed rather than a solid coloured block;
- table/seating and sofa have recognisable construction and decorative detail;
- windows feel integrated into the cottage through framing/curtains and exterior detail;
- treasure display and Wonderbook areas read as intentional room features;
- the approved exit remains an obvious open gap with a subtle threshold rather than a floor-mounted door panel;
- no interaction, save, navigation or decoration behaviour changes are introduced;
- no H2.3 collision/layering changes are pulled forward.

## Human gate

Review the deployed cottage visually. Approve H2.2 only when the permanent furnishings feel sufficiently detailed and stylistically coherent to serve as the shapes H2.3 will build collision and front/back layering around.
