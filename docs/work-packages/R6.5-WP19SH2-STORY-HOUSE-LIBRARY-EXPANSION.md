# SH2 - Story House Library Expansion: Ten New Titles

## Programme decision and outcome

**Approved for roadmap planning on 15 October 2026.** The ten-title proposal is an **editorial slate**, not final approved manuscripts, source-rights clearance, finished artwork or permission to publish. The existing Story House is mature enough for normal content-first expansion.

- **Starting library:** 17 published titles.
- **Target:** 27 distinct published titles after ten individually approved and merged additions.
- **Mix:** eight classic/traditional works using cleared sources, two original Quill's Valley Tales.
- **Dependency:** close [SH1.7 consolidation](R6.5-WP19SH1.7-STORY-HOUSE-CONSOLIDATION.md), then complete SH2.0 editorial foundation before bulk title production.
- **Delivery model:** independent short-lived `agent/sh-*` branches, draft PR and preview per book, fresh-main catalogue generation and final exact-head CI/Merge Gate. Do not run all ten books in one PR.
- **Concurrent development:** Main World and Just Games remain independent. Story House title work must not alter their scenes, world placements, minigame mechanics or root engineering state.

The overall slate is accepted for planning; **each title remains subject to separate manuscript, art, copyright/source and preview approval gates**. If a title proves unsuitable, propose a documented substitute, do not silently change the ten-title promise.

## SH2.0 - Editorial and implementation foundation

**Purpose:** make the ten-title programme safe and repeatable before mass generation.

1. Read the actual 17-title catalogue and existing Story House authoring/rights rules. Confirm the canonical work package, stable ID and title namespace for each proposed book.
2. For each classic, identify a precise source work, **edition and translation**, source URL/publication details and intended distribution territory. Check public-domain/permission status of the **text, translation, illustrations, individual cover and new edition separately**. Older stories do not automatically authorise later translation or historic art. Pay particular attention to later Wind in the Willows illustrated editions; do not copy protected picture composition or files.
3. Specify a child-friendly original **Story House Edition** and determine whether the verified source justifies a separate **Full Classic Text**. A full edition is conditional, never produced from a dubious or partial source. Text edition switching is independent of classic/modern illustration switching.
4. Produce a specific brief per title: source evidence, audience, plot and content-sensitive treatment, proposed chapter/semantic `blockId` outline, cover strategy, edition decision, series metadata, image density, visual bible and checkpoint gates.
5. Confirm the modern-cover convention: choose the strongest **approved reader illustration** after art acceptance, use a normal centre-safe 3:4 catalogue crop and UI text overlay. Dedicated cover-only artwork needs an explicit human-approved exception. Historic/source art remains separately credited.
6. Align original Valley tales with actual canonical World lore and the established Quill/Rowan/locations; record a conflict rather than inventing an unexplained world canon.
7. Keep manuscripts and image batches small enough for meaningful human review. Set indicative visual budgets below only after inspecting real chapter lengths and source text.
8. Define title-level verification and accessibility checks without bespoke reader code. All images and chapters must be demand-loaded through the existing Story Library system.

**SH2.0 acceptance:** David approves title briefs, source-rights decisions, reading-mode/edition plans, overall production order and preliminary visual-bible/scene inventories. No source or illustration publication before this gate.

## Stage 2 - Short illustrated books

### SH2.1 - The Tale of Benjamin Bunny

**Author/source:** Beatrix Potter, 1904, verify source and illustrations for each planned edition. **Format:** illustrated short story.

- Build on accepted Peter Rabbit family/garden continuity. Lock Peter's blue jacket, Benjamin, old Mr Benjamin, garden cat, Mr McGregor's vegetable beds, rabbit scale and settings.
- Create an original age-appropriate Story House retelling. Consider original Full Classic Text only if source and historic illustrations are cleared and it provides value.
- Indicative modern art: **5-7** images, after two or three continuity anchors; reuse accepted reader art for cover.
- **Acceptance:** consistent characters/location with Peter Rabbit, cleared text/art evidence, full new-title catalogue/reader review and human acceptance.

### SH2.2 - Goldilocks and the Three Bears

**Source:** traditional tale. Select a specifically documented historic telling; do not assume a later adaptation is rights-free. **Format:** quick read.

- Child-friendly telling based on curiosity, choices and consequences; no gratuitous frightening scenes.
- Lock three distinct bear sizes and stable bowls/chairs/beds/cottage. Indicative modern art: **4-5** scenes.
- Full Classic edition **optional** if a specific rights-verified version warrants it.
- **Acceptance:** readable quick-format, consistent character/prop sizes, approved copy and responsive images.

### SH2.3 - The Velveteen Rabbit

**Author/source:** Margery Williams, 1922; separately verify the original and William Nicholson illustration rights and later edition assets. **Format:** short illustrated chapter/picture story.

- Original Story House adaptation about love, belonging and becoming real; explicitly review the illness, disposal and ending for intended age.
- Consistent Velveteen Rabbit, Skin Horse and nursery, including deliberate changes in Rabbit's condition/appearance.
- Indicative modern art: **6-9** images in reviewed batches. Full Classic Text only after confirmed wording/edition clearance.
- **Acceptance:** emotional/story accuracy, verified rights and strong visual continuity through transformation.

### SH2.4 - Little Red Riding Hood

**Source:** traditional fairy tale; select and credit the exact Perrault, Grimm or other telling/translation used. **Format:** short story.

- An age-appropriate interpretation of the woods, grandmother, wolf and rescue/ending, with clear notes distinguishing the selected historic version from the new adaptation.
- Anchor cape, characters, woodland route and cottage. Indicative modern art: **4-6** images.
- **Acceptance:** coherent narrative, no unnecessarily graphic depiction, human review and independent source provenance.

### SH2.5 - The Emperor's New Clothes

**Author/source:** Hans Christian Andersen, 1837; choose a rights-cleared translation if applicable. **Format:** comic quick read.

- Focus on honesty, the tailors' scheme and social pressure without explicit nudity in art.
- Stable court, clothing/imaginary garments and parade compositions. Indicative modern art: **4-6** images.
- Consider Full Classic Text only where source-rights and age presentation justify it.
- **Acceptance:** funny and understandable, child-appropriate visuals, final manuscript/asset clearance.

**Stage 2 milestone:** SH2.1-SH2.5 individually approved and merged, **22 published titles**.

## Stage 3 - Longer illustrated classics

### SH2.6 - The Snow Queen

**Author/source:** Hans Christian Andersen, 1844, with specific translation/edition rights check. **Format:** illustrated multi-chapter fairy tale.

- Preserve the key arc across the traditional **seven stories**. Build a cohesive shorter Story House edition; conditional Full Classic only from a verified complete text.
- Anchor Gerda, Kay, Snow Queen, reindeer, icy palace and contrasting warm locations before generation.
- Indicative modern art: **15-22** images in per-story/chapter review batches.
- **Acceptance:** character/location continuity across settings, approved tone, chapter flow and long-session reader checks.

### SH2.7 - The Wind in the Willows

**Author/source:** Kenneth Grahame, 1908. **Format:** substantial chapter book.

- Plan around the **12 original chapters**; editorially assess a complete rights-cleared Full Classic edition and a separate shorter Story House Edition.
- Anchor Mole, Rat, Toad, Badger, riverbank, Wild Wood and Toad Hall. Distinguish original painterly art from potentially protected later illustrated editions.
- Indicative modern art: **20-30** scenes in bounded chapter groups, preserving lazy loading and sensible image density.
- **Acceptance:** complete narrative/source plan, consistent recurring cast, verified image provenance, saved progress and performance checks.

### SH2.8 - The Secret Garden

**Author/source:** Frances Hodgson Burnett, 1911. **Format:** substantial novel.

- Plan for the **27 original chapters** in any verified Full Classic and a separately crafted child-friendly Story House retelling.
- Deliberate editorial handling of dated class, colonial and disability-related language, **without silently altering a labelled historic Full Classic Text**.
- Anchor Mary, Colin, Dickon, manor, garden geography and its seasonal transformation.
- Indicative modern art: **24-36** chapter-distributed images, not 36 unreviewed up-front assets.
- **Acceptance:** human editorial review for historic context, character/environment continuity and responsive long-form reading.

**Stage 3 milestone:** SH2.6-SH2.8 approved and merged, **25 published titles**.

## Stage 4 - Original Quill's Valley Tales

### SH2.9 - The Missing Moonflower (working title)

**Format:** original 4-5 chapter illustrated mystery/adventure. **Series:** Quill's Valley Tales, Book 2.

- Set in Moonflower Glade, subject to an audit of its real in-game NPC, quest, landscape and historical canon.
- Create a complete original script and chapter/semantic block map **before** visual production; do not use lore inventions to impose gameplay requirements.
- Lock Quill and established returning characters, Moonflower Glade's physical world cues and a title-specific visual bible.
- Indicative modern art: **10-15** images; one original Story House edition only, no artificial historical/classic choice.
- **Acceptance:** manuscript and canon human-approved, complete illustrated narrative, original-rights metadata and correct series order.

### SH2.10 - The Rainbow Meadow Mystery (working title)

**Format:** original 4-5 chapter illustrated mystery. **Series:** Quill's Valley Tales, Book 3.

- Build a self-contained story within accepted Rainbow Meadow geography, windmill and gameplay lore; check continuity with the existing Valley stories.
- Approve script, mystery logic and intended age range, then recurring character/environment anchors.
- Indicative modern art: **10-15** images, selected reader-image modern cover, single original Story House edition.
- **Acceptance:** understandable mystery resolution, no world-canon contradiction, series integration, reader visual and functional review.

## Repeatable title-level work pattern

Each **SH2.n** work package is completed through bounded sub-checkpoints, split into multiple A/B/C steps as needed:

1. **A - Source and manuscript:** exact source/rights audit, editorial adaptation or original script, chapters and stable `blockId` anchors, approval.
2. **B - Visual bible and anchors:** establish recurring-character, environment, style, proportions, palette, image budget and continuity; approve representative anchors.
3. **C... - Illustration batches:** generate with ChatGPT, human-review small groups, stage in Drive, materialise via existing WIF/Actions pipeline, preserve original/historic art and separate rights; repeat without changing shared runtime.
4. **Penultimate - Integration:** wire approved assets, reuse an approved reader illustration for modern cover, generate/check `catalogue.json`, verify chapter and asset paths, accessibility, progress and discovery.
5. **Final - Acceptance/merge:** Cloudflare PR preview for human approval, reconcile actual diff, focused tests and documentation, then the authoritative exact-head CI and live-main Merge Gate. Rebase/update to latest main when needed before final qualification; merge only once green.

No ordinary content PR may modify shared StoryReaderOverlay/Story Library runtime, root `AGENTS.md`, `STATUS.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.json`, test scheduler or World/Just Games files. Shared defects go to separately scoped engineering/remediation work. No production deployment or user-facing public publication is authorised merely by accepting the roadmap.
