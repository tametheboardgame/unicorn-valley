# R6.5-WP19H1-H13 - Area-by-Area Final Polish Programme

Approved by David on 11 September 2026.

This programme begins only after R6.5-WP19H audio integration is complete. It replaces the previous vague idea of one final graphics-specific pass. Every playable area receives its own bounded work package before R6.5-WP19I integrated qualification.

## Governing principle

The first stage of every area package is human review, not implementation.

David must review the area on:

- laptop/desktop;
- tablet;
- phone.

He then records everything he thinks should be improved in that area. No agent-led visual redesign should begin before that feedback exists.

## Mandatory five-stage contract for every area

### Stage 1 - Human review

David explores the current released/candidate version of the area on laptop, tablet and phone and records all observed improvements, defects and visual opportunities.

The review may include graphics, scenery density, atmosphere, layout, paths, layering, collision, navigation, NPC placement/behaviour, interactions, responsive presentation, readability, local activities, transitions and anything else specific to that area.

### Stage 2 - Area audit

After David's review, inspect the area technically and visually. Reconcile the human feedback with:

- scenery density and composition;
- graphical consistency and depth/layering;
- paths, boundaries and collision;
- tap/click navigation and approach points;
- interactions and NPC behaviour;
- local subareas/interiors/activities;
- laptop/tablet/phone presentation;
- performance and object/lifecycle health;
- known feedback-led defects assigned to the area.

The audit may identify additional local issues, but must not discard or dilute the human feedback.

### Stage 3 - Remediation

Implement the agreed area tightening.

This is not restricted to bug fixing. Mild visual redesign is explicitly allowed where justified by the review, including adding/repositioning scenery, changing density/composition, improving area identity and correcting weak visual structure.

Preserve accepted progression, saves, rewards, quest semantics, movement/race rules and positive human feedback unless the package explicitly owns a bounded correction.

### Stage 4 - Cross-device validation

Re-test the completed area on laptop, tablet and phone. Add targeted automated regression and representative responsive visual evidence for the changed behaviour.

Do not consider an area complete because one desktop screenshot looks correct.

### Stage 5 - Human acceptance

David reviews the finished area and explicitly accepts or rejects the package. Do not close or merge across this gate without that acceptance.

## Inventory reconciliation before WP19H1

Before the first package begins, reconcile this list against the then-current playable scene/location inventory.

Any independently explorable area missing from the list must receive its own additional numbered package rather than being silently skipped. A small sublocation or interior may remain within its parent package only where it is not meaningfully an independent exploration area; record that decision.

This means H13 is not a hard maximum. H14+ may be added if the inventory shows additional distinct areas.

## Explicit current visual direction

Whispering Woods already has one requested direction for its eventual human review: consider substantially increasing tree density and strengthening its magical woodland identity. The exact implementation remains subject to the cross-device Stage 1 review rather than being pre-designed now.

The previously noted issue where some locations appear to show two path layers is also owned by the relevant area packages. Fix it where it occurs rather than applying a risky global graphics patch without area context.

## Package boundaries

Each area package should normally affect only that area plus shared systems where a proven shared defect makes a local-only fix inappropriate. If a shared change is required, regression-test other already-approved areas before closing the package.

Do not use an area pass as permission for major new gameplay systems or R7-scale content expansion. Small scenery additions, mild visual redesign, local interaction fixes and presentation improvements are explicitly in scope.

## Programme exit

The programme is complete only when:

- every current independently explorable area has a recorded package owner;
- every package has completed the five stages;
- every area has explicit David acceptance;
- any residual known issue has an explicit accepted exception;
- the integrated candidate is ready for R6.5-WP19I qualification.

R6.5-WP19I, WP18H and WP17 remain blocked until this programme is complete.
