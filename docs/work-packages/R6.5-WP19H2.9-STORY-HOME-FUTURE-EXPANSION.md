# R6.5-WP19H2.9 – Story Home & Future Expansion

**Status:** Complete / human-approved 19 September 2026

## Purpose

Make Moonflower Cottage story content depend on the canonical semantic room model rather than legacy
coordinates, while protecting deliberate capacity for later story displays and the future portal.

H2.9 does not implement fast travel or invent new story gameplay.

## Baseline findings

H2.0 already created semantic anchors for the egg nest, Willow, Nova, five future story/display sockets
and the portal bay. Visitor rendering and interaction already resolve their positions from those anchors.

The remaining legacy exception was the strange-egg world manager, which still used a private hard-coded
cottage nest position. Reserved future capacity was also duplicated as raw rectangle coordinates in the
room map rather than being owned by the semantic anchors themselves.

## Implementation

- the strange egg now renders and checks interaction distance through the canonical
  `cottage.egg-nest` anchor;
- egg presentation receives a stable semantic diagnostic name and remains on the existing growth,
  inspection and hatch service;
- the egg nest, five future story/display anchors and portal bay own explicit reservation footprints;
- `CottageInteriorMap` derives protected room zones from those semantic-anchor reservations rather than
  duplicating coordinates;
- `HomeDecorationService` rejects any authored decoration slot that intrudes into protected story
  capacity;
- the five future story anchors were repositioned into deliberately clear room capacity rather than
  overlapping the approved Cosy Corner decoration slot or current story content;
- Willow and Nova remain anchor-driven, with tests now guarding that every cottage friend-visit
  definition resolves to a semantic anchor whose purpose is `visitor`;
- the future portal bay has a deliberately inert architectural treatment, with no input, destination or
  fast-travel behaviour;
- the portal architecture is loaded behind the cottage story boundary so first-load budgets remain
  protected.

## Automated contracts

Unit contracts cover:

- exactly five future story/display anchors;
- explicit protection of the current egg nest plus future story and portal capacity;
- semantic visitor-anchor ownership;
- protected-zone coordinates being derived from their semantic anchors;
- all ordinary decoration slots remaining clear of protected capacity;
- protected capacity remaining clear of permanent collision footprints.

Browser coverage exercises:

- the live strange egg appearing at the canonical egg-nest position;
- one deliberate egg-growth inspection;
- transition from cracking to the existing hatch scene and successful Luma hatch completion;
- the future portal bay appearing at its canonical position while remaining non-interactive.

## Human gate

Use a save where the strange egg is present and confirm it still appears in the intended nest location,
can be inspected, grows normally and can hatch without disrupting the rebuilt cottage.

If a Willow or Nova cottage visit is available, confirm the visitor still appears in a sensible room
position and dialogue behaves normally.

Visually confirm the right-side future portal bay reads as deliberate empty cottage architecture rather
than an active portal, and that ordinary Decorate mode does not expose placement points inside protected
story/portal capacity.
