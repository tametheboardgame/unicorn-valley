# R6.5-WP19H6 - Crystal Brook Final Area Pass

## Current stage

**H6.0 baseline/ownership audit and H6.1 canonical master layout/ownership migration are complete and reviewed. H6.2 - Continuous Brook and Rainbow Meadow hydrology is the next bounded checkpoint and has not started.**

David selected Crystal Brook as the next exploration-area package on 3 October 2026 after R6.5-WP19H4 Rainbow Meadow completed its final architecture/documentation pass and merged to `main` at `04f3a71c0abc8c28645a5af1bbb4c179d0d81f77`.

R6.5-WP19H5 Rainbow Run is intentionally deferred. The current plan is to complete the remaining exploration areas first, then perform one coherent racing review covering the Rainbow Run hub, Crystal Cup hub, race entry/return flow, race family presentation and MG-WP11 racing improvement work. H6 therefore depends on the accepted H4 world baseline rather than on completion of H5.

This document records the approved Crystal Brook direction after a code/architecture audit of the current scene, map, story/depth managers, gateway systems, race integration, existing tests and the independent mini-game platform roadmap.

## Purpose

Rebuild Crystal Brook into a coherent authored river valley whose geography explains the wider world.

The defining idea is simple:

**the Brook is the map.**

A continuous stream should visibly run through the full area. The eastern/upstream water system feeds the Brook, the Brook crosses the valley through pools, shallows and stepping-stone bends, then the water continues through the western rocky gorge toward the waterfall and pool already established in Rainbow Meadow.

Crystal Brook should no longer read as several blue ellipses, a stroked stream, generic paths and late presentation overlays placed on top of an open green rectangle. It should read as a bounded rocky valley with one believable water system, deliberate routes, physical transition points, authored local activities and a clear relationship to Rainbow Meadow, Crystal Grotto, Whispering Woods and racing.

## Initial human requirements

H6 must directly incorporate the following approved direction:

1. **Continuous stream / hydrology**
   - Make the stream run through the whole area.
   - Treat it as the same water system that ultimately feeds the Rainbow Meadow waterfall/pool.
   - Rework the Rainbow Meadow entrance so the player comes out of a rocky cave/gorge while the stream continues past/upstream through the threshold.
   - Do not draw water as overlapping closed shapes with visible duplicate seam lines.

2. **Hard physical boundaries**
   - Give the area strong edges, primarily cliffs, irregular rocks and boulders.
   - Boundary collision should correspond to visible scenery rather than unexplained invisible walls.
   - Deliberate route openings remain for Rainbow Meadow, Crystal Grotto, Whispering Woods and the Crystal Cup Race Hub.

3. **Crystal merge mini-game**
   - Add a crystal-themed merge game inspired by the satisfying merge/progression structure of games such as Flambé: Merge & Cook, but without customer-serving fiction, energy timers or mobile monetisation-style waiting.
   - The game needs an in-world purpose that belongs to Crystal Brook.
   - It must comply with the independent Mini-Game Platform architecture from first implementation.

4. **At least one additional mini-game**
   - Add another local game so Crystal Brook has more repeatable play.
   - Approved direction is a crystal-rock version of checkers/draughts, provisionally called **Crystal Checkers**.

5. **Crystal Cup Race Hub**
   - Crystal Brook must no longer launch straight into the Crystal Cascade race.
   - The Brook should contain an authored access point to a separate **Crystal Cup Race Hub**.
   - The hub then owns access onward to the race.
   - H6 creates/establishes the hub transition and minimum functional hub shell only. Full racing presentation/mechanics review remains deferred with H5/MG-WP11.

## Audit findings

### 1. Water is currently fragmented

`CrystalBrookScene.createWater()` currently renders:

- an upper pool as two ellipses;
- a lower pool as two ellipses;
- a broad stroked stream between a limited set of mid-map points;
- a separate cascade/waterfall presentation injected later by `R6RegionGatewayArtManager`;
- additional waterfall/reflection-pool landmarks supplied by `CrystalBrookDepthWorldManager`.

These elements do not currently form one authoritative hydrological model.

The main stream in `CRYSTAL_BROOK_MAP.shallowStream` begins around the left/middle of the map and stops around the lower-right/middle rather than visibly connecting the region from the Rainbow Meadow threshold through the major water landmarks.

H6 should replace this with one canonical area-owned river/water model. Pools, widening/narrowing channels, shallows, deep zones, waterfall/cascade sections and threshold mouths should derive from that model.

### 2. The Rainbow Meadow threshold is functional but not area-owned

The Meadow ↔ Brook traversal is already canonical enough to preserve location/spawn semantics, but Crystal Brook-side presentation still comes from `R6RegionGatewayArtManager`, which injects a generic cave mouth into Crystal Brook.

The H4 Meadow-side gateway now presents a hidden waterfall/cave recess and an integrated water threshold. H6 should make the Brook-side geography explain the same transition: the player emerges from a rocky gorge/cave while the Brook water visibly continues through that gorge toward Rainbow Meadow.

The transition remains a real two-way world route. H6 should not change save/location semantics merely to improve presentation.

### 3. Crystal Brook has weak perimeter ownership

`CRYSTAL_BROOK_MAP.colliders` currently contains only a limited set of pools, cliff blocks, vegetation blocks and the cascade collision. Large parts of the 3500 × 2200 map therefore remain visually open even where the region should feel enclosed.

The new perimeter should be mostly rock-based, with variety in form rather than repeated identical boulders:

- slabs;
- spires;
- rounded rocks;
- lopsided rocks;
- stacked clusters;
- crystal-bearing rocks;
- short cliff shelves;
- occasional vegetation/reeds where appropriate.

Visible footprints and collision footprints must agree.

### 4. Paths and route presentation have split ownership

Crystal Brook currently combines:

- the broad scene-owned main path;
- the scene-owned Prism Grotto trail;
- a Woods route injected by `R6RegionGatewayArtManager`;
- a Crystal Cascade race route injected by `R6RegionGatewayArtManager`;
- branch guidance injected by `ExplorationGeometryPresentationManager`.

H6 should converge these into one canonical route/trail network. Shared traversal managers may still own transition behaviour where appropriate, but should not own competing Crystal Brook composition coordinates.

### 5. Static composition is spread across too many managers

Current Crystal Brook presentation is assembled by several owners:

- `CrystalBrookScene`;
- `EnvironmentProductionPresentationManager`;
- `ExplorationGeometryPresentationManager`;
- `R6RegionGatewayArtManager`;
- `R6RegionGatewayArtPerformanceManager`;
- `CrystalBrookDepthWorldManager`;
- `CrystalBrookStoryWorldManager`;
- supporting-resident/population systems.

This mirrors the architecture debt removed from Sunbeam Village and Rainbow Meadow.

The H6 target is not to force every behaviour into one file. It is to make **static area composition area-owned**, while story, progression, population and stateful behaviour consume canonical H6 positions rather than drawing competing replacement layers.

### 6. Existing story/exploration depth is worth preserving

Crystal Brook already contains useful content that should survive the rebuild:

- River Crystal and Singing Shell discoveries/collectables;
- Prism Grotto secret route;
- Ripple story content;
- Echo and the Crystal Song;
- Waterfall Mist Rainbow;
- Reflection Pool Stars;
- Stepping-Stone Chime;
- Crystal Grotto entrance/progression;
- atmospheric/time-sensitive discoveries.

H6 is a presentation/composition and local-activity expansion, not a reason to discard this existing exploration depth.

### 7. Character presentation is inconsistent

Echo already uses the supporting-resident/presence systems. Ripple is still manually assembled from primitive Phaser shapes inside `CrystalBrookStoryWorldManager`.

H6 should move Ripple to the modern unicorn visual language and ensure Echo/Ripple placement and interaction positions are derived from the final layout.

### 8. Current race access is too direct

`R5RegionGatewayManager` currently exposes `gateway:crystal-brook-crystal-cascade` at a hard-coded Brook position and launches `RaceScene` directly after selecting the Crystal Cascade course.

That is explicitly superseded by H6.

The Brook should transition into a Crystal Cup Race Hub. The hub is the place where the player chooses/starts the Crystal Cascade race. The actual race runtime remains preserved until the later racing review.

### 9. Mini-game platform dependency is real

The accepted `MINIGAMES-ROADMAP.md` and `docs/architecture/MINI-GAME-PLATFORM.md` require every new mini-game to have, from first implementation:

- one stable `MiniGameId`;
- one canonical gameplay implementation;
- one catalogue definition;
- manifest-backed loading;
- shared launch/session/return behaviour;
- Just Games exposure;
- world-compatible launch behaviour;
- context-safe persistence/rewards;
- touch/keyboard support;
- automated catalogue and launch/return validation.

At the H6.0 evidence baseline, **MG-WP0 has now merged to `main`** and the canonical `src/game/minigames/**` foundation exists: `MiniGameCatalogue`, `MiniGameSession`, `MiniGameLauncher` and `MiniGameOutcomeGateway` are present with manifest-backed contracts.

Since H6.0, **MG-WP1 and MG-WP2 have completed and merged to `main`**. Existing mini-game families use the shared launch/session contract, and sandbox rewards/persistence isolation is now established. MG-WP3-WP4 remain outstanding: Just Games catalogue/home exposure and future-game authoring guardrails are not yet complete.

Therefore H6.1-H6.7 may proceed independently, but **H6.9 and H6.10 must not implement bespoke activity launch architecture**. They remain blocked at H6.8 until MG-WP3-WP4 satisfy the remaining readiness contract.


### 10. H6.0 found additional late compatibility owners

The full H6.0 audit identified Brook-specific ownership beyond the original roadmap summary. Later retirement work must explicitly account for:

- `ExplorationPathPolishManager`, which repaints the Brook main route;
- `R6FinalPlaythroughCleanupManager`, which hides/replaces the earlier Woods/Cascade paths and forwards a large Crystal Cascade tap target;
- `FinalGraphicsTighteningManager`, which hides the original stream and draws another map-backed stream;
- `WorldLayerAlignmentManager`, which corrects depths for the R6 Brook production/cascade containers;
- `R65WorldExperiencePresentationManager`, which adds another set of crystal clusters and another Prism Grotto rock/mouth composition;
- `LegacyWorldFeedbackMigrationManager`, which contains Brook-specific feedback suppression.

These managers are not automatically “wrong”; they are explicit migration/retirement inputs. H6 must not leave them running against removed objects or allow them to recreate superseded composition.

### 11. H6.0 confirms structural coordinates are widely duplicated

The baseline shows that Brook structural coordinates currently exist across `CrystalBrookMap`, `CrystalBrookScene`, `ExplorationPathRoutes`, gateway managers, story/depth managers, resident content and Grotto return logic.

H6.1 must therefore canonicalise at least:

- west Meadow threshold and safe approach/arrival;
- Woods threshold and Brook-side return;
- future Crystal Cup hub route/threshold;
- Prism/Crystal Grotto exterior approach and safe return;
- main land-route network;
- river/pool/cascade landmark anchors;
- Ripple and Echo anchors/routes;
- activity-pocket reservations;
- story/discovery interaction anchors that are structural rather than narrative-only.

Where a story/content file legitimately owns narrative sequencing, it should consume area-owned positions instead of duplicating structural coordinates.

### 12. H6.0 records the legacy Crystal Cascade return topology

Current Brook race access is not merely a visual gate. `R5RegionGatewayManager`:

- starts Crystal Cascade directly from Brook at `2860,850`;
- records the Brook return anchor at `2660,900`;
- after the race's existing Meadow-oriented return flow, detects the completed Crystal Cascade run and redirects the player back into Crystal Brook at that saved return position.

H6.6 must replace this topology deliberately. The future hub must own the race start and race return before the player can return to Brook.

### 13. H6.0 performance starting point

The exact H6 starting build succeeds, but the existing performance policy already reports one baseline failure before H6.1 changes anything:

- 113 JavaScript chunks against a budget of 112.

Measured H6.0 starting values are approximately:

- entry: 490.3 KiB raw / 137.4 KiB gzip;
- initial/title/first-playable graph: 560.0 KiB gzip across 44 chunks;
- largest lazy chunk: 22.2 KiB gzip;
- total JavaScript: 2747.9 KiB raw / 791.6 KiB gzip.

H6 must not misattribute the pre-existing chunk-count failure to later Brook work, but later slices must also avoid worsening it without an explicit architecture decision.

## Governing implementation principles

H6 inherits the accepted H1-H4 rules:

- **one composition owner:** structural Crystal Brook coordinates belong to the area-owned map/layout;
- **river first:** water geometry establishes the composition before detail props are scattered around it;
- **remove old producers rather than hiding output:** superseded Crystal Brook overlays should be retired at source;
- **physical boundaries:** visible cliffs/rocks explain where the player can and cannot walk;
- **physical routes:** routes continue into caves, passes, hubs and off-map thresholds rather than ending as decorative stubs;
- **open water joins:** do not stroke closed pool/stream shapes across intentional joins and create double overlap lines;
- **behaviour consumes layout:** stories, residents, discoveries and gateways should consume canonical positions instead of owning independent magic coordinates;
- **environmental affordance over emoji markers:** the place/object should communicate interest, with the standard contextual interaction prompt appearing on approach;
- **preserve accepted progression:** do not rewrite saves, quest semantics, discoveries, rewards or race rules outside the explicitly owned H6 change;
- **mini-game platform compliance from day one:** no world-first H6 game may be created as a one-off scene integration to “migrate later”;
- **bounded checkpoints:** complete and review one logical H6 slice at a time;
- **performance discipline:** preserve the WP19H0 startup/lazy-loading budget and keep optional games appropriately lazy;
- **human visual gate:** substantive composition/visual slices require a trustworthy preview and explicit acceptance before later work depends on them.

## Target composition

Exact coordinates are owned by H6.1/H6.2 visual implementation, but the intended relationship is:

- **west / Rainbow Meadow threshold:** rocky cave/gorge arrival with the Brook continuing through the gorge toward Rainbow Meadow;
- **central valley:** the principal meandering stream, stepping-stone crossings, banks, reeds, crystals and exploration pockets;
- **north/east upstream:** stronger rock shelves/cascade language, with the water clearly flowing into the main Brook;
- **south/east:** Prism Grotto / Crystal Grotto approach retained as a distinct side route rather than swallowed by the main river;
- **one calm bank / activity pocket:** Crystalarium merge-game location;
- **one separate social/play pocket:** Crystal Checkers rock table;
- **race route:** authored Crystal Cup Race Hub entrance, not a direct `RaceScene` trigger;
- **Woods route:** clear pass/trail toward Whispering Woods, preserving the future H8 ownership boundary.

The whole region should feel enclosed by terrain while still having readable, generous movement corridors.

## Crystalarium design contract

The merge game is intentionally inspired by the satisfying progression structure of merge games such as Flambé: Merge & Cook, not by their commercial/mobile economy.

Keep:

- obvious merge chains;
- satisfying visual upgrades;
- short-term requested targets;
- discovery of higher-tier forms;
- a board that develops visibly over a session.

Do not copy:

- customer/restaurant fiction;
- energy timers;
- recharge waiting;
- advertisements;
- monetisation loops;
