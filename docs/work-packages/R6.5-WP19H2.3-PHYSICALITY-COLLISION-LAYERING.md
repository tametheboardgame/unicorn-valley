# R6.5-WP19H2.3 – Physicality, Collision & Layering

## Purpose

Make the human-approved H2.2 Moonflower Cottage furnishings behave like physical room objects rather than flat artwork with broad prototype blockers.

David approved H2.2 on 16 September 2026 and explicitly asked to start H2.3. After the H2.3 walkaround and two final collision corrections around the bed rails and lower room wall, David approved H2.3 on 16 September 2026 and asked to start H2.4.

## Scope delivered

- replaced the old one-rectangle-per-furnishing collision model with floor-footprint blockers that track the production silhouettes;
- included separate physical blockers for the two tea chairs rather than hiding them inside one large table rectangle;
- kept the fireplace and treasure shelf continuous with the back-wall boundary without blocking empty floor around their upper artwork;
- kept the Wonderbook lectern physically blocked while preserving its interaction approach;
- split the bottom wall collision around the approved architectural exit opening and protected only the exit frame/posts;
- used canonical furniture depth anchors so the player can render behind or in front of furniture according to foot position;
- sorted the cottage player by the bottom of the physics body rather than a fixed depth value;
- aligned cottage visitor depth with the same Y-aware room model;
- widened the bed-side physical guard enough to protect the full visible unicorn head/tail, not just the narrower physics body;
- moved the bottom wall blocker inward so the unicorn stops before the visible wall rather than appearing to stand on it;
- preserved keyboard, touch, Gallop, interactions, save state and scene transitions.

## Canonical ownership

- `CottageInteriorMap.ts` owns permanent furniture positions, H2.3 physical footprints and permanent-furniture depth anchors.
- `CottageFurnitureRenderer.ts` renders permanent furnishings at depths derived from those authored anchors.
- `CottageInteriorScene.ts` builds static collision from the map and owns the moving player's foot-based depth.
- `CottageFriendVisitManager.ts` uses Y-aware visitor depth so visitors participate coherently in room layering.
- H2.4 separately evolves the approved bed footprint into a walk-in sleep arrangement.

## Acceptance

Human gate passed on 16 September 2026. Deliberate cottage walkaround found only two final issues: visible head/tail clipping at the bed sides and the lower wall collider sitting too low. Both were corrected and the follow-up preview was approved as “much better”.
