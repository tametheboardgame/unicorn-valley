# R6.5-WP19H2.3 – Physicality, Collision & Layering

## Purpose

Make the human-approved H2.2 Moonflower Cottage furnishings behave like physical room objects rather than flat artwork with broad prototype blockers.

David approved H2.2 on 16 September 2026 and explicitly asked to start H2.3.

## Scope

- replace the old one-rectangle-per-furnishing collision model with floor-footprint blockers that track the production silhouettes;
- include separate physical blockers for the two tea chairs rather than hiding them inside one large table rectangle;
- keep the fireplace and treasure shelf continuous with the back-wall boundary without blocking empty floor around their upper artwork;
- keep the Wonderbook lectern physically blocked while preserving its interaction approach;
- split the bottom wall collision around the approved architectural exit opening and protect only the exit frame/posts;
- use canonical furniture depth anchors so the player can render behind or in front of furniture according to foot position;
- sort the cottage player by the bottom of the physics body rather than a fixed depth value;
- align cottage visitor depth with the same Y-aware room model;
- preserve keyboard, touch, Gallop, interactions, save state and scene transitions.

## Canonical ownership

- `CottageInteriorMap.ts` owns permanent furniture positions, H2.3 physical footprints and permanent-furniture depth anchors.
- `CottageFurnitureRenderer.ts` renders permanent furnishings at depths derived from those authored anchors.
- `CottageInteriorScene.ts` builds static collision from the map and owns the moving player's foot-based depth.
- `CottageFriendVisitManager.ts` uses Y-aware visitor depth so visitors participate coherently in room layering.
- H2.4 will separately split the bed into rear/foreground layers for the dedicated sleep interaction; H2.3 does not implement sleep.

## Acceptance criteria

- the unicorn cannot walk through the physical base of the bed, sofa, table, chairs, fireplace, shelf or Wonderbook lectern;
- empty visual space around furnishings is not blocked by obsolete broad rectangles;
- the player can circulate behind and in front of suitable furniture and the visual order changes naturally;
- fireplace and shelf stay behind a player standing on the floor in front of them;
- table/chairs and sofa do not permanently render over or under the player regardless of position;
- the approved exit opening is physically open while its side frame remains solid;
- interaction approach points and visitor anchors remain reachable;
- back-wall collision still prevents the whole unicorn entering the wall;
- keyboard, touch navigation and Gallop remain usable;
- no H2.4 sleep behaviour or H2.5 Decorate-mode changes are introduced.

## Human gate

Deliberately walk around every permanent furnishing on the deployed preview. Approve only when there are no obvious pass-throughs, invisible blockers or incorrect front/back layering. Pay particular attention to the table/chairs, sofa, bed edges, fireplace, Wonderbook and exit gap.
