# R6.5-WP19H2.4 – Bed & Sleep

## Purpose

Turn the approved Moonflower Cottage bed into a natural home interaction using the existing room geometry, production unicorn and atmospheric time system.

David approved H2.3 on 16 September 2026 and explicitly asked to start H2.4.

## Scope

- split the bed art into rear and foreground layers so a unicorn inside the bed sits visually between them;
- evolve the H2.3 bed blocker into a U-shaped headboard/side-rail arrangement with an open foot entry;
- bind the sleep action to a semantic `cottage.sleep` anchor at the bed centre;
- expose `Sleep` only when the player has walked into the bed-centre interaction radius;
- settle the unicorn into a vertical sleeping pose with its head above the foreground bedding;
- fade to black and show `A lovely night's sleep`;
- reset the canonical `AtmosphericTimeService` to a fresh automatic morning;
- wake the player on the floor at the foot of the bed, restore ordinary physics/presentation, then fade back;
- provide stable future audio event hooks without shipping new audio assets;
- shorten transition motion coherently when reduced motion is enabled;
- preserve the approved H2.3 room collision/layering everywhere outside the bed.

## Canonical ownership

- `CottageSemanticAnchors.ts` owns the semantic bed-centre sleep trigger.
- `CottageInteriorMap.ts` owns bed entry/wake geometry and the H2.4 U-shaped bed blockers.
- `CottageFurnitureRenderer.ts` owns bed rear/foreground presentation.
- `CottageSleepController.ts` owns the sleep transition state machine, reduced-motion timings and future audio hooks.
- `AtmosphericTimeService.ts` owns the canonical reset-to-morning operation.
- `CottageInteriorScene.ts` owns the sleep interaction and connects the player to the controller.

## Acceptance criteria

- player can enter the bed naturally from the foot without walking through either side rail or headboard;
- the sleep interaction appears only once the player is genuinely inside the bed-centre area;
- activating sleep positions the unicorn between the bed layers with its head visibly above the bedding;
- gameplay input cannot move the player during the sleep sequence;
- the screen fades to black and displays exactly `A lovely night's sleep`;
- waking restores the player outside the bed at its foot with normal movement/collision;
- leaving the cottage after sleeping presents the outdoor world as morning;
- any previous manual atmospheric-time override is cleared by the canonical sleep reset rather than competing with morning;
- reduced-motion mode skips the settle movement and uses brief opacity transitions rather than the normal animation timings;
- future audio can bind to explicit sleep settle/asleep/wake hooks without changing the sleep state machine;
- no H2.5 Decorate-mode work is pulled forward.

## Human gate

Walk into the bed from the foot, trigger Sleep, check the tucked-in pose/message/fade, wake and leave the cottage. Approve only when the bed entry feels natural and the outdoor world is clearly morning afterwards.
