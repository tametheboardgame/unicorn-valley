# R6.5-WP19H2.1 – Room Shell, Layout & Proportions

## Purpose

H2.1 rebuilds the spatial composition of Moonflower Cottage before permanent furniture receives its production-art pass. The goal is a believable home-scale room with clear zones and circulation, not a long prototype corridor with objects distributed around hard-coded coordinates.

## Approved H2.0 prerequisite

David approved the H2.0 human gate on 16 September 2026 and explicitly authorised H2.1 to begin. H2.0 CI also completed successfully before this checkpoint started.

## Layout direction

The H2.1 room is organised around five readable zones:

- **Arrival:** centred south entrance with a clear route into the room.
- **Sleep / hearth:** bed and fireplace on the left, with enough open floor around them for the later H2.3/H2.4 physicality and sleep passes.
- **Social centre:** tea table and central rug form the middle of the home without blocking the entrance route.
- **Display / reading:** treasure display and Wonderbook occupy the right side without competing with the main circulation path.
- **Future expansion:** a protected portal bay and protected story/display anchors remain deliberately clear of permanent furniture.

## Engineering rules

- `CottageInteriorMap.ts` owns the authored shell and permanent-furniture layout geometry.
- `CottageInteriorScene.ts` renders the current placeholder furniture from that shared geometry rather than copying positions and dimensions.
- Collision definitions must resolve from the same shared layout values used by presentation.
- `CottageSemanticAnchors.ts` may move anchors for the new shell, but consumers continue to bind by semantic ID.
- H2.1 must not perform the H2.2 permanent-furniture art redesign, H2.3 physical layering work, H2.4 sleep implementation or H2.5 Decorate-mode cleanup.

## Acceptance criteria

- room footprint is materially more compact and proportionate to the production unicorn;
- wall/floor balance reads as a room rather than a long flat map;
- entrance route into the room is clear;
- major permanent furniture has deliberate zones and sensible circulation gaps;
- fireplace and key room content are not positioned beneath persistent UI presentation;
- portal bay and future story/display capacity are spatially reserved;
- semantic anchors remain in bounds and usable;
- presentation geometry and collision geometry are driven by the same canonical layout values;
- existing door, Wonderbook, visitor and decoration systems remain functional;
- no H2.2+ behaviour is pulled forward.

## Human gate

Review the deployed cottage as a layout-only checkpoint. Approve H2.1 only if the room reads as a believable home with a clear entrance, understandable zones, comfortable circulation and obvious capacity for later furniture/detail work.
