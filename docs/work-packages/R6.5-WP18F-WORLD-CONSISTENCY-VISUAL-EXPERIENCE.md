# R6.5-WP18F - World Consistency, Visual Quality and Experience Improvements

## Objective

Address the non-shell world-consistency, character-presentation, environmental-readability and selected daughter-led experience issues exposed by the WP17 playthrough, while preserving areas and activities that already received strong positive feedback.

This is a targeted quality/experience package, not a licence for indiscriminate art replacement.

## Delivered

- `CoreNpcPresenceService` makes Nova's race-host, picnic and cottage states mutually exclusive using existing save/progression authorities.
- Nova's canonical world body and Rainbow Meadow interaction target follow that authority, while stale duplicate/placeholder representations are removed.
- Core cottage visitors use production character bodies where available.
- Reusable NPC mane/neck coverage and saved-appearance-aware player mane coverage improve the exposed-neck silhouette without changing creator/save semantics.
- Player mane synchronisation is code-split through a lazy world manager to preserve the application-entry budget.
- Shell Cove has stronger enclosed-water/rock/shell composition plus a small unicorn sandcastle.
- Crystal Brook has clearer crystal clusters and a Prism Grotto destination treatment.
- Sunbeam Village shops have additional function-specific exterior props while retaining their existing entrances/interiors.
- Whispering Woods has restrained magical pools, motes and highlights while preserving its established identity.
- Hollow Tree Nook mushrooms have one lightweight `Say hello` interaction.
