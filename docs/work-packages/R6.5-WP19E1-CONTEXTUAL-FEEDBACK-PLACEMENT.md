# R6.5-WP19E1 - Contextual Feedback Placement Remediation

Status: planned after WP19E human approval; must complete before WP19F.

Origin: David's WP19E human review on 11 September 2026. The conversation system is substantially improved, but non-dialogue world feedback still uses legacy top-screen placement that competes with the fixed HUD and does not distinguish guidance from local environmental reactions.

## Goal

Give non-dialogue feedback one consistent visual language that tells the player whether a message is guidance about what to do next or a small reaction to the thing she just touched, without covering the top navigation shell.

## Feedback classes

### 1. Guidance / next-step hints

Examples:

- "The Prism Grotto trail ends at a cool crystal doorway. Echo has been listening nearby and may know how to open it."
- equivalent quest/context hints that explain the next useful action or nearby person/place.

Presentation:

- move out of the legacy top-centre banner area;
- use the lower feedback zone occupied by the conversation system, but as a smaller non-modal guidance card rather than a speech card;
- visually distinct from dialogue: no character portrait/name ribbon, and use a separate guidance colour family (target direction: pale aqua/mint with a stronger teal/blue edge, subject to implementation review);
- readable on desktop, landscape tablet, landscape phone and portrait phone;
- must not overlap the contextual Talk/Interact/Enter action control;
- may remain visible long enough to read but must not lock movement or require dismissal unless the source interaction explicitly requires acknowledgement.

### 2. Local environmental reactions / observations

Examples:

- "The pebble skipped across the stream — plip, plop."
- a crystal chiming, flower reacting, water splashing, object wobbling or similar small world response.

Presentation:

- do not use the lower guidance card or the global top HUD area;
- show a compact anchored callout close to the world object/interaction that caused it;
- use the same spatial idea as a Wonderbook discovery callout, but a clearly different style and lower visual weight so it cannot be mistaken for a permanent discovery/unlock;
- target direction: small warm/gold/peach reaction bubble or tag, subject to implementation review;
- auto-dismiss after a short readable duration;
- follow camera/viewport bounds so the callout never clips off-screen or lands under fixed HUD controls;
- queue or replace repeated reactions safely so rapid interactions do not create stacked unreadable text.

### 3. Existing categories that remain separate

- NPC/quest dialogue remains owned by `WorldConversationPresenter` / `DialogueCard`.
- Wonderbook discoveries retain their own discovery presentation and meaning.
- The contextual action button (Talk / Interact / Enter / Start) remains the WP19D interaction affordance and is not replaced by feedback text.
- Errors/confirmations keep their own explicit semantics; this package may move them only where required to eliminate overlap and must not turn failures into ambient feedback.

## Implementation scope

- inventory all current legacy top-screen world feedback producers and classify each as guidance, local environmental reaction, discovery, error/confirmation or obsolete duplication;
- introduce or consolidate reusable presenters for guidance and local reaction feedback rather than hand-positioning each call site;
- migrate the identified legacy banners/callouts to the correct presenter;
- remove retired top-centre feedback only after all call sites are accounted for;
- preserve existing quest, interaction, reward and discovery semantics exactly once;
- avoid changing dialogue content or interaction distance/collision behaviour in this package.

## Validation

- desktop/laptop, landscape tablet, landscape phone and portrait phone;
- rotation/resize while guidance is visible;
- guidance and contextual action visible together without overlap;
- local reaction close to source object at left/right/top/bottom viewport edges;
- rapid repeated environmental interactions do not stack indefinitely;
- Wonderbook discovery and local reaction are visually and semantically distinguishable;
- no old feedback banner appears underneath/behind the fixed top HUD;
- reduced-motion behaviour where any entry/exit animation is used;
- full browser regression and performance budget remain green.

## Human gate

David should review at least:

1. one guidance message in Crystal Brook (the Echo / Prism Grotto case is the reference example);
2. one small local environmental reaction such as a pebble/crystal/water interaction;
3. one Wonderbook discovery beside those treatments to confirm the three meanings are visually distinct;
4. the same guidance on the Samsung Galaxy Tab S8 landscape reference device.

## Acceptance

- no ordinary non-dialogue world feedback competes with the top HUD;
- guidance appears in the lower feedback zone with its own visual identity;
- small environmental reactions appear near the thing that reacted, not as global banners;
- Talk/Interact/Enter remains the sole contextual action affordance;
- dialogue, discovery and feedback categories are immediately distinguishable;
- no progression, quest, save or reward semantics change.
