# R6.5-WP16 - Valley Tidy-up, Balance and Content Polish

## Goal

Resolve accumulated small defects and make the broadened R6.5 content feel coherent, readable and balanced before the R6.5-WP17 human playthrough/readiness gate.

## Canonical source

This package implements the WP16 section of `docs/07V-R6.5-VALLEY-COMPLETENESS-BREADTH.md` and reconciles the released-R6 playthrough feedback captured before R6.5 expansion.

Accepted starting baseline: `bdc86e05472d0f061e3b748cc42706cc6541f523` (R6.5-WP15 merge via PR #144).

## Mandatory sweep

### 1. Released-R6 playthrough backlog reconciliation

Re-audit each previously reported issue against the current R6.5 build and fix it if it still exists. Do not blindly reimplement work that a later package already resolved.

Items to verify include:

- mobile unicorn customisation/marker UI was cramped and visually unclear; progressive disclosure or equivalent simplification should keep the screen readable on phone layouts;
- small mobile text and unclear interaction affordance around prompts such as `Talk to Pip`; important actions should read as actions and remain obvious in portrait and landscape;
- hint/help text should not compete with movement controls or the game viewport on mobile;
- legacy circular placeholders must not sit over production unicorns;
- Sunbeam Village shop presentation should now match the usefulness of the implemented interiors rather than reading as weak facades;
- point-and-click backwards movement must not flicker the unicorn between forward/backward facing every other step;
- the Crystal Brook entrance/path should read as a deliberate route into the region rather than an awkward race-adjacent transition.

### 2. Mobile interaction and readability sweep

- inspect touch target sizing and spacing across global HUD, Wonderbook, shops, activities, race UI, dialogue, customisation and major world interactions;
- ensure important text remains legible on supported phone/tablet layouts;
- verify portrait and landscape behaviour where the game supports both;
- remove overlapping prompts, controls or panels that hide gameplay or each other;
- preserve keyboard/controller support while keeping touch the primary child-facing standard.

### 3. NPC crowding, navigation and world clarity

- inspect all major outdoor regions and authored interiors for resident overlap, blocked routes, interaction competition and awkward spawn/path positions;
- preserve autonomous-life/story-anchor rules from `docs/07X-R6.5-AUTONOMOUS-UNICORN-LIFE.md`;
- sweep paths, signs, gateways and entrances for child-readable orientation;
- remove duplicate, obsolete or legacy presentation elements that conflict with production art.

### 4. Quest pacing and reward balance

- inspect substantive R6.5 quests for excessive backtracking, unclear hand-offs or repeated low-value collection beats;
- keep hints useful without over-directing exploration;
- verify Shimmer, friendship, item and decoration rewards are proportionate and duplicate-safe;
- ensure no required progression depends on grind or repeated farming.

### 5. Shop/economy balance

- audit current prices against normal earnings from quests, discoveries, races and repeatable activities;
- make desirable early purchases attainable through varied normal play;
- verify stock unlocks, sold/owned state and one-time purchase rules remain clear;
- preserve safe touch purchasing and duplicate-tap protection.

### 6. Race/reward balance

- audit all five regular courses and Rainbow Cup participation for assistance-friendly completion;
- ensure race rewards remain useful without requiring first place;
- verify Cup/ribbon progress remains consistent across save/reload and Wonderbook/hub display;
- do not weaken race accessibility to create artificial difficulty.

### 7. Region density and dead-zone sweep

For Moonflower Glade/Cottage, Sunbeam Village, Rainbow Meadow/Run, Crystal Brook/Grotto, Whispering Woods and Starlight Beach:

- reconcile the final build against the R6.5 interaction-density contract;
- verify each major outdoor region has at least 8 meaningful interaction points unless an explicit accepted exception is documented;
- verify at least 3 optional environmental toys/observations, 2 secrets/discoveries/collection opportunities, 1 material story use, 1 revisit reason, 1 persistent reaction and 1 strong orientation landmark;
- find long stretches with no meaningful action and either improve them using existing systems/content or document why they are acceptable;
- avoid adding filler interactions solely to satisfy a count.

### 8. Performance, load and deployment hardening

- keep the hard application-entry limit at **520 KiB**;
- retain the R6.5 interim total bundle envelope through WP15 unless the existing contract explicitly says otherwise;
- check larger-content load behaviour, lazy chunks and diagnostics isolation;
- run formatting, lint, type-check, unit tests, production build, static smoke, performance budget, full Chromium playtest and Chromium/Firefox/WebKit compatibility;
- do not perform production deployment without explicit user approval.

## Implementation order

1. Establish an evidence-based audit table for every mandatory sweep item and classify each as already resolved, needs fix, or accepted exception.
2. Fix high-impact child-facing/mobile issues first.
3. Reconcile movement/navigation/crowding and route clarity.
4. Apply economy, quest and race balance corrections only where evidence shows a real mismatch.
5. Run the region-density reconciliation and make bounded density/clarity improvements without inventing filler.
6. Remove duplicate/legacy visual residue.
7. Run the full technical gate and freeze an exact validated head.
8. Merge WP16 only when green, then stop at R6.5-WP17 for the required human playthrough/readiness decision.

## Acceptance

1. No known blocker or obvious high-impact presentation defect remains.
2. Every major region meets the R6.5 interaction-density contract or has a documented, explicit accepted exception.
3. Previously captured R6 mobile/readability/navigation/visual issues have been individually reconciled against the current build rather than silently forgotten.
4. Important child-facing actions and text remain readable and touch-safe on the target mobile/tablet form factors.
5. NPCs do not materially obstruct navigation or compete confusingly for interaction.
6. Quest, shop and race rewards support varied normal play without grind, punitive first-place requirements or duplicate-award exploits.
7. Paths, signs and entrances are visually understandable, including the route into Crystal Brook.
8. Duplicate/legacy placeholders or obsolete visual layers do not conflict with production presentation.
9. The project remains performant with the **520 KiB application-entry hard limit unchanged**.
10. Formatting, lint, type-check, unit tests, production build, static smoke, performance budget, full Chromium playtest and Chromium/Firefox/WebKit compatibility all pass on one exact head.
11. No production deployment is performed without explicit user approval.
12. R7 remains blocked until the R6.5-WP17 human gate is explicitly released.

## Guardrails

- This is a reconciliation/polish package, not an excuse for broad new feature systems.
- Prefer fixing existing content and presentation over adding new content purely to inflate counts.
- Preserve save compatibility and existing progression authorities.
- Never weaken or increase the 520 KiB application-entry budget.
- Do not begin R7 after technical completion. WP17 is a hard human gate.
