# R6.5-WP19SH1.2B - Direct Story House Access from Title

## Purpose

Make Story House a first-class reading mode rather than requiring the player to start or continue
exploration, travel to Sunbeam Village and enter the Story House interior before opening the
library.

## Scope

- add a clear **Story House** action to the healthy title menu for both fresh and returning players;
- open the existing `StoryReaderOverlay` directly while `TitleScene` remains active behind it;
- do not boot an exploration/world/interior scene;
- disable title actions while the reader owns interaction and restore them when the reader closes;
- expose the same action through the touch/mobile title DOM layer;
- preserve minimum 48 px touch targets in short landscape layouts;
- preserve existing New Game, Continue, My Unicorn and Settings semantics;
- reuse the existing Story House library, reading-progress and illustration-choice services.

## Non-goals

- no duplicate Story House/library scene;
- no new reader implementation;
- no save-schema bump;
- no change to world access to the physical Story House;
- no change to story content or illustration assets.

## Acceptance

- a fresh player can open Story House from the title screen without first creating a unicorn;
- a returning player can do the same without loading the world;
- closing Story House returns to the same title menu state;
- no exploration scene becomes active merely to read;
- desktop keyboard/pointer and touch/mobile title controls expose the action;
- short phone landscape retains 48 px actions and remains inside safe bounds;
- existing title actions keep their established behaviour.

## Validation

During implementation:

- Biome formatting/lint/static architecture;
- ownership-selected unit/browser checks;
- H0.5 title-screen browser regression, including direct reader opening;
- production build/static smoke where selected by policy.

Before merge, run the authoritative package gate required by repository policy.
