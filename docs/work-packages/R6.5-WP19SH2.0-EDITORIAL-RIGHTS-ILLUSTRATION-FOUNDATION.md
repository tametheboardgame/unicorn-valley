# SH2.0 — Editorial, rights and illustration foundation

## Baseline and authorisation

- **Started:** 16 October 2026 on `agent/story-house-sh2-0-editorial-foundation`.
- **Parent roadmap:** [SH2 ten-title expansion](R6.5-WP19SH2-STORY-HOUSE-LIBRARY-EXPANSION.md).
- **Satisfied dependency:** SH1.7 and parent SH1 completed; SH1.7 consolidation PR #325 merged as `e73fca9d2b59fbd49f7ba776678b4eea0da83f2a`. Final handover PR #327 merged as `d45859a69dd0bb89cb32f1d7d44d401a486fe2e9`.
- **Baseline:** 17 published books + one hidden sampler. Target after SH2.1–SH2.10 is 27 published books.
- **Work status:** research and design *in progress*. The ten-title slate is accepted for planning, but exact source editions, manuscripts, original-illustration sets and final production briefs are subject to the human/editorial gate.

## Scope: bounded SH2.0 outputs

1. **Catalogue and stable IDs.** Verify all proposed IDs are new and agree with the existing `public/stories/<slug>/book.json` structure, `coverSets`, `editions`, series metadata and generated catalogue contract.
2. **Source and rights register.** Distinguish underlying story, precise language/translation and particular text edition, original source illustrations, new generated illustration set, cover and publication/typographic layout. Record source bibliographic details, author/translator/artist death years where known, territory assumptions and evidence/unknowns; no unsupported `public-domain` assertion.
3. **Editorial brief for each of ten titles.** Working narrative, audience, age-sensitive points, reading-mode/chapters and unique semantic scene anchors, modern-cover and illustration budget; condition any Full Classic edition on a verified complete and suitable source.
4. **Cross-title visual foundation.** Establish shared painterly art rules, approved preceding-character continuity for Peter Rabbit and existing Valley Tales, composition/crop/accessibility rules, sensible iterative anchor and image-generation gates.
5. **Canonical Valley reference check.** Use established Quill, Rowan, Moonflower Glade and Rainbow Meadow lore for the two original tales; defer unverified world details to their separate manuscript decision gate.
6. **Production and testing gates.** A reproducible per-title checklist for asset staging through Google Drive → WIF → GitHub Actions, metadata, chapter/block ID integrity, `story:catalogue` generation/check, Story Library regressions, visual preview, protected CI and merge.
7. **Review and handover.** Present the rights/edition choices, ten title outlines, production sequence, visual bibles and unresolved matters for user approval. SH2.1 cannot begin bulk generation until the human gate is satisfied.

## Authority and invariants

- `docs/story-library-authoring.md`, `docs/story-house/GENERATED-ASSET-PIPELINE.md` and the SH2 parent roadmap govern implementation.
- Each title owns its content/manifests/visual brief and its own short-lived Story House PR. No shared reader rewrite, World/Just Games changes or edits to `PROJECT_STATE.json`/`STATUS.md` as a Story House PR.
- Maintain a single catalogue card per title, separate text-edition progress, independent illustration style and complete existing reader/legacy save behaviour.
- Modern covers normally reuse a human-approved reader image at a centre-safe 3:4 crop; dedicated cover-only art requires a recorded human-approved exception.
- No new image is considered approved merely because it has been generated; source archives, older pictures and staged Drive files are preserved pending explicit approval before deletion.
- New story manuscript and art are **not authorised for publication** by rights research alone. No public-domain claim inferred solely from Project Gutenberg's US-specific notice.
- No production deployment or external publication in SH2.0.

## Acceptance

- Ten existing-free stable IDs, individually useful title briefs, proper UK-first and intended-distribution territory rights status, no conflation of original text with later translation/illustration/edition.
- Coherent Story House / Full Classic decision per title; Full Classic conditional on a *complete verified edition*, never blindly copied from a mixed-source ebook.
- Coherent painterly visual direction and staged per-title anchor/image budgets, with named continuity dependencies and child-safe editorial handling.
- Verified original-tales canon against the current World content before final manuscript approval (flagged unknowns are explicit).
- Production checklist maps to current reader service, Drive/WIF staging and validated test suite; no changes in runtime and no title published.
- **Human acceptance:** review/approve the ten briefs, source choices, reading-mode plans, illustration direction, production order and unresolved rights restrictions before SH2.1.
- Final documentation PR meets selected draft checks; if moved to Ready for review it must pass current authoritative exact-head CI/Merge Gate. Do not mark SH2.0 `complete` while the human gate is pending.

## Known uncertainties at start

- Project Gutenberg's US copyright label is not sufficient for UK/global rights clearance; translators and illustrators need separate identification. In particular, the US Gutenberg *Wind in the Willows* illustrated file attributes pictures to Paul Bransom (d. 1979), so its images are **not presumed UK public-domain**.
- Traditional variants of Goldilocks/Red Riding Hood can diverge sharply in ending; a specific source version and adaptation choice must be approved.
- Original Valley Tales must match actual game lore, not an improvised Quill or Rowan history.
- The independently carried-forward **Duck Bread Baker rights investigation, issue #326**, is not part of licensing any new SH2 content and must remain visible.
