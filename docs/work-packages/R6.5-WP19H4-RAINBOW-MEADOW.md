# R6.5-WP19H4 - Rainbow Meadow Final Polish

## Current stage

**H4.4 - Crystal Brook hero gateway and right-side composition rebuild is in active redesign following human review. H4.3 was visually approved and merged on 1 October 2026; the first H4.4 cave/descent concept was explicitly rejected as compositionally weak and remains unmerged.**

David selected Rainbow Meadow as the next area after completing and approving R6.5-WP19H3 Sunbeam Village on 30 September 2026. The initial review explicitly identified that Picnic Hill should be moved, the Crystal Brook entrance should be moved and made more coherent, Nova still reads as an old character design, the south-west/bottom-left should gain a playable unicorn disc sport, entrance/exit roads should visibly run off-map, and obsolete visual residue should be removed rather than hidden.

This document records the agreed detailed H4 plan following a code/visual architecture audit. H4 is intentionally open-ended: H4.1-H4.10 are the currently approved substantive slices, but additional H4.11+ slices may be added from human review. Do not pre-allocate the final H4 number to hardening. The final consolidation/responsive/regression/hardening/documentation/qualification slice receives the next unused H4.x number only when David explicitly confirms that substantive Rainbow Meadow work is complete.

## Purpose

Bring Rainbow Meadow to the same authored, coherent standard achieved in the approved Sunbeam Village pass while preserving the Meadow's distinct identity.

Rainbow Meadow should read as the valley's **open, active outdoor countryside**: broad grass, recreation, nature, picnic space and the Rainbow Run sports hub, with clear physical routes to Sunbeam Village, Rainbow Run and Crystal Brook. It should not feel like a flat canvas with individually added feature islands or UI-like interaction markers.

The target spatial identity is approximately:

- north / north-centre: Windmill Lookout, pond and nature;
- west edge: physical road to Sunbeam Village;
- south-west: open recreation lawn and Rainbow Disc activity;
- south / centre: relocated Picnic Hill;
- upper-east / east-centre: compact Rainbow Run event hub, deliberately separated from other authored districts;
- lower-east / south-east: Crystal Brook as the dominant right-side landmark, using water, rock and a physical crossing rather than a freestanding cave.

Exact coordinates remain subject to H4.2 visual composition work. The district relationship is the approved intent.

## Initial human feedback

The H4 plan must directly address the following observations/requests:

- move Picnic Hill and make the whole picnic story area spatially coherent;
- move the Crystal Brook entrance and make it feel like a real route between regions;
- replace Nova's remaining old-looking presentation with the modern unicorn visual language;
- use the bottom-left/south-west as an active recreation area, with unicorns visibly playing an ultimate-style flying-disc sport and a mini-game the player can join;
- apply the Sunbeam lesson that entrance/exit paths should continue naturally off the map rather than stopping inside the playable canvas;
- identify and remove old visual/prototype residue rather than retaining obsolete objects underneath newer presentation layers;
- carry forward the successful Sunbeam principles around authoritative composition ownership, collision-aware spacing, deliberate districts, physical environmental storytelling and coherent NPC presence.

## Audit findings

Rainbow Meadow currently has the same structural layering problem that Sunbeam had before its H3 rebuild. The final view is assembled by several systems that independently draw, replace or hide Meadow content.

Confirmed current ownership includes:

- `RainbowMeadowScene` draws the base ground, old path network, pond, groves, Rainbow Run hub, an old hand-built Nova, old entrance furniture and simple flowers;
- `WorldTraversalPolishManager` draws another Meadow path network and gateway presentation, while explicitly hiding the old entrance rectangle/text at runtime;
- `VisualTighteningManager` searches for and hides the scene-owned Nova, then creates another `NovaIdentity` sprite;
- `CoreNpcProductionPresentationManager` subsequently hides older Nova presentations again and creates the current production Nova according to authoritative presence;
- `EnvironmentProductionPresentationManager` adds another Meadow-specific background/signature/foreground/ambient layer;
- `MeadowDepthWorldManager` adds Windmill Lookout, Picnic Hill, race evidence and permanent emoji/glow interaction markers;
- `R6RegionGatewayArtManager` adds the visual branch/cave towards Crystal Brook;
- `R5RegionGatewayManager` owns the actual Rainbow Meadow → Crystal Brook transition at a separate hard-coded coordinate.

The current Crystal Brook transition is particularly important. The Meadow-side transition is hard-coded at approximately `(3030, 1750)` in `R5RegionGatewayManager`, while later art is drawn around that coordinate to make it look like a path/cave. It is not represented as a canonical entrance in `RAINBOW_MEADOW_MAP`. The corresponding return position is also separately hard-coded. H4 should remove that split ownership.

Nova similarly carries multiple generations of presentation: the original scene-drawn Nova, the later `NovaIdentity` replacement and the current `CoreNpcProductionArt` presentation. Runtime compatibility code hides older versions. H4 should converge this to one authoritative modern identity rather than adding a fourth layer.

Picnic Hill is also fragmented. Its hill/label, story presentation, Marigold, Nova, Maple movement and interaction semantics are not currently organised around one canonical spatial definition. H4 should move the complete picnic system together rather than changing only the hill graphic.

## Governing implementation principles

H4 inherits the successful Sunbeam rules:

- **one composition owner:** canonical Meadow coordinates and static structure should live in one area-owned layout/composition model rather than several global overlays;
- **remove superseded art at source:** do not keep obsolete Meadow objects alive only to search for and hide them later;
- **physical routes:** main roads, race approaches and region transitions should visibly continue beyond the map boundary where the world continues;
- **district first, decoration second:** large spatial composition is approved before local props are polished;
- **behaviour and presentation stay separate:** story/progression/presence services may remain shared, but they should consume canonical Meadow positions rather than own competing coordinates;
- **interaction-safe spacing:** approach points, click/tap movement, physical collision and visible sprite footprints must agree;
- **environmental affordance over emoji markers:** the object/place itself should communicate what is interesting, with the standard contextual prompt appearing on approach;
- **preserve accepted systems:** do not rewrite working quests, race rules, save semantics or discoveries merely because their presentation is being rebuilt;
- **bounded human review:** every substantive visual/gameplay slice gets a trustworthy preview and explicit human acceptance before the next risky composition step builds on it.

## Approved H4 slices

### H4.1 - Legacy residue and composition ownership cleanup

Establish a clean base before moving content.

- Audit every Rainbow Meadow-specific object created by the base scene and later presentation managers.
- Remove the original scene-owned Nova art and any labels/presentation that exist only for later managers to hide.
- Remove the old solid/legacy west entrance furniture that is already hidden by traversal polish.
- Remove duplicate/obsolete Meadow path presentation so only the intended structural path owner remains.
- Retire Meadow-specific runtime “find the old object and hide it” compatibility code where the old object can now be removed at source.
- Identify Meadow-specific environment/tightening overlays that should become part of the area-owned composition rather than late global injection.
- Preserve behavioural managers where they genuinely own progression, story, population, presence or transition behaviour.
- Introduce/extend a canonical Meadow layout/composition module, following the successful Sunbeam pattern, to become the source of truth for structural coordinates used by later H4 work.

**Acceptance:** Rainbow Meadow can load with the same current gameplay/progression but without stacked generations of base Nova, gateway and path presentation. No H4 work should depend on hiding obsolete Meadow visuals that can instead be deleted.

**Implementation checkpoint, 30 September 2026:** the H4.1 review branch introduces a canonical `RainbowMeadowLayout`, removes the scene-owned Nova/west-gateway/path residue, makes `WorldTraversalPolishManager` the sole current main-path/gateway presentation owner, retires the Meadow-specific Nova replacement/scaling chain, and removes the hidden superseded Meadow→Crystal Brook route/divider at source while preserving the currently accepted Crystal Brook cave/route location until H4.4. Meadow-only `VisualTighteningManager` and `EnvironmentProductionPresentationManager` decoration remains explicitly inventoried for migration during later composition slices rather than being moved prematurely in H4.1.

### H4.2 - Master Meadow layout rebuild

Recompose the full 3400 × 2100 Meadow as a deliberate set of connected outdoor districts.

Approved spatial intent:

- **west:** Sunbeam Village gateway and arrival road;
- **north / north-centre:** Windmill Lookout, pond and nature/exploration features;
- **south-west:** open recreation/sports lawn reserved for H4.9;
- **south / centre:** relocated Picnic Hill;
- **east:** Rainbow Run event/race hub;
- **south-east:** Crystal Brook route and threshold.

Tasks:

- define canonical district/feature coordinates in the Meadow layout;
- rebalance empty versus overcrowded areas without filling every grass space;
- preserve broad open countryside as an intentional part of the area's identity;
- ensure the sports lawn, Picnic Hill and Crystal Brook branch do not compete for the same movement space;
- maintain clear click/tap corridors between major destinations;
- reposition trees/groves/flowers and discoveries where required by the new composition;
- keep enough separation between interaction hotspots that the nearest intended target wins reliably.

**Acceptance:** the Meadow reads as one designed outdoor region before detail passes begin, with obvious room for every existing and planned activity.

**Implementation checkpoint, 30 September 2026:** H4.2 defines six canonical composition districts for Sunbeam arrival, north nature, the future Rainbow Disc lawn, Picnic Hill reserve, Rainbow Run and the Crystal Brook corridor. The south-west recreation lawn and south-east transition corridor have been cleared of competing grove/detail clutter; the flower circle, butterfly parade, petal patch and Sunshower Feather have been moved into the north nature pocket with interaction-safe spacing; scene-owned ground, trees, flowers, pond polish and production decoration now consume the same area-owned layout. The complete picnic event, current Crystal Brook transition, Nova identity and Rainbow Run redesign remain deliberately deferred to H4.5, H4.4, H4.6 and H4.7 respectively.

### H4.3 - Canonical paths, roads and gateway network

- Carry the main Meadow route naturally across the area toward Rainbow Run.
- Make the Rainbow Run approach continue decisively into/through the eastern edge/race threshold rather than terminate as a decorative stub.
- Add narrower organic spurs to Windmill Lookout, pond/nature, Picnic Hill, Rainbow Disc and Crystal Brook.
- Ensure route joins are tucked beneath/into destination structures and thresholds, avoiding visible dead ends or gaps.
- Eliminate doubled path layers and mismatched widths.
- Preserve path readability under atmospheric/time effects and on phone/tablet camera framing.
- Align physical gateway trigger/approach positions with what the path art tells the player.

**Acceptance:** every major route visually explains where the player came from or is going, and the west/east/region routes read as parts of a larger world rather than paths painted onto a closed rectangle.

**Implementation checkpoint, 1 October 2026:** the main Meadow road now begins beyond the west boundary and continues beyond the east boundary through the Rainbow Run threshold. Canonical narrower spurs now serve Windmill Lookout, the north nature/pond pocket, the Picnic Hill reserve, the future Rainbow Disc lawn, the Rainbow Run hub and the current Crystal Brook corridor. The former `R6FinalPlaythroughCleanupManager` Meadow→Crystal Brook road has been retired, leaving the Meadow path network with one presentation owner. H4.4 still owns relocating/canonicalising the functional Crystal Brook transition itself.

### H4.4 - Crystal Brook hero gateway and right-side composition rebuild

Human review rejected the first rocky-cave composition even though the traversal architecture was sound. H4.4 is therefore widened into a deliberate right-side recomposition rather than another cave-polish pass.

**Approved composition intent:**

- Crystal Brook becomes the dominant visual feature of the lower/right-hand Meadow rather than a small portal competing with Rainbow Run furniture.
- Replace the freestanding cave-mouth concept with a **water-led threshold**: a broad brook/pool basin, waterfall at the world edge, mist/crystals/rock shelves, and a readable stepping-stone crossing that carries the player from Meadow bank to the transition point.
- The approach road descends into the basin and should stop reading as a beige road once the water crossing begins.
- Keep increasing rock/crystal language near the basin, but use rocks to frame the route and water rather than scatter isolated obstacles across the grass.
- Make the waterfall/pool/stepping-stone composition itself explain “Crystal Brook”; the player should not need a large floating sign to understand the destination.
- The functional transition, safe return spawn, water-crossing approach and visible threshold must all derive from one canonical Meadow definition.

**Rainbow Run relocation is part of this composition pass, not a later excuse to overlap content:**

- move the current Meadow-side Rainbow Run cluster away from the Crystal Brook hero area as one coordinated group;
- establish an explicit compact race-hub footprint before moving individual props;
- clear/reposition trees, flowers, interaction markers or other Meadow detail that would collide with that new footprint before placing race content;
- relocate the preparation tent, ribbon board, Rainbow Cup board/evidence, course poster, flags, Nova race-hub presence point and race entrance together;
- update all corresponding interaction coordinates and collision bodies to the new canonical hub positions;
- keep a clear main-road and click/tap corridor between the relocated race hub, north nature, Picnic Hill and Crystal Brook route;
- do not consume the future Rainbow Disc lawn or Picnic Hill reserve to solve the race-hub problem;
- H4.7 still owns the **final artistic redesign** of Rainbow Run, but it must inherit this non-overlapping H4.4 footprint rather than move the district again.

**Architecture/traversal tasks retained from the original H4.4:**

- keep Crystal Brook as a canonical `RAINBOW_MEADOW_MAP` entrance with explicit position/approach/direction;
- remove Meadow magic-coordinate ownership from the legacy gateway manager and make it consume canonical Meadow/Brook entrance data;
- preserve location IDs, save checkpoints, arrival facing and current world topology;
- suppress obsolete generic portal presentation where the authored water threshold owns the visual transition;
- make return travel place the player on a safe Meadow bank/approach point, outside the transition trigger;
- ensure collision, visual threshold, stepping-stone route and gateway trigger are mutually consistent.

**Acceptance:** the right-hand side reads as an authored landscape with **Crystal Brook as the hero landmark**. The player follows a descending route to a pool/brook, crosses stepping stones toward/through a waterfall threshold and transitions naturally. Rainbow Run remains clearly available but occupies a separate compact, collision-safe footprint with no props dumped onto nature, picnic, roads or the Crystal Brook basin. There is no portal-in-grass presentation and no duplicated transition-coordinate authority.

**Superseded implementation note, 1 October 2026:** the first H4.4 review branch successfully canonicalised the Meadow↔Crystal Brook traversal and removed the old duplicated portal ownership, but its rocky cave/descent presentation was visually rejected. Those architectural improvements are retained; the cave-led composition is not. The current branch is being rebuilt around the approved waterfall/pool/stepping-stone concept and coordinated Rainbow Run relocation.

### H4.9 - Rainbow Disc recreation lawn and mini-game

Use the south-west/bottom-left as an active recreation area.

#### World presentation

- Clear/compose a broad open grass sports lawn during H4.2 rather than dropping the activity on top of the existing flower/butterfly cluster.
- Add a small group of approximately four or five distinct modern unicorns visibly playing a flying-disc sport.
- Use light field/end-zone markers or pennants that fit the Meadow rather than a formal stadium.
- Add simple ambient pass/catch movement so the group visibly looks like it is playing even when the player does not join.
- Provide one clear interaction such as **Join the game** through a nearby player/captain or field-edge prompt.
- Use the in-world name **Rainbow Disc** or **Meadow Disc Match** rather than depending on a real-world brand name.

#### Repeatable activity

Build a bounded, touch-first mini-game using the established pause/launch/return architecture used by Wobbly Cake, chess and beachcombing.

Initial approved gameplay direction:

- short sessions rather than a full simulation of ultimate rules;
- the player participates in a few linked possessions;
- aim/choose a receiver and control the throw with timing/aim;
- successful catches advance play toward an end zone;
- complete a short chain and make an end-zone catch to score;
- failed/poorly timed throws reset or turn over quickly without punitive downtime;
- touch should support an intuitive drag/aim/release or equivalent gesture;
- keyboard must have a usable directional/throw alternative;
- clear, child-friendly feedback for good throws, catches and scores;
- immediately replayable after completion.

Do **not** introduce a new economy loop, save-schema expansion, deep team AI or complex rules engine by default. Add persistent rewards/progression only if later explicitly approved.

**Acceptance:** the south-west feels alive before interaction, the player can naturally join a short fun disc game, and exiting returns cleanly to the same Meadow context without destabilising world input/UI.

- Add edge trees, bushes, rocks, wildflowers and small landmark details without destroying the intentionally open Meadow character.
- Remove obsolete old visual debris exposed by the new composition.
- Complete a collision/layering pass for every substantial prop, gateway and activity.
- Verify click/tap move-to-interact approaches, dialogue movement lock and post-dialogue control restoration.
- Verify laptop/desktop, tablet and phone framing/readability.
- Check object lifecycle/performance against the WP19H0 contract, especially persistent ambient sports animation and any migrated Meadow presentation managers.
- Run an integrated Sunbeam → Meadow → Picnic/Rainbow Disc/Rainbow Run/Crystal Brook → Meadow traversal and interaction pass.

**Acceptance:** Rainbow Meadow feels like one coherent, populated outdoor region at the maturity level of the approved Sunbeam Village work.
