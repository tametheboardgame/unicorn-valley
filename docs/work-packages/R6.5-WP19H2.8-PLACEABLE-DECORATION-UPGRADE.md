# R6.5-WP19H2.8 – Placeable Decoration Upgrade

**Status:** Complete / human-approved 19 September 2026

**Parent package:** R6.5-WP19H2 – Moonflower Cottage & Home Customisation

**Previous checkpoint:** H2.7 Furniture Colours & Variants, human-approved 19 September 2026.

## Purpose

Upgrade earned cottage decorations so they behave like a coherent home-customisation system rather than prototype slot content. Preserve the proven ownership, compatibility and persistence rules in `HomeDecorationService`, while improving the player-facing edit flow and the in-room presentation of placed items.

## Existing baseline

- `HomeDecorationService` already owns quantity-aware place/remove behaviour and compatibility filtering.
- Cottage decoration slots remain semantic world geometry from `COTTAGE_INTERIOR_MAP`.
- H2.5 already ensures slot markers and slot interactions only exist while Decorate mode is active.
- `CottageDecorateScene` currently presents one slot at a time with Previous/Next cycling and an explicit empty option.
- `CottageInteriorScene` currently renders placed decorations as a generic glow, item icon/emoji and permanent floating item name.
- The current service may move an existing placement of an item automatically when every owned copy is already placed; H2.8 must make this quantity behaviour explicit and safe in the editing UX rather than silently confusing the player.

## Implementation decisions

- Every fresh game owns an unplaced starter collection made specifically for cottage decorating:
  **Moonflower Hoop**, **Star Bunting**, **Meadow Rug** and **Daisy Vase**.
- The starter profiles cover wall, floor, table, shelf and display spots. Moonflower Hoop and Star
  Bunting provide two wall alternatives for Replace; Moonflower Hoop can move between the two wall
  spots, while Daisy Vase can move among table, shelf and display spots.
- Starter ownership is canonical in inventory quantities. The legacy `ownedDecorationIds` mirror is
  maintained only for the current save contract, and no starter is pre-placed.
- Schema-v5 saves migrate to v6 by receiving at least one of each dedicated starter. The grant is
  idempotent, preserves larger quantities and unrelated inventory/progression, and does not change
  placements or grant any existing quest, race, discovery or shop decoration.
- Keep `HomeDecorationService` as the canonical owner of placement, removal, quantity reconciliation and compatibility.
- Do not create a second decoration inventory or cottage-specific ownership store.
- Extend the service only where a clear move/replace operation needs an explicit API or stronger result contract.
- Keep semantic slot categories explicit and testable: wall, floor, table, shelf and display.
- Replace the prototype sequential picker with a child-readable visual selection surface consistent with the established Unicorn Designer / Room Style interaction language where practical.
- Surface owned and placed counts clearly enough that moving the last available copy is understandable.
- Provide explicit flows for **Place**, **Replace**, **Move** and **Remove** rather than relying on hidden cycling semantics.
- Returning from any decoration edit operation must restore H2.5 Decorate mode.
- Replace permanent generic glow/emoji/name presentation in the finished room with item-specific cottage presentation profiles where feasible.
- Placed decorations must not show permanent floating item names during normal play.
- Item labels/descriptions remain available inside the editing UI.
- Presentation upgrades must not alter item ownership, quest/reward semantics or compatibility rules.
- Unknown/retired decoration IDs must remain recoverable and must not crash the cottage.

## Acceptance criteria

- Owned quantity and placed quantity remain correct through place, replace, move and remove operations.
- A decoration cannot end up placed more times than the player owns it.
- Moving the last available copy from another compatible slot is explicit to the player and leaves only one canonical placement.
- Replacing a slot returns the previous item to available inventory semantics without changing total ownership.
- Removing a placed decoration makes that owned copy available for placement elsewhere.
- Wall/floor/table/shelf/display compatibility remains enforced by the canonical catalogue/service.
- The editing UI uses visible choices rather than requiring repeated Previous/Next cycling.
- Empty/removal is a deliberate action and not an ambiguous final carousel entry.
- Placed decorations use item-specific cottage presentation where a profile exists.
- Normal play has no permanent floating names attached to placed decorations.
- Decorate mode markers remain the only edit affordance in the room itself.
- Existing save data and placed decorations continue to load without migration loss.
- Fresh and upgraded saves own the four unplaced starter decorations without losing larger stacks or
  unrelated inventory.
- Leaving/re-entering the cottage and browser reload preserve all final placements exactly.
- Relevant unit and browser contracts cover compatibility, quantity correctness and the principal place/replace/move/remove flows.

## Non-goals

- No new quest reward logic or home-style entitlement framework; H2.10 owns that work.
- No fast travel or portal implementation.
- No change to permanent furniture geometry, collision or H2.7 furniture variants.
- No speculative expansion of decoration slots beyond the canonical H2 room map.

## Human feedback – 19 September 2026

David's live starter-set playtest confirmed that placement, replacement, movement, removal, save persistence
and continued positioning of decorations all behave correctly.

Two H2.8 acceptance blockers remain:

- returning from `CottageDecorateScene` must restore the unicorn at the exact pre-editor position rather
  than recreating the room at the default centre spawn;
- the decoration picker must use the current rounded Unicorn Valley production UI language rather than
  square legacy panels/cards/buttons.

A second live review on 19 September 2026 rejected the first rounded picker as structurally weak rather
than merely under-polished. The redesign pass therefore keeps the proven placement/save core but changes
the player-facing model:

- the editor follows the established Room Style pattern: one large selected-item preview plus a separate
  collection grid, rather than showing the same selected object repeatedly around a mostly empty panel;
- compatible owned decorations are additionally classified into player-facing groups such as Lighting,
  Flowers & Plants, Rugs & Cushions, Trophies & Ribbons, Keepsakes, Ornaments and Hangings; category tabs
  only appear when more than one useful group exists for the selected physical slot;
- blue placement markers are direct touch/mouse controls in Decorate mode, while proximity/controller
  interaction now uses the semantic action label `Decorate here`;
- decoration physicality is explicit: rugs sit below the unicorn with no collision, wall-mounted and
  furniture-supported items do not add competing blockers, and freestanding floor objects use authored
  collision footprints plus Y-aware world depth;
- the generic coloured-oval renderer is replaced with recognisable vector forms for hoops, bunting, rugs,
  vases, cushions, lanterns, ribbons, rosettes, pennants, baskets, jars, mobiles/charms/chimes, bookends and
  keepsakes.

## Human gate

Recheck the redesigned placeable-decoration experience from several physical slots. Confirm the editor
reads as a coherent collection browser, category filtering is useful when several item groups fit a spot,
blue placement markers can be tapped/clicked directly, the contextual action reads `Decorate here`, and
Back / Place / Replace / Move / Remove all preserve the exact room position. In the room, confirm rugs are
under the unicorn, solid freestanding floor objects block movement naturally, supported/wall items do not
create inappropriate collision, and the decoration artwork reads as actual objects rather than coloured
placeholder blobs. The previously verified ownership/save/persistence behaviour must remain unchanged.
