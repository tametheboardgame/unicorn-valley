# R6.5-WP17 - Full Human Playthrough and R7 Readiness Gate

## Objective

Verify through a real human playthrough that the completed R6.5 valley is broad, readable and coherent enough for later daughter-led preference evidence to be meaningful.

This is a hard human gate. It is not an autonomous implementation package.

## Canonical source

`docs/07V-R6.5-VALLEY-COMPLETENESS-BREADTH.md`, especially the R6.5-WP17 section and release-wide breadth minimums.

## Dependency / entry condition

R6.5-WP16 must be merged after its final exact-head technical gate is green. No R7 work may begin merely because WP16 is technically complete.

## Human playthrough questions

During normal free play, assess:

1. Can the player find several different worthwhile things to do without adult direction?
2. Do all major regions feel populated and interactive rather than empty?
3. Are shops genuinely interesting and useful?
4. Is there enough quest/story content that questing feels like a real choice?
5. Are five race experiences enough to judge whether racing is genuinely attractive?
6. Are non-racing repeatable activities discoverable and worth replaying?
7. Does Starlight Beach feel like a complete place rather than a future-content shell?
8. Do supporting residents make the valley feel inhabited?
9. Does returning to older regions reveal changed states, dialogue or new reasons to visit?
10. Do remaining minor defects feel like tidy-up items rather than barriers to free play?

## Release acceptance

The human gate may be released only when all of the following are true:

- no progression blocker remains;
- no major mobile/control/readability blocker remains;
- the player can spend multiple sessions choosing among genuinely different activities and places;
- the user explicitly confirms that the build is broad enough for preference-led observation.

## What to report

The user can report observations naturally while playing. Useful evidence includes:

- anything confusing, unreadable, cramped or difficult to tap;
- places where the player gets stuck or cannot tell where to go;
- regions that still feel empty or repetitive;
- activities/shops/quests/races the player seeks out or ignores;
- bugs, odd animations, duplicate visuals or progression failures;
- spontaneous preferences and repeated choices.

Screenshots are useful for visual defects but are not mandatory for every observation.

## Gate handling

- If the playthrough finds blockers or meaningful R6.5 defects, keep WP17 unreleased and create bounded remediation work under R6.5; fix, revalidate and return to this gate.
- Minor non-blocking tidy-up items may be captured without forcing a redesign, but the user decides whether their combined impact blocks readiness.
- Do not interpret technical CI success as human acceptance.
- Do not infer acceptance from silence or from a single successful session.
- Only an explicit user readiness decision releases this gate.

## Technical invariants while waiting

- The hard application-entry budget remains **520 KiB**.
- Save compatibility and existing progression authorities remain unchanged unless a playthrough defect requires a bounded fix.
- No intentional production deployment is authorised by this package. Production deployment still requires explicit user approval.
- R7-WP7.1 remains blocked.

## Exit

On explicit human acceptance:

1. mark this package `complete`;
2. record the acceptance/playthrough evidence in durable project state;
3. update `ROADMAP.md`, `STATUS.md` and `PROJECT_STATE.json` to release the R7 block;
4. create a fresh bounded R7-WP7.1 package before implementing preference-led expansion.

Until then the correct project state is `waiting_human`.
