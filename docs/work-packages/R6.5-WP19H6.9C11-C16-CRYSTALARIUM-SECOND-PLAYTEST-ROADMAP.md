# H6.9C11–C16 — Crystalarium second-playtest refinement roadmap

**Approved planning direction, 27 October 2026.** The user played the redesigned Crystalarium and accepted its substantial visual improvement while identifying an inadequate tutorial, weak right-hand desktop composition and insufficient sense of evolving crystal growth. This records approval of the *direction and detailed staged plan*, not final UI art, any numeric recharge balance, save migration, economy implementation or release. World draft PR #337 remains unmerged. The current baseline passed 16/16 selected Crystalarium browser tests on CI #40540785493, followed by successful documentation CI #40542204675. H6.9D remains blocked on human playtesting and final qualification.

## Approved principles and decisions

1. **True interactive tutorial.** The highlighted action is the only *gameplay* action permitted at each step: grow one shard, pick up the indicated piece, merge on the indicated matching piece, inspect the *same artwork* in the recipe, then offer. Non-target actions must be visibly disabled. Leave/Save, Skip, Help, accessibility and Sound/Settings always remain available. Do not globally disable Phaser input, trap focus or reset earned progress.
2. **Three physically distinct UI surfaces.** A prominent **Resonance Pattern altar** contains pictured requirements, quantities, harmony and its own Offer action. It must be **separate** from a **Crystal Growing bed** containing producer selection/growth. Harmony, Polish, Wash and reset belong to a quieter **Tools** surface. Use the same hierarchy on phone without hiding the 42-cell board or safe shelf.
3. **Offer-ready celebration.** The offer button and pattern altar gain a gentle glow when the canonical rules say the pattern is ready. Readiness must also be conveyed by text/icon/accessible state, without animation under reduced motion; it must clear after consumption and never falsely glow when harmony is missing.
4. **Board-integrated crystal generators.** Prototype magical rooted crystal veins visibly attached to the board, rather than disconnected source buttons. Low/Bright/Bell unlock by current chapter progress. Advanced growing can have limited optional charges, replenish through play and optionally over elapsed time, and be boosted by **Crystalarium Growth Fluid** earned in the game or, separately, by optional exploration rewards.
5. **Absolutely no hard-wait economy.** **Unlimited free basic tier-zero growth for every unlocked family is permanent.** Charges restrict *accelerated higher-tier production only*. When all charges and Growth Fluid are zero, the player must still be able to complete all 50 patterns. No adverts, purchases, stamina gate, daily rewards, login pressure, scarcity manipulation, mandatory countdown or world-exploration dependency. A passive refill is a bonus, never the only continuation route.
6. **One canonical game.** Keep game identity crystalarium, shared scene/launcher/return, H6.9B short opening-melody world gate, independent later in-cave three-note Crystal Song quest, 50 authored optional resonance patterns, 18 legible crystals, 42 usable cells, adventure save safety and Just Games sandbox isolation. Do not move the main altar outside the cave or change Grotto access.
7. **Early design gates before risky changes.** The generator location, initial charge values, recharge mechanism, fluid stock/use and specific world reward sources are **proposals to prototype and approve**, not frozen numerical requirements. Avoid silently weakening the original no-waiting promise.

## Staged development sequence

### H6.9C11A — Spotlight tutorial storyboard and command contract

**Scope:** Specify exactly six novice moments: (1) Grow button alone, (2) pick up the designated shard, (3) drop it on its designated matching twin, (4) spotlight the actual resulting crystal and identical pictured recipe goal, (5) highlight and activate Offer once the rules report ready, (6) celebrate and release all controls. The player can only *perform* the currently requested gameplay action, not merely receive a text hint. Clearly distinguish disabled controls from locked future mechanics.

**Architecture:** Build the action allowlist in the canonical Crystalarium controller, upstream of pure-rule mutations and persistence; connect Phaser and native portrait UI to the same guide phase/target. Do not use a blocking transparent DOM overlay over the board or scene-wide input disable. Stop drags safely on cancellation, pointer loss, rotation or exit. Skip, Leave and Save, Help/Replay, keyboard/screen-reader navigation and sound settings are always operable. Help derives from actual saved state, never fabricates crystals.

**Acceptance:** annotated 1280×720, 390×844 and 320×700 storyboard including spotlit action, disabled targets, focus and safe shelf. **Gate:** user approval of first-play visual sequencing before implementation.

### H6.9C11B — Locked, replayable and contextual learning implementation

**Scope:** Complete C11A with visual pointer cues, target pulse or non-animated outline, short low-reading text and actual accepted-action progression. First-play Grow -> real drag/merge -> illustrated goal -> glowing Offer; then remove restrictions. Later use short, skippable micro-lessons when shelf, Wash, new families, advanced producers, Harmony, Polish and fluid first become relevant. Do not make older saves replay the opening tutorial by force.

**Tests:** state-machine/authorisation table including reject/no mutation, skip at every step, wrong drag/drop, one accepted action advancing exactly one stage, assistance muted and reduced-motion, touch/mouse/keyboard, scene teardown, storage failure, resume, existing progressed campaign, Just Games versus adventure guide keys. Preserve original 4×3 opening-melody behaviour. **Gate:** player unfamiliar with rules can complete the first pattern without adult explanation; Skip/Leave always recover control.

### H6.9C12A — Resonance Pattern altar / Growing bed / Tools visual designs

**Scope:** Redesign desktop right-hand column and responsive phone/tablet composition into **three separate** visually related magical pieces. Top Pattern altar: chapter, progress, exact board-identical illustrated goal pieces and quantities, harmony demand, prominently separated Offer. Middle Growing bed: Low/Bright/Bell physical producer icons, active/inactive/locked states, obvious grow affordance. Bottom Tools: Harmony, Polish, free Brook Wash and confirmed Fresh Board. Tools are subordinate, not a third equally loud primary action.

**Offer state:** once canOfferPattern(state) is true, give the pattern card and Offer button a restrained magical glow and optional chime. Provide ready icon/word and ARIA announcement; no perpetual pulse under reduced motion and no reliance on sound/colour. When requirements become unsatisfied, remove the glow immediately. Still legible after chapter 50.

**Responsive contract:** no overlapping panel, fixed shelf or hidden board row at 320px; no tiny touch actions; 390px portrait, tablet, desktop and landscape composition. Existing artwork, design system, focus, accessibility and lazy-loading rules apply. **Gate:** actual concept screenshots reviewed/approved before broad UI replacement.

### H6.9C12B — Unified interface implementation and interaction tests

**Scope:** Implement C12A in existing CrystalariumFullMode and CrystalariumPortraitView, driven by the same pure rules and presentation state. No new mini-game ID, separate engine, competing UI token system or duplicated source/offer controls. Keep keyboard and tap parity, Sound/Exit, safe shelf and accessible feedback.

**Tests:** ready/not-ready transitions, absent harmony, consumed goal, no double offer, identical recipe/board art, desktop pointer/phone touch, scene return, 320px first-row visibility, narrow sideways scroll, reduced-motion, sound-off and performance. **Gate:** human accepts right panel, phone composition and glowing Offer before producer economy changes.

### H6.9C13A — Rooted generator proof of concept: location and unlock

**Scope:** Prototype three distinct crystal growth structures physically attached to the board, matching existing unlock thresholds **Low 0 / Bright 4 / Bell 7** completed patterns. **Preferred baseline:** a fixed nursery strip or edge sockets inside the board's physical frame but **outside the 42 merge cells**, preserving the real 7×6 free-grid capacity and preventing occupied producers being accidentally merged, washed or lost. Compare with an actual-cell alternative using board-space recovery and 320px usability evidence, not opinion alone.

Allow tap/keyboard to grow, and assess dragging from a rooted source onto a free cell if intuitive. Convey locked, active, spent *advanced* charges and refill states visually. Low-reading feedback, target affordance, scroll/drag safety and art readable at 48px are mandatory. Prototype may be a presentation-only branch/commit without persisted counters.

**Gate:** human chooses location, board capacity, producer artwork and touch model. No charge-state/schema changes before this choice.

### H6.9C13B — Optional advanced producers, charges, refresh and migration

**Baseline rule:** every unlocked family can always produce a free tier-zero shard. Keep existing optional higher-tier accelerations: tier-one after 20 offers, tier-two after 40. An advanced producer has a finite *bonus* charge meter, not a general energy bar.

**Provisional tuning experiments, NOT frozen decisions:** 4 advanced charges per producer; restore 1 charge per completed pattern up to cap; optionally 1 passive charge every 3–5 minutes up to cap, including offline. Compare to a no-real-time, achievement-only recharge option. Advanced charges are consumed only on **successful** advanced placement, never on wrong tap, missing space, cancelled drag, tutorial-rejected action, failed save or invalid family. Passive clock arithmetic must be bounded and safe against backwards/system clock changes. If optional timing damages child experience, remove it without removing unlimited base production.

**Persistence:** extend only canonical resonance-patterns rules/state with producer counters and optional timestamp if needed; version and migrate existing full-state v1 into the new shape without discarding saved cells, chapters, shelf, harmonies or recipe progress. Preserve adventure v10 and historical v9 migrations, and Just Games sandbox v1 independently; make any root-schema upgrade a separately reviewed Amber gate. Fail closed on malformed/future saves, and never claim a success when save fails.

**Tests:** pure-rule action acceptance/charge consumption, fake-clock tests, reload/offline cap, cancellation, full-board/empty-shelf, migration, corrupt data, source isolation, advanced at zero, zero-fluid solver through all 50 patterns, post-50 free play. **Gate:** approve actual numbers and refill policy only after novice/midgame/late-game pacing comparisons.

### H6.9C14A — Growth Fluid as a helpful, nonessential Crystalarium resource

**Provisional first balance:** one bottle replenishes **up to two** advanced charges of a chosen rooted producer (up to its cap); a full producer must not waste a bottle, and the effect is explained before confirming. Suggested *initial tuning only*: earn one vial per three successful offered patterns, wallet cap 12, supplemented by finite authored milestones. Review the distribution against endgame demand. Vials never enable basic growth; they provide convenience/upgrades only.

Growth Fluid and producer state are **separate for adventure and Just Games**, with independent ways to earn usable resources. Just Games never grants adventure currency/quests, and adventure resources never need to be brought over to play independently. Saving, fresh-board reset, overflow, replay, corruption, reward duplication and no-op spending all require explicit tests. No microtransactions, daily claims, ads, external analytics, random loot crates or mandatory waiting.

**Gate:** user approves the actual name, artwork, basic reward cadence, refill generosity and consumption clarity.

### H6.9C14B — Optional wider-Unicorn-Valley Growth Fluid rewards

**Direction:** exploration, completed resident help or existing Brook/Grotto activities may award *adventure-only* Growth Fluid or one-off generator upgrades, giving Crystalarium a meaningful connection to the wider world. This is a separate World-integration gate, **not** a prerequisite for ordinary Crystalarium completion.

Before coding, nominate exact authored reward sources and their owners. Do not invent new quests or retroactively award fluid from existing flags without explicit approval. Any accepted grant must use canonical world/save services with idempotent reward identity; prevent duplicates on scene return, restarts or replay. Never tie Grotto door unlock or Crystal Song rewards to fluid. Additional central inventory or save-schema edits, if needed, require their own approval and downstream migration review.

**Tests:** world-only authorisation and exact trigger, duplicate prevention, old save handling, Grotto quest and world-Just Games isolation, full wallet, no required external visit. **Gate:** explicit sign-off for each reward source and any expanded architecture.

### H6.9C15 — Long-session balancing, recovery and real-player evaluation

Playtest first five minutes, 15 minutes and a longer ~45-minute session, including 320/390px portrait and the intended tablet. Sample all five chapters, Harmony/Polish, 18 pieces, producer unlocks, zero fluid, depleted advanced generators, full board, shelf and free Brook Wash. Prove the full fifty-pattern campaign remains solvable with **zero advanced charges and zero fluid**, with no waiting or mandatory world rewards.

**Undo decision:** evaluate a one-step undo limited to an accepted board move/merge versus the present shelf/free Brook Wash. Never silently reverse consumed offered patterns, quests, world grants or paid charges. If approved, design an atomic, non-exploitable action snapshot and test persistence; otherwise document no Undo with human acceptance. Reproduce the originally reported unclear post-crystal action bug on a deterministic interaction path before claiming it resolved.

**Gate:** the user accepts the gameplay and art, genuinely intuitive tutorial and Offer feedback, upgrade utility, no-wait economy, sound-off and world/Just Games behaviour on a **SHA-verified deployed Cloudflare preview**. If advanced charges feel punitive, retain approved C11/C12 UI and use infinite growing instead.

Each mechanical gate needs Vitest pure-rule tests, physical pointer/touch browser contracts and real screenshots. The all-50-pattern legal-action solver and old-to-new save migration are mandatory, not optional.

Do not launch H6.10, restart piecemeal races or redesign H7 Grotto interior as a side effect.
- **Current stage:** planning only. C11–C16 have **not** been implemented or visually accepted by committing this document. Next implementation work is C11A and C12A.
