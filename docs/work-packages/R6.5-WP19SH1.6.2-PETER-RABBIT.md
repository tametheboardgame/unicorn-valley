# R6.5-WP19SH1.6.2 - The Tale of Peter Rabbit

## Purpose

Create a coherent original Modern Illustrations set for the five-chapter Story House retelling of
The Tale of Peter Rabbit while preserving the complete historic Full Classic experience.

The modern set must use the established Story House cinematic/painterly visual language and original
compositions. It must not imitate Beatrix Potter's illustration style or reconstruct historic
Potter compositions.

## Frozen source baseline

- Story House Edition: five chapters with 13 stable content blocks.
- Existing Story House historic illustration baseline: four Potter images.
- Full Classic Text: ten reader pages.
- Full Classic historic illustration baseline: all 26 Beatrix Potter narrative illustrations.
- All 26 Full Classic images, their order, paths and provenance are frozen for this package.
- Alice SH1.6.1 is the architecture reference for independent Classic / Modern illustration sets.
- Reader runtime, save architecture and general Story House UI are out of scope unless a genuine
  shared regression is found.

## Checkpoints

### SH1.6.2A - Audit + visual bible - ACTIVE

Complete before bulk story generation:

- audit all five Story House chapters and stable block IDs;
- define the bounded modern scene inventory;
- write and commit the title visual bible;
- generate and human-review four continuity anchors:
  1. Peter Rabbit;
  2. Mrs Rabbit with Flopsy, Mopsy and Cotton-tail;
  3. Mr McGregor;
  4. Mr McGregor's garden environment.

The audit and scene inventory are recorded in
`docs/story-house/visual-bibles/the-tale-of-peter-rabbit.md`.

Human approval of the four anchor designs is required before SH1.6.2B begins.

### SH1.6.2B - Chapters 1-2

Generate and review the opening family/warning and garden-entry sequence using only the approved
anchor designs.

### SH1.6.2C - Chapters 3-5

Generate and review the pursuit, lost/escape and home-again sequence, preserving Peter's scale,
garden geography, jacket continuity and family/home continuity.

### SH1.6.2D - Integration + modern cover + closeout

- materialise approved images through the normal generated-asset pipeline;
- wire the modern set to stable Story House block IDs;
- expose Classic / Modern illustration selection for the Story House edition;
- leave Full Classic Text and all 26 historic images unchanged;
- reuse an approved modern image as the 3:4 cover only if the crop is strong, otherwise generate a
  dedicated modern cover;
- run focused Story House validation and preview review;
- merge only after human title approval.

## Scope boundaries

In scope:

- Peter Rabbit title content under `public/stories/the-tale-of-peter-rabbit/**`;
- generated-asset manifests for this title;
- title-specific Story House documentation;
- catalogue output when final integration requires it.

Out of scope:

- rewriting the Story House retelling;
- changing the Full Classic text or any of its 26 historic images;
- reader architecture changes;
- world/exploration changes;
- unrelated Story House titles;
- Alice fixes unless a genuine shared regression is discovered.
