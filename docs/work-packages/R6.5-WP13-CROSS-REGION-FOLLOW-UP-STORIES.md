# R6.5-WP13 - Quest Pack B: Cross-Region and Follow-up Stories

## Goal

Connect the broadened valley into longer-lived relationships and return visits, while closing any remaining gap to the release-wide minimum of at least six new substantive R6.5 story threads.

## Canonical source

This bounded package implements the WP13 section of `docs/07V-R6.5-VALLEY-COMPLETENESS-BREADTH.md`. That release contract remains authoritative if wording here is ambiguous.

## Delivery contract

- Audit the current R6.5 story-thread matrix before adding content. Count substantive threads separately from explicitly lightweight microstories/microquests.
- Add only the additional substantive threads required so the reconciled R6.5 total reaches at least six new substantive threads. WP11's Juniper and Maple microstories, and any other explicitly lightweight microquests, do not count toward that six-thread minimum.
- Include at least one Starlight Beach follow-up.
- Include at least one core-character friendship follow-up.
- Include at least one supporting-resident chain.
- Include at least one story that deliberately revisits an older region after newer content has unlocked.
- Add at least one further persistent visual or dialogue state change.

- Prefer stories that combine different verbs, characters and places rather than repeated collection errands.

## Story-thread audit

The evidence-based WP5-WP12 audit found that the release-wide substantive-story minimum was already exceeded before WP13:

- WP5: Pip and the Hollow Tree Whispers.
- WP6: Breeze and the View From Up There.
- WP7: Echo and the Crystal Song.
- WP8: Fern: Fireflies Know the Way.
- WP10: Coral and the Shells With Stories; Skipper and Follow the Wind.
- WP11: Willow and the Moonflowers After Dark; Nova, Clover and a Race With No Finish Line; Pebble and the Stone That Does Not Match.

That is nine substantive R6.5 story threads before WP13. WP11's Juniper's Butterfly Count and Maple's Perfect Picnic Spot remain explicitly lightweight microstories and are not included in that count.

Because the required minimum is already satisfied, WP13 deliberately adds one coherent cross-region follow-up rather than padding the release with unnecessary quests.

## Implemented story

`Lumi, Coral and the Light That Found the Sea` is the bounded WP13 follow-up.

- Unlocks only after Coral's Shells With Stories quest is complete, the Starwell is revealed and Lumi's introductory friendship beat is complete.
- Begins with an unusual green shell-glimmer at Moonlit Point on Starlight Beach.
- Coral identifies it as an unfamiliar light and sends the player back inland.
- The player deliberately revisits Whispering Woods, asks core character Lumi about the light and compares it with the older Starwell.
- The Starwell reflects the same sea-pattern and green glow, sending the player back to Coral.
- Completion leaves a permanent Shore and Starwell Lantern at Shell Cove.
- Coral acts as a supporting-resident multi-step story participant; Lumi receives a genuine core-character friendship follow-up.
- Five Wonderbook discoveries/memories record the story beats.
- Completion awards a placeable Shore and Starwell Lantern, friendship for Lumi and Coral, and the standard substantive-story Shimmer reward.
- The implementation reuses the existing quest, discovery, relationship, inventory, economy, save and Cottage-decoration authorities and introduces no save-schema change.
- The WP13 world manager is dynamically imported so the hard 520 KiB application-entry budget remains binding.
