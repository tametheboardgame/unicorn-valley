# R6.5-WP10 - Starlight Beach Content Pack

## Metadata

- ID: `R6.5-WP10`
- Autonomy: Green implementation with Amber visual/content acceptance
- Dependencies: `R6.5-WP9`, `R6.5-WP3`
- Delivery: PR required
- Temporary base: `r6.5-wp9-starlight-beach-core` until the R6.5 dependency train reaches `main`

## Goal

Turn Starlight Beach from a complete region shell into a content-bearing destination with stories, secrets, rewards and clear reasons to return.

## Scope

- Coral-led shell story using the finite Starlight Shell family;
- Skipper-led wind-marker story that prepares, but does not implement, the later Beach race;
- at least two new interaction-led Beach secrets;
- conditional Coral and Skipper dialogue across active/completed story states;
- persistent post-story Beach props/state;
- a touch-safe beachcombing station foundation with first-use discovery and replay flavour;
- Shimmer rewards for both substantive Beach stories;
- Wonderbook integration through registered characters, discoveries and reward items;
- one Beach-themed Twinkle & Thread stock item unlocked by Beach progression;
- a visible race-planning board/hook for R6.5-WP12.

## Non-goals

- no full Beach race course;
- no Rainbow Cup implementation;
- no grindable currency loop;
- no daily timer or FOMO mechanic;
- no full R6.5-WP14 activity expansion beyond the bounded beachcombing foundation;
- no production deployment.

## Story contract

### Coral - Shells With Stories

The player talks to Coral, finds the three finite Starlight Shells already authored by WP9, then arranges them at Shell Cove to reveal a new Shell Story Circle discovery. The shells remain owned. Completion leaves a visible display at Shell Cove, unlocks the beachcombing station, grants a memory-style reward and unlocks a Beach cosmetic in Twinkle & Thread.

### Skipper - Follow the Wind

The player talks to Skipper, investigates a dune wind marker, follows the clue to Moonlit Point, then returns to Skipper. Completion leaves a shoreline-course planning board at Star Dunes and a persistent `beach-race-route-ready` hook for WP12 without starting a race.

## Acceptance

### Technical

- Coral and Skipper are registered characters as well as ambient residents;
- talking to either resident can start and advance their linked standard quest flow;
- Coral's story cannot complete until all three Starlight Shells are owned;
- Skipper's two secret discoveries advance in a safe authored order;
- both quests persist completion, friendship and economy rewards;
- at least two new Beach secrets are registered and Wonderbook-visible;
- beachcombing foundation is touch-safe, replayable and non-grindable;
- completed stories visibly change the Beach on revisit;
- Twinkle & Thread exposes the Beach cosmetic only after Coral's story is complete;
- the Beach race hook contains no race implementation;
- format, lint, typecheck, unit, build, performance, static smoke and browser CI remain green.

### Human/visual

- the first Beach visit presents several credible optional directions rather than one forced activity;
- Coral and Skipper read as locals with changing concerns rather than static exposition NPCs;
- completed story props are visible enough to make a return visit feel changed;
- new prompts and props remain clear on target phone/tablet layouts.

## Stop conditions

Stop and surface a new Amber boundary if implementation requires:

- changing the protected save schema;
- increasing the approved R6.5 performance envelope;
- implementing the Beach race early;
- introducing a new activity architecture rather than reusing existing world interaction/input patterns.
