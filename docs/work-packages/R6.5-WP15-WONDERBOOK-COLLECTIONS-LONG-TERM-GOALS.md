# R6.5-WP15 - Wonderbook, Collections and Long-Term Goals

## Goal

Make the Wonderbook reflect the broadened valley and give exploration, friendship, activities and racing visible continuity without adding pressure, streaks or a second progression authority.

## Canonical source

This package implements the WP15 section of `docs/07V-R6.5-VALLEY-COMPLETENESS-BREADTH.md` and the collection/revisit intent in `docs/07W-R6.5-CONTENT-BLUEPRINT.md`.

## Starting baseline

Accepted `main`: `5d246ae012ef351e9dd356befc3a9608222041e6` (R6.5-WP14 merge).

The current audit shows useful foundations but a narrow presentation:

- `WonderbookScene` currently presents only discovery stickers plus an All/Secrets filter;
- `WonderbookCharacterModel` already derives known characters and friendship tiers but is not surfaced in the Wonderbook scene;
- save schema v2 already stores discoveries, memories, relationship progress, changed world objects, race records/ribbons and generic mini-game records;
- WP12 already records the five regular Rainbow Cup courses through `activities.racesById` and awards ribbons through those race records;
- WP14 already records Bakery and Beachcombing finite collection progress through `activities.miniGameRecords`, alongside Firefly Lantern records;
- therefore WP15 should aggregate existing saved facts and must not introduce a parallel completion ledger or save migration unless a genuine blocker is proven.

## Delivery contract

### 1. Clear child-readable Wonderbook sections

Expand the book from the current discovery-only view into a small set of obvious sections covering the major existing progression families:

- discoveries and secrets;
- friends;
- places/regions and their local collection progress;
- races/ribbons;
- gentle long-term goals.

Section navigation must remain touch-first, readable and reversible. Existing keyboard/controller navigation and close behaviour must remain intact.

### 2. Discoveries, secrets and memories

- preserve every registered discovery already represented by the current Wonderbook;
- keep undiscovered entries mysterious with child-readable promises rather than spoiler text;
- retain secret handling so undiscovered secrets are not presented as a punitive checklist;
- surface useful memory/world-change summaries where the existing save already proves that the valley changed;
- include the R6.5 Starlight Beach discoveries and the WP13 Shore/Starwell follow-up naturally in the broader book.

### 3. Friends

Use the existing character registry and relationship authority to provide a real friendship section:

- known characters show their name, role and friendship tier;
- unknown characters appear as friendly silhouettes/promises rather than named spoilers;
- the section should celebrate meeting and deepening friendships without suggesting the player has failed when someone remains unknown.

### 4. Places and region collections

Provide coherent region pages/cards for the major playable valley areas:

- Moonflower Glade / Cottage;
- Sunbeam Village;
- Rainbow Meadow / Rainbow Run;
- Crystal Brook / Crystal Grotto;
- Whispering Woods;
- Starlight Beach.

Each region should show useful existing progress derived from registered discoveries, memories, world changes, stories and/or activities. The Beach must have first-class representation rather than being folded into a generic discovery list.

Where a region has a finite authored collection, show positive progress such as `2 of 3 found`. Where it does not, use discoveries/memories or a qualitative explored/not-yet-explored state instead of inventing grind targets.

### 5. Races and ribbons

Add a race section based on `activities.racesById`:

- track the five regular Rainbow Cup courses individually;
- show whether each has been finished;
- show earned ribbon count/recognition without requiring first place;
- show overall Rainbow Cup participation progress;
- keep racing framed as one optional part of valley life, not the dominant completion metric.

### 6. Gentle long-term goals

Provide at least three distinct long-term tracks derived entirely from existing save state. Initial target set:

1. **Valley Explorer** - visit/discover something meaningful in each major region.
2. **Friendship Garden** - meet and build friendships with valley residents.
3. **Ribbon Journey** - finish the five regular race courses, regardless of placing.
4. **Curiosity Cabinet** - discover authored secrets/memories/collections across different activities and regions.

Rules:

- no daily/weekly timers;
- no streaks;
- no FOMO;
- no punitive red incompletion state;
- no reward is required for core progression;
- progress wording should emphasise what the player has done and suggest something interesting still available.

### 7. Reveal feedback and presentation

- newly discovered/revealed cards should retain or improve the existing celebratory sticker/reveal motion;
- section changes should feel book-like but remain quick;
- touch targets and text must remain readable at supported tablet/mobile layouts;
- do not add heavy assets or weaken the hard 520 KiB application-entry budget.

## Acceptance

1. Opening the Wonderbook now provides useful views for discoveries/secrets, friends, places/region collections, races/ribbons and long-term goals.
2. All major R6.5 content families have a coherent Wonderbook representation where appropriate, including Starlight Beach, WP12 race/Cup content, WP13 follow-up memories/world change and WP14 repeatable activity collections.
3. Unknown content is represented with silhouettes/promises or qualitative hints rather than spoiler-heavy checklists.
4. At least three distinct long-term goal tracks are visible and entirely derived from existing persisted state.
5. No save-schema migration or second progression authority is introduced unless an unavoidable blocker is documented.
6. Existing Wonderbook discovery pages, secret behaviour, close/return behaviour and input support remain intact.
7. The experience contains no streaks, real-world timers, FOMO or punitive completion pressure.
8. Formatting, lint, type-check, unit tests, production build, static smoke, browser compatibility and full Chromium playtest pass with the 520 KiB application-entry budget unchanged.

## Implementation order

1. Add a pure Wonderbook progress/collection model that derives friends, regions, races and long-term goals from existing save/registry state.
2. Add focused unit coverage for unknown/revealed state, regional collections, race participation and goal progress.
3. Expand `WonderbookScene` into sectioned presentation while preserving current discovery/secret behaviour.
4. Add child-readable region collection definitions for the six major areas, including Starlight Beach and WP14 finite collections.
5. Add race/ribbon cards for the five regular Rainbow Cup courses.
6. Add long-term goal cards derived from existing state only.
7. Add focused browser acceptance for section navigation, hidden/revealed presentation and persisted progress.
8. Run the full technical gate and merge before beginning WP16.

## Guardrails

- No production deployment without explicit user approval.
- Never weaken or increase the 520 KiB application-entry budget.
- Do not introduce a new save-schema field simply to store Wonderbook completion percentages.
- Do not begin WP16 until WP15 is fully validated and merged.
- R7 remains blocked until R6.5-WP17 human readiness is explicitly released.
