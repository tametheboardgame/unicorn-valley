# R6.5-WP15 - Wonderbook, Collections and Long-Term Goals

## Goal

Make the Wonderbook reflect the broadened valley and give exploration, friendship, activities and racing visible continuity without adding pressure, streaks or a second progression authority.

## Canonical source

This package implements the WP15 section of `docs/07V-R6.5-VALLEY-COMPLETENESS-BREADTH.md` and the collection/revisit intent in `docs/07W-R6.5-CONTENT-BLUEPRINT.md`.

## Starting baseline

Accepted `main`: `5d246ae012ef351e9dd356befc3a9608222041e6` (R6.5-WP14 merge).

The foundation audit showed:

- `WonderbookScene` originally presented only discovery stickers plus an All/Secrets filter;
- `WonderbookCharacterModel` already derived known characters and friendship tiers but was not surfaced in the Wonderbook scene;
- save schema v2 already stored discoveries, memories, relationship progress, changed world objects, race records/ribbons and generic mini-game records;
- WP12 already recorded the five regular Rainbow Cup courses through `activities.racesById` and awarded ribbons through those race records;
- WP14 already recorded Bakery and Beachcombing finite collection progress through `activities.miniGameRecords`, alongside Firefly Lantern records;
- WP15 therefore aggregated existing saved facts without a parallel completion ledger or save migration.

## Delivered

### 1. Clear child-readable Wonderbook sections

The Wonderbook now provides five primary sections:

- discoveries and secrets;
- friends;
- places/regions and local collection progress;
- races/ribbons;
- gentle long-term goals.

The existing discovery All/Secrets behaviour and book close/return/input contract remain intact.

### 2. Discoveries, secrets and memories

- registered discovery entries remain represented;
- undiscovered content remains mystery-led rather than spoiler-heavy;
- secret filtering is preserved;
- R6.5 Starlight Beach and WP13 Shore/Starwell follow-up discoveries feed naturally into the broader book;
- reveal motion remains celebratory rather than punitive.

### 3. Friends

The existing character registry and relationship authority now provide the friendship section:

- known characters show their name, role and friendship tier;
- unknown characters appear as friendly `Someone to meet...` promises rather than named spoilers;
- no second relationship or completion state was added.

### 4. Places and region collections

The book now has coherent region cards for:

- Moonflower Glade / Cottage;
- Sunbeam Village;
- Rainbow Meadow / Rainbow Run;
- Crystal Brook / Crystal Grotto;
- Whispering Woods;
- Starlight Beach.

Region progress derives from existing discoveries and activity state. Finite WP14 Bakery and Beachcombing collections show authored progress, while Firefly Lantern recognition is derived from its existing activity records.

### 5. Races and ribbons

The race section derives from `activities.racesById` and tracks the five regular Rainbow Cup courses individually. It recognises finishes and ribbons without requiring first place.

### 6. Gentle long-term goals

Four derived long-term tracks are now visible:

1. **Valley Explorer** - remember something meaningful in every major region.
2. **Friendship Garden** - meet valley residents through normal play.
3. **Ribbon Journey** - finish the five regular race courses, regardless of placing.
4. **Curiosity Cabinet** - notice a broad set of authored discoveries/memories.

These remain timer-free, streak-free, FOMO-free and non-punitive.
