## Scope and acceptance

Implement exactly one `crystalarium` mini-game family, one lazy manifest-backed scene and one pure matching engine shared by a world-only `opening-melody` and an introductory Just Games `resonance-patterns` practice mode. The first round is a small fixed 4×3 board with six shards, three requested note families (low, bright, bell), no timer, non-destructive mistakes and reset/exit. Show visible and optional audible pitches, distinct shape/colour labels, pointer/touch/drag and keyboard controls, safe return.

After Echo starts the existing Crystal Song quest, the Grotto remains sealed until a **verified winning world session** unlocks the intro-resonance discovery. Keep quest step indices 3–10 intact and preserve completed songs, chimes, previously opened entrances and old progressed saves. Win callbacks must be idempotent, and Just Games / null / wrong-source / wrong-variant / wrong-location sessions must never mutate adventure saves. After first completion, ordinary `Go inside` resumes without another forced gate; the interior low/bright/bell song remains a separate quest.

H6.9B's initial `resonance-patterns` practice deliberately reuses the small introductory board. H6.9C expands that very same scene to 7×6 tiers/harmonies/recycling, and H6.9D finishes polish and integrated acceptance. Do **not** introduce a duplicate game or defer first Just Games registration to H6.9D.
