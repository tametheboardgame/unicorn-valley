# R6.5-WP16 Evidence Audit

This audit is the evidence-first reconciliation required by `R6.5-WP16`. It distinguishes defects that were already fixed and human-verified during R6 from genuine gaps introduced by later R6.5 content.

## Classification key

- **Already resolved** - current source/tests and the R6 playthrough closure record show the issue was fixed and accepted; retain regression coverage rather than reworking it.
- **Needs fix** - current R6.5 implementation has a demonstrable residual gap.
- **Covered / no change** - current implementation and tests already satisfy the WP16 requirement.
- **Pending regional review** - content exists, but final density/dead-zone evidence is still being reconciled.

## Region density and navigation

The canonical density contract remains:

- at least 8 meaningful interaction points per major outdoor region unless an explicit accepted exception is documented;
- at least 3 optional environmental toys/observations;
- at least 2 secrets/discoveries/collection opportunities;
- at least 1 material story use;
- at least 1 revisit reason;
- at least 1 persistent reaction;
- at least 1 strong orientation landmark;
- no long dead stretch without a meaningful reason;
- no filler added solely to satisfy counts.

Current authored depth/playtest coverage exists for Glade/Cottage, Rainbow Meadow/Run, Crystal Brook/Grotto, Whispering Woods and Starlight Beach, with Village content covered across the Village-depth/interior/shop packages. The final WP16 pass will reconcile each region against the complete post-WP15 interaction set and document counts or any explicit exception.

Classification: **Pending regional review**.
