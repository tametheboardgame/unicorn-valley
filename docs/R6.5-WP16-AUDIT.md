## Economy, quest and race balance

No rebalance is justified by the completed earning/price evidence.

- `R4_SHOP_STOCK` uses the intended 2 / 4 / 6 Shimmer early / standard / aspirational price bands.
- `EconomyRewardPolicy.ts` contains 18 substantive quest rewards at 2 Shimmer each, discovery milestones worth a further 6 Shimmer, and first-completion rewards for Firefly Lantern, Maple Baking and Coral Beachcombing worth 2 Shimmer each.
- Normal varied play therefore exposes ample earning capacity before repeat farming is considered; a child does not need to grind one activity to afford desirable stock.
- `ShopProgression.ts` deliberately mixes unlock conditions: always-available starter items, quest count, discovery count, first race completion, combined quest/race progress, and Coral story state.
- `EconomyRewardLoop.test.ts` proves idempotent reward reconciliation/reload safety and early stock attainability; `ShopPurchaseTapGuard` protects duplicate taps.
- `R65RaceExpansion.test.ts` asserts five regular race courses and distinct rhythms, and proves the Rainbow Cup can complete from five last-place finishes with one-time reward delivery. First place is not a progression gate.
- Existing R6/R6.18 touch and assistance coverage remains authoritative for race accessibility.

Classification: **Covered / no change**. No price, quest-reward or race-reward correction is required.

Ordinary exits/global UI do not count, and filler must not be added merely to reach a number.
