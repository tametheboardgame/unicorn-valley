# R6.5-WP19D feedback

## Scope

WP19D consolidates explicit world interactions into the shared semantic target registry and `WorldInteractionCoordinator`, while preserving automatic region crossings and existing authored action side effects.

## Browser remediation evidence

- World interaction keyboard edges are captured once by the shared coordinator so legacy scene movement adapters cannot consume `E`, Enter or Space before contextual actions run.
- Mobile contextual hints use semantic target labels instead of retired instructional copy.
- Crystal Cascade keeps its enlarged 360 × 360 touch affordance through target presentation geometry without expanding interaction range or restoring a second activation route.
- Cottage tactile markers remain present for world/layout diagnostics but no longer own interaction input.
- Legacy visual objects such as the odd stone remain non-interactive; coordinator-owned direct zones provide their actionable hit areas when the player is eligible.
- Regression coverage asserts that enlarged direct-tap geometry cannot make an out-of-range target selectable.

## David review - 2026-09-11

The interaction foundation was provisionally successful, but the first candidate was not accepted for merge. David's live review found the following positive behaviour:

- the contextual Interact action is working well;
- roaming NPCs stop and turn towards the player during engagement;
- interactions cannot be triggered from an unreasonable distance;
- general NPC behaviour is materially improved.

The review then required bounded remediation for desktop control ownership, NPC physical collision, lower-screen conversation ownership, discovery notification placement, Starlight Beach HUD continuity and clean preparation for WP19E's in-world conversation system.

## Corrected candidate - 2026-09-11

Runtime candidate: `8c4c995c654dda2168d82610eeb07b5d6ff8208f`

Immutable preview: `https://a13f68a2.unicorn-valley.pages.dev`

The remediation now implements the review contract:

1. Desktop control ownership
   - Touch D-pad and Gallop controls are no longer enabled merely because a desktop/laptop reports touch capability.
   - Primary coarse/touch devices retain touch movement controls.
   - Contextual Talk/Interact/Enter/Start remains independent from touch movement visibility.

2. NPC physical presence
   - Visible Talk targets contribute physical personal space so player and visible unicorn residents cannot pass directly through one another.
   - Tween-driven supporting residents pause before walking through the player and resume once clear.
   - Separation preserves held player velocity and slides around residents instead of turning NPCs into hard path-blocking walls.
   - Engaged resident stop/facing behaviour remains authoritative.

3. Lower-screen conversation ownership
   - Supporting-resident speech uses the protected lower-screen presentation area.
   - Contextual target/action/hint presentation is suppressed while conversation is active.
   - Modal interaction state prevents background movement and duplicate interaction activation while speech owns that area.

4. Discovery/reward feedback
   - Reward/discovery feedback is presented in the lower safe area rather than over the persistent top HUD.
   - When an interaction modal is active, non-essential feedback is coalesced/deferred until the conversation releases the lower area.

5. Starlight Beach HUD
   - Starlight Beach remains in the canonical exploration-shell scene set.
   - The persistent HUD overlay is explicitly kept topmost when lazily started exploration scenes would otherwise render above it.
   - No Beach-specific replacement HUD was introduced.

6. WP19E hand-off
   - WP19D does not add a temporary conversation return-position/checkpoint workaround.
   - Ordinary conversation consistency remains assigned to WP19E, which will keep normal NPC/quest dialogue inside the active world scene.
