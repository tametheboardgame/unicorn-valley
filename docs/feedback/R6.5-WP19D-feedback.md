# R6.5-WP19D feedback

## Scope

WP19D consolidates explicit world interactions into the shared semantic target registry and `WorldInteractionCoordinator`, while preserving automatic region crossings and existing authored action side effects.

## Browser remediation evidence

- World interaction keyboard edges are captured once by the shared coordinator so legacy scene movement adapters cannot consume `E`, Enter or Space before contextual actions run.
- Mobile contextual hints now use semantic target labels instead of retired instructional copy.
- Crystal Cascade keeps its enlarged 360 × 360 touch affordance through target presentation geometry without expanding interaction range or restoring a second activation route.
- Cottage tactile markers remain present for world/layout diagnostics but no longer own interaction input.
- Legacy visual objects such as the odd stone remain non-interactive; coordinator-owned direct zones provide their actionable hit areas when the player is eligible.
- Regression coverage asserts that enlarged direct-tap geometry cannot make an out-of-range target selectable.

## David review - 2026-09-11

The interaction foundation is provisionally successful, but the current candidate is not accepted for merge. David's initial live review found the following positive behaviour:

- the contextual Interact action is working well;
- roaming NPCs stop and turn towards the player during engagement;
- interactions cannot be triggered from an unreasonable distance;
- general NPC behaviour is materially improved.

The following remediation is required before another WP19D review candidate:

1. Desktop control ownership
   - Mobile movement controls are visible on laptop/desktop.
   - Desktop should retain contextual action buttons such as Interact, Enter, Talk or Start, but hide touch/mobile movement controls.

2. NPC physical collision
   - Other unicorns currently have no physical presence for collision purposes.
   - Player and NPC unicorns can pass directly through each other.
   - Add appropriate soft/physical collision or separation without making roaming NPC movement brittle.

3. Conversation panel placement
   - World speech/conversation boxes are partially obscured by the new top HUD/menu.
   - Reposition conversation presentation so it cannot sit beneath the top chrome. Bottom placement is the preferred direction unless another bounded safe area is demonstrably better.

4. Wonderbook discovery presentation
   - New-discovery notifications currently occupy the same overlapping top area.
   - Prefer a smaller contextual discovery treatment near the discovered object/place where practical, otherwise place it in a safe lower notification area.
   - It must not collide with the persistent HUD or conversation UI.

5. Starlight Beach HUD regression
   - The persistent top menu disappears in Starlight Beach.
   - Restore the same top-level HUD ownership and availability expected in other world scenes.

6. Return-position continuity after detailed conversations
   - Conversations that transition into a more detailed/full conversation scene often return the player to the entrance of the general area rather than the position where the NPC conversation began.
   - Preserve the exact or safely adjusted pre-conversation world position and facing/scene context when returning.

7. Interaction consistency
   - The new interaction system works, but the way conversations are initiated/presented is still inconsistent between characters.
   - Normalise equivalent NPC conversation entry behaviour through the shared interaction owner while preserving intentionally different authored conversation types.

## Review disposition

This is enough feedback for a bounded WP19D remediation pass. Do not require exhaustive testing of the current candidate before fixing the known defects above. The current candidate remains rejected for merge, while the WP19D interaction architecture itself remains accepted as the direction to continue.
