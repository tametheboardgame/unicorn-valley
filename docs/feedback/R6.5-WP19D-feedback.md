# R6.5-WP19D feedback

## Scope

WP19D consolidates explicit world interactions into the shared semantic target registry and `WorldInteractionCoordinator`, while preserving automatic region crossings and existing authored action side effects.

## Browser remediation evidence

- World interaction keyboard edges are captured once by the shared coordinator so legacy scene movement adapters cannot consume `E`, Enter or Space before contextual actions run.
- Mobile contextual hints now use semantic target labels instead of retired instructional copy.
- Crystal Cascade keeps its enlarged 360 × 360 touch affordance through target presentation geometry without expanding interaction range or restoring a second activation route.
- Cottage tactile markers remain present for world/layout diagnostics but no longer own interaction input.
- Legacy visual objects such as the odd stone remain non-interactive; coordinator-owned direct zones provide their actionable hit areas when the player is eligible.
- Regression coverage asserts that enlarged direct-tap geometry cannot make an out-of-range target selectable.

## David review - 2026-09-11

The interaction foundation is provisionally successful, but the current candidate is not accepted for merge. David's initial live review found the following positive behaviour:

- the contextual Interact action is working well;
- roaming NPCs stop and turn towards the player during engagement;
- interactions cannot be triggered from an unreasonable distance;
- general NPC behaviour is materially improved.

The following remediation is required before another WP19D review candidate:

1. Desktop control ownership
   - Mobile movement controls are visible on laptop/desktop.
   - Desktop should retain contextual action buttons such as Interact, Enter, Talk or Start, but hide touch/mobile movement controls.

2. NPC physical collision
   - Other unicorns currently have no physical presence for collision purposes.
   - Player and NPC unicorns can pass directly through each other.
   - Add appropriate soft/physical collision or separation without making roaming NPC movement brittle.

3. Conversation panel and lower-screen ownership
   - World speech/conversation boxes are partially obscured by the new top HUD/menu.
   - Conversation presentation should use the protected lower-screen speech area rather than the top chrome.
   - The contextual target/name/hint surface that normally occupies that lower area must disappear for the lifetime of the conversation. The conversation panel becomes the sole owner of that space while speech is active; target name/hint/action guidance must not remain visible underneath or alongside it.
   - On close, the contextual target/name/hint may return only after the close input has been released/consumed and only if the target is still eligible. This prevents visual overlap and accidental immediate re-trigger.

4. Wonderbook discovery presentation
   - New-discovery notifications currently occupy the same overlapping top area.
   - Prefer a smaller contextual discovery treatment near the discovered object/place where practical, otherwise place it in a safe lower notification area when no conversation owns that space.
   - It must not collide with the persistent HUD or active conversation UI.

5. Starlight Beach HUD regression
   - The persistent top menu disappears in Starlight Beach.
   - Restore the same top-level HUD ownership and availability expected in other world scenes.

6. Detailed-conversation return-position defect is superseded by the accepted conversation design
   - Current detailed/full conversation scenes return the player to the entrance of the general area rather than where the NPC conversation began.
   - Do not add a new ordinary-conversation return-point/checkpoint mechanism as the primary fix.
   - The accepted direction is to retire scene-changing/cutscene-style screens for ordinary NPC and quest conversations, so the player never leaves the active world scene merely to exchange dialogue. Eliminating the scene transition removes the normal return-position failure mode entirely.
   - Genuine scene-changing activities, interiors or gameplay modes may retain their existing explicit return-state handling where actually required; this decision is specifically about conversation-only scene transitions.

7. Interaction and conversation consistency
   - The new interaction system works, but conversation presentation is still inconsistent between characters.
   - Equivalent NPC conversations should enter one shared in-world conversation route from the shared interaction owner.
   - Normal NPC/quest dialogue should remain in the current world scene with the player and speaker visible. The player is interaction/movement locked while the conversation is active; the engaged NPC remains stopped and facing the player.
   - Short lines, multi-line dialogue, quest hand-ins and choices should all use the same conversation visual family and lower-screen speech space, expanding as required instead of launching a separate conversation cutscene scene.
   - Separate/full-screen scenes remain appropriate for actual activities or modes such as races, shops/interiors where required, creator flows and minigames, not simply because dialogue contains several lines.

## Review disposition

This is enough feedback for a bounded WP19D remediation pass. Do not require exhaustive testing of the current candidate before fixing the known defects above. The current candidate remains rejected for merge, while the WP19D interaction architecture itself remains accepted as the direction to continue.

The ordinary-conversation presentation redesign belongs to WP19E's approved conversation-system scope. WP19D should prepare clean interaction ownership and lower-screen suppression/locking contracts, but should not build a temporary return-position system that WP19E would immediately remove.

## Delivery gate

PR #164 remains draft and must not be merged or deployed until the complete CI/browser matrix is green and the WP19D user review hard stop is satisfied on a corrected candidate.
