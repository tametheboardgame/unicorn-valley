# R6-WP6.18 - Playthrough Status and Remediation Record

## Purpose

This document records the R6-WP6.18 human-playthrough gate, the end-to-end playthrough findings, the bounded remediation packages created from those findings, and the final release of the R6 hard stop.

It supplements `07F-R6-WP6.11-6.18-PRE-R7-DELIGHT-PHASE.md` and now records that the hard stop defined there has been satisfied.

## Current status

R6-WP6.11 through R6-WP6.18 are complete and merged.

R6-WP6.18 is **COMPLETE - HUMAN HARD STOP RELEASED**.

The first human playthrough produced remediation packages R6-WP6.18A/B through R6-WP6.18F. Follow-up mobile evidence produced R6-WP6.18G and R6-WP6.18H. A final mobile confirmation produced R6-WP6.18I/J. All are complete, merged and deployed.

The final human confirmation verified that:

- Crystal Cascade can now be entered reliably on mobile;
- the Rainbow Meadow/Crystal Brook connector paths now read as part of the native path network rather than differently coloured overlays;
- the previously reported high-impact mobile and map defects are resolved;
- remaining observations are small enough to defer to a later bounded tidy-up pass.

The user explicitly confirmed the R6 defects are fixed and released the R6-WP6.18 human gate.

**Important roadmap decision after release:** R7-WP7.1 is deliberately **not** starting yet. Human judgement after the completed R6 playthrough is that the game is not content-complete enough for preference-led observation to be reliable. There are not yet enough places, races, shop uses, quests, populated regions, unicorn residents and interactions for a child's apparent preference to represent a fair choice between mature play fantasies.

A mandatory **R6.5 - Valley Completeness and Breadth** release has therefore been inserted before R7. Detailed scope is recorded in `07V-R6.5-VALLEY-COMPLETENESS-BREADTH.md`.

R7 is now blocked by the R6.5-WP17 human breadth/readiness gate, not by any unresolved R6 defect.

## First playthrough findings

The first R6-WP6.18 playthrough identified the following issues:

- the Unicorn Creator was too cramped and difficult to read on a portrait mobile display;
- mobile interaction text was too small and `Talk to Pip` did not read clearly as an action;
- prototype circular NPC markers remained visible around production character art;
- point-and-click backwards movement could alternate the unicorn's facing direction every step;
- Sunbeam Village shopfronts still looked like prototype blocks and were not convincing places to enter;
- the Crystal Brook entrance did not read as a natural world route and sat too close to the race route;
- Crystal Brook and Whispering Woods needed stronger authored gateways/environment identity;
- Crystal Cascade felt structurally unwinnable during normal human play.

These findings were treated as defect evidence, not as permission to begin speculative expansion.

### R6-WP6.18D1 - Sunbeam Village shops and interiors

### R6-WP6.18D2 - Region gateways and environment art

Merged in PR #112.

- visually separated Crystal Brook travel from the race hub route;
- added a stronger Crystal Brook to Whispering Woods trail/threshold;
- replaced portal-like race access with a dedicated crystal race path/gate;
- strengthened Crystal Brook and Whispering Woods environment identity;
- preserved established traversal coordinates, save state and race semantics.

### R6-WP6.18E - Crystal Cascade human-winability balance

Merged in PR #113.

The playthrough finding exposed a genuine data defect: the reused NPC mistake tables referenced only Rainbow Run obstacle IDs, so Crystal Cascade NPCs effectively attempted the whole course without their intended mistakes.

The remediation:

- added Crystal Cascade obstacle IDs to the existing Nova, Clover and Breeze mistake data;
- left player speed, race physics, assistance settings and shortcut mechanics unchanged;
- proved through production-system simulation that a clean standard player can win without Prism Current;
- proved a competent one-mistake player still has a first-place route;
- added real-input browser validation requiring first place, zero obstacle hits and zero shortcut use for the clean route.

Prism Current therefore remains an optional advantage rather than a hidden requirement.

### R6-WP6.18F - Prototype NPC marker cleanup

Merged in PR #114.

- removed residual prototype circles/icons beneath production Willow, Marigold and Pebble art;
- preserved NPC names, interaction targeting, story state and collision;
- left deliberate portrait framing untouched;
- added browser regression coverage.

### Shared R6.18 CI gate hardening

Merged in PR #115.

This was test/infrastructure-only work required because slow CI timing was obscuring otherwise valid remediation changes.

- replaced synthetic mobile clicks with real touch/tap gestures where appropriate;
- separated transition hitch limits from settled frame-performance measurements;
- replaced fragile fixed-duration movement/race waits with bounded state-based waits;
- gave the complete Chromium suite a realistic CI job budget without weakening individual acceptance thresholds.

No production gameplay behaviour was changed by this package.

## Follow-up playthrough findings

A later mobile playthrough confirmed that a large proportion of the first remediation pass was working as intended. It found:

- Pebble still had a beige circle and rock icon visibly covering the production character;
- the Rainbow Meadow path to the Crystal Brook cave branched too late and crossed directly through the Ribbon Board area;
- old R5 destination sign labels remained visible behind the newer production cave, woodland and race-gate labels;
- mobile race controls still overlaid the race graphics and were too small and awkward for comfortable touch play.

These findings produced R6-WP6.18G and R6-WP6.18H.

## Final human confirmation findings

The next real mobile confirmation reported that the build was otherwise substantially improved, but exposed two final blockers:

1. **Crystal Cascade race entry** - the gate prompt appeared and the player could stand at the gate, but tapping did not reliably enter the race.
2. **Gateway path presentation** - the newer connector paths appeared as random differently coloured strips laid over the maps rather than as part of the surrounding path network.

These were treated as bounded final R6 defects rather than expansion work.

## Completed final remediation: R6-WP6.18I/J

Merged in PR #119.

- hid the high-layer differently coloured R6 connector-path graphics;
- preserved their authored routes and gateway destinations;
- redrew the Meadow connector using Rainbow Meadow's native path treatment;
- redrew the two Crystal Brook connectors using Crystal Brook's native path treatment;
- kept the replacements at the ground-path layer so they read as natural branches rather than overlays;
- preserved collisions, coordinates, transitions and race behaviour.

Detailed scope is recorded in `07U-R6-WP6.18I-J-FINAL-GATE-PATH-FIXES.md`.

## Final human gate result

**PASSED.**

The final required human check confirmed:

- tapping Crystal Cascade naturally starts the race on the tested mobile device;
- the Rainbow Meadow to Crystal Brook route now visually belongs to Rainbow Meadow's native path network;
- the Crystal Brook to Whispering Woods and Crystal Cascade routes now visually belong to Crystal Brook's native path network;
- no new blocker was found;
- remaining small observations can be captured into the later R6.5 tidy-up package rather than holding the release.

R6-WP6.18 is therefore closed.
