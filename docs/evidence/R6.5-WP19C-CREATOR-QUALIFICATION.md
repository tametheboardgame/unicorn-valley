## Drawn-bounds alignment correction

David's phone evidence and large-landscape recheck exposed the remaining root cause: isolated components were drawn with the attachment coordinates used by the complete unicorn and then assigned one fixed scale per component type. Those coordinates are intentionally asymmetric, and style extents differ substantially, so the nominal card centre was not the artwork centre. The preview similarly used a fixed origin and scale that did not account for the selected tail's leftmost extent. The final category positions were also centred at x=923 while the cream panel inner rectangle is centred at x=940.

The shared appearance renderer now owns catalogue-aware drawn bounds, including strokes and decorations, and a uniform post-draw fit transform. The landscape progressive owner supplies a dedicated inset art region above each label band and recomputes the fit whenever it creates cards; the scene preview owner applies the same mechanism to the complete silhouette. No mask conceals overflow. The progressive owner also centres the 3x2 tab group on the panel inner rectangle, moves it upward, and leaves a positive gap before the separate heading/card/swatch/footer regions. Browser diagnostics expose the calculated post-transform bounds so the sequential six-category journey asserts every six Mane, six Tail, six Horn, six Marking and eight Accessory render is contained and clear of its label.
