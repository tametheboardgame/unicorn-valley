## Delivered baseline

H3.11.4 rebuilt Story House as a walkable library and a reusable reading platform rather than a quest kiosk.

- **A - physical Story House:** polished walkable room, Quill as authoritative storykeeper, coherent shelves/furniture/interactions and preserved Story House progression.
- **B - content architecture:** lightweight catalogue metadata with independently addressable chapter/story assets and stable semantic block IDs.
- **C - reader engine:** responsive DOM long-form reader over the Phaser world with typography controls, chapter navigation and clean world-input suppression.
- **D - persistence:** schema-v9 independent reading positions for multiple books, global reader preferences, Continue Reading and completion/progress state.
- **E - illustration/media:** demand-loaded artwork, real *The Duck Bread Baker* content and a reusable `paged-picture-book` mode alongside the flowing prose reader.
- **F - library UX/discovery:** metadata-driven search/filter/category discovery, compact library surfaces, concise catalogue copy, scalable authoring guidance and final reader/browser polish.

## Final UX accepted on 26 September

The final library/reader review accepted:

- compact collapsible Search, Filters and Categories controls;
- Continue Reading and Valley Story Cards outside the scrolling catalogue shelf;
- compact library progress statistics;
- contained fixed-height book cards with two-line title, author, restrained badges, concise two-line blurb and pinned progress footer;
- an authored `catalogueBlurb` contract of about 80 characters with safe fallback for older content;
- equal-width Previous and Next reader controls;
- tap right gutter for next page and left gutter for previous page;
- swipe left for next page and swipe right for previous page;
- subtle page-turn feedback while retaining the visible navigation buttons.

All page-turn inputs use the same authoritative page/chapter path, so reading progress/resume state does not diverge by input method.

## Closeout regressions fixed

Two shared-system issues were exposed during final qualification and were corrected at the canonical owner rather than worked around inside Story House:

1. **Reader-close HUD click-through.** Closing the DOM reader could leak the same pointer action into the fixed exploration HUD and open Inventory/other navigation. `ExplorationHudOverlayScene` now hides/blocks during interaction modals and the source `ExplorationShell` launchers also respect the shared active/post-close interaction-suppression guard.
2. **Related CI interaction timing.** Existing dialogue/settings and Nova first-run tests were hardened to assert the current product contracts rather than stale timing assumptions. The product guard remained intact.
