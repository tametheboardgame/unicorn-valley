# R5-WP5.10 Final Tightening Pass

## Trigger

The final R5 human playtest confirmed that the major R5 remediation goals are working, but identified four small issues worth resolving before R6 begins.

This package is deliberately bounded. It is not an R6 production-art pass and must not expand into broad visual replacement work.

## Scope

### UI fit and alignment

- keep Firefly Lantern mode/difficulty choices fully inside their selector panel;
- keep suggestion-card controls and footer clearly inside their card;
- avoid changing underlying game progression or activity rules.

### Rain readability

- improve Gentle Rain contrast over light morning/afternoon outdoor palettes;
- keep the effect soft enough to remain child-friendly and non-obstructive;
- preserve the existing weather state model and discovery conditions.

### Wonderbook return position

- opening the Wonderbook from exploration must pause the current world scene;
- closing the Wonderbook must resume that exact scene rather than recreate it;
- player position and world presentation state must therefore be preserved.

### Cottage window alignment

- make the upper cottage area read as a wall rather than continuous floorboards;
- visually seat both windows on the wall with a clear sill/baseboard relationship;
- do not alter cottage collision, decoration slots or persistence.

## Technical boundaries

- no save-schema migration;
- no progression changes;
- no collision changes;
- no new R6 art assets;
- prefer presentation-only fixes except where the Wonderbook lifecycle requires a real scene-resume correction.

## Acceptance

- Firefly selector choices remain comfortably inside the selector panel;
- suggestion-card footer no longer collides with the lower border;
- Gentle Rain remains visible over the lightest current outdoor regions;
- closing the Wonderbook returns to the exact pre-book exploration position;
- cottage windows visually belong to the wall rather than the floor;
- formatting, lint, TypeScript, unit tests, build, static smoke and the full browser playtest suite pass;
- after deployment, one short human smoke check of these four findings closes the remaining R5 gate.
