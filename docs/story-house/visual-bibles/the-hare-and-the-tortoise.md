# The Hare and the Tortoise — modern Story House visual bible

Work package: **R6.5-WP19SH1.3.1**

## Purpose

Create a coherent modern illustration set for the existing Joseph Jacobs / Richard Heighway Story
House title without replacing or altering its historic public-domain illustration set.

The existing Heighway artwork remains the **Classic Illustrations** option. These generated images
become the **Modern Illustrations** option introduced by SH1.2A.

## Visual direction

- warm hand-painted children's storybook illustration;
- gouache/watercolour feel with soft visible texture;
- rounded, readable silhouettes and expressive but natural animal faces;
- sunny English-meadow / woodland-edge palette: fresh greens, warm ochres, cream, dusty blue;
- playful energy rather than slapstick humiliation;
- suitable for a young child;
- no typography, captions, logos, borders or UI baked into artwork;
- no clothing or anthropomorphic human accessories.

## Character continuity

### Hare

- adult brown hare;
- long upright ears with darker tips;
- warm chestnut/tawny coat with cream underside;
- lean, fast silhouette and long hind legs;
- confident and energetic, but not cruel or frightening;
- expressions may move from smug confidence → peaceful nap → startled realisation.

### Tortoise

- small land tortoise;
- rounded olive-brown shell with warm amber hexagonal markings;
- mossy olive head and legs;
- compact, low silhouette;
- calm, determined and quietly pleased;
- never made to look weak, distressed or comically helpless.

The same Hare and Tortoise designs must remain recognisable across every scene.

## Environment continuity

- one continuous meadow race route;
- soft dirt path running through grasses and small wildflowers;
- low hedges / scattered leafy trees;
- warm daylight with gentle cloud shapes;
- simple wooden finishing post/ribbon may appear only in the final scene;
- keep backgrounds supportive and uncluttered so the two characters remain readable at reader size.

## Modern illustration set

### 1. The challenge
- block: `the-challenge`
- landscape reader illustration;
- Hare confidently issuing the race challenge, standing tall and lively;
- Tortoise calmly accepting beside him;
- a few woodland/meadow animals may watch from a respectful distance;
- emotional read: boastful energy versus steady calm.

### 2. The race and the nap
- block: `the-race`
- landscape reader illustration;
- Hare asleep peacefully beside the course beneath a small tree or grassy bank;
- Tortoise steadily walking past on the race path;
- no embarrassment or aggression;
- clearly show that the race is continuing while Hare sleeps.

### 3. Plodding wins
- block: `plodding-wins`
- landscape reader illustration;
- Tortoise reaching/crossing a simple finish marker first;
- Hare approaching behind, surprised rather than devastated;
- celebratory but gentle;
- emotional read: persistence paid off.

## Final approved modern set

The final SH1.3.1 Modern Illustrations set was approved on 30 September 2026 and replaces the
temporary selector-test artwork in place through the same Google Drive source IDs.

Final scene mapping:

1. `the-challenge` — Hare confidently issues the challenge while Tortoise calmly accepts.
2. `the-race` — Hare naps beneath the tree while Tortoise overtakes him on the race path.
3. `plodding-wins` — Tortoise crosses the finish ribbon first as Hare arrives too late.

The three final images use the same Hare, Tortoise, meadow palette and painterly treatment so the
Modern Illustrations option reads as one coherent picture-book set.
