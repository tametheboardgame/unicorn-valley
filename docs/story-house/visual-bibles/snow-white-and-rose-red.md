# Snow-White and Rose-Red — modern Story House visual bible

Work package: **R6.5-WP19SH1.4.1**

## Purpose

Create one coherent four-image modern illustration set for **Snow-White and Rose-Red** and make it independently selectable against both the Story House retelling and the Full Classic Text.

The existing Otto Ubbelohde files remain the **Classic Illustrations** option. The generated set becomes **Modern Illustrations**.

## Visual direction

- warm hand-painted children's storybook illustration;
- soft gouache / watercolour texture with natural woodland detail;
- recurring-character consistency is the primary quality bar;
- cosy winter cottage interiors contrasted with luminous spring/summer forest scenes;
- magical transformation should feel wondrous rather than flashy;
- child-friendly handling of the dwarf confrontation;
- no baked-in typography, captions, logos, borders or UI.

## Character continuity

### Snow-White

- young girl / early teen;
- dark brown hair worn long and neat;
- cream blouse with a deep blue-green dress or pinafore;
- quieter, observant expressions;
- same face, hair and clothing identity across every scene.

### Rose-Red

- same age range as Snow-White;
- warm auburn / chestnut hair, slightly wilder;
- cream blouse with a deep rose-red dress or pinafore;
- more energetic posture and expressions;
- clearly recognisable as Snow-White's sister without looking identical.

### The bear / prince

Bear:
- very large black-brown bear;
- thick winter coat, gentle amber-brown eyes;
- imposing silhouette but calm, trustworthy expression.

Prince:
- same warm amber-brown eyes and facial warmth as the bear;
- young adult with dark brown hair;
- gold-accented forest-green clothing;
- transformation should make the visual connection between bear and prince obvious.

### The dwarf

- very small adult dwarf;
- long white beard;
- weathered face;
- muted brown / moss-green clothing;
- irritable rather than frightening;
- sacks / boxes of stolen treasure recur around him.

## Environment continuity

### Cottage

- small timber cottage beside the forest;
- stone hearth as the visual anchor;
- simple wooden furniture;
- warm amber firelight against snowy blue exterior light.

### Forest

- old mossy trees, ferns, brook and rocky clearings;
- treasure scenes use jewel colour sparingly so the sisters remain the focus;
- the final clearing should be recognisably the same place immediately before and after the transformation.

## Independent text / illustration contract

### Story House Edition

- existing Ubbelohde mapping becomes `classic`;
- generated four-image set becomes `modern`;
- default illustration set: `modern`.

### Full Classic Text

- existing Ubbelohde mapping becomes `classic`;
- the same generated files are reused with Full Classic page/block anchors;
- default illustration set: `classic`.

The reader must allow all four combinations:

1. Story House + Classic Illustrations;
2. Story House + Modern Illustrations;
3. Full Classic Text + Classic Illustrations;
4. Full Classic Text + Modern Illustrations.

Text and art choices remain independent; do not duplicate prose or generated image files.

## Human gate

Do not merge SH1.4.1 until all four modern illustrations have been viewed together in the real reader and Full Classic Text + Modern Illustrations has also been checked.
