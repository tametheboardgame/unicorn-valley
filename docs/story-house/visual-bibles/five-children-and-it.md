# Five Children and It — modern Story House visual bible

Work package: **R6.5-WP19SH1.5.2**

## Approved set

The approved modern set contains **13 illustrations** covering the Psammead discovery, the principal wish consequences, and the final farewell.

The existing H. R. Millar files remain the **Classic Illustrations** option. The generated set is the independently selectable **Modern Illustrations** option.

The Story House rewrite of Chapter 10 has its own modern image. That image is intentionally **not** mapped into Full Classic Text because the original chapter contains materially different dated racial and imperial content.

## Approved visual language

- bright dimensional / animated-storybook 3D rendering;
- warm Edwardian English countryside rather than Oz-style saturated fantasy;
- stable recurring faces, ages, clothing colours and silhouettes;
- magic changes the requested property only: wings, scale, architecture or apparent beauty;
- expressive comedy without making danger visually brutal;
- no collage layouts, baked-in captions, logos or UI in final repository assets.

## Recurring character anchors

- **Cyril:** tallest older boy; brown tweed clothes; confident posture.
- **Anthea:** older girl; blue dress and blue hair ribbon; practical and observant.
- **Robert:** younger brown-haired boy; green jacket/shorts; energetic and impulsive.
- **Jane:** youngest of the four older children; pink dress; openly expressive.
- **The Lamb:** blond toddler in cream clothing. When magically grown, he is a slight blond young man in a neat grey flannel suit, green tie and small black moustache.
- **Psammead:** squat sandy-brown furry body, large round belly, long independent eye stalks, heavy whiskers, broad hands and feet, permanently grumpy expression.

## Independent text / illustration contract

### Story House Edition

- H. R. Millar mapping is `classic`;
- generated 13-image set is `modern`;
- default illustration set: `modern`.

### Full Classic Text

- H. R. Millar mapping is `classic`;
- 12 generated images with genuinely matching scenes are available as `modern`;
- the rewritten Chapter 10 image is excluded rather than being misleadingly attached to the original text;
- default illustration set: `classic`.

## Human gate

The generated assets were individually reviewed and regenerated for cast, clothing and scale continuity. Final merge still requires the complete Story House sequence and a Full Classic Text + Modern Illustrations sample to be checked in the real reader.
