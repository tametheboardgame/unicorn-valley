# Thumbelina — modern Story House visual bible

Work package: **R6.5-WP19SH1.4.4**

## Purpose

Create one coherent six-image modern illustration set for **Thumbelina** and make it independently
selectable against both the Story House retelling and the Full Classic Text.

The existing Vilhelm Pedersen, Eleanor Vere Boyle and Bertall files remain the **Classic
Illustrations** option. The generated set becomes **Modern Illustrations**.

This title is the SH1 miniature-scale continuity test. The same tiny heroine must remain recognisable
while her surroundings change from a human room to river leaves, giant grass and snow, underground
burrows, a swallow's tunnel and finally a flower-sized community.

## Cross-edition constraint

The two text editions diverge materially at the ending.

- Story House: Thumbelina is welcomed by the flower people and chooses her future on her own terms.
- Full Classic Text: the flower prince proposes immediately, she accepts, is crowned and is renamed
  May Blossom.

The shared modern set therefore stops at the **arrival and welcome in the flower country**, before
marriage/crowning/renaming becomes visually canonical. Do not create one ending image that contradicts
either edition.

## Visual direction

- warm hand-painted children's storybook illustration;
- soft gouache / watercolour texture with luminous natural colours;
- miniature fantasy should come from scale, framing and environment, not glossy fairy CGI;
- Thumbelina remains human in appearance, simply extremely small;
- surrounding flowers, leaves, insects, birds and household objects should sell scale consistently;
- no baked-in typography, captions, logos, borders or UI;
- original compositions, not recreations of historical source illustrations.

## Thumbelina continuity

- tiny young woman, roughly thumb-height relative to an adult human;
- warm chestnut-brown hair, softly wavy, usually worn loose to the shoulders;
- expressive brown eyes;
- practical rose-petal / soft-pink dress with green leaf-like trim;
- simple flexible slippers;
- no crown and no wings until the very end of the Story House journey;
- same face, hair colour, dress palette and proportions throughout;
- keep her capable and alert rather than doll-like or helpless.

### Scale anchors

Use recurring size cues so her scale feels stable:

- walnut shell = bed-sized;
- tulip / lily leaf = platform or small boat-sized;
- butterfly = roughly pony-sized relative to Thumbelina;
- beetle = large flying animal relative to her;
- field mouse = much larger than her, but able to share the burrow;
- swallow = enormous enough to carry her comfortably;
- ordinary flower blossoms = room / pavilion-sized in the final land.

## Supporting character continuity

### Swallow

- barn-swallow-like bird with dark blue-black upper feathers, warm cream chest and subtle rust-red throat;
- same markings in tunnel, reunion and flight scenes;
- intelligent, gentle expression without humanisation;
- visibly enormous beside Thumbelina.

### Field mouse

- warm brown-grey fur;
- practical, kind face;
- cosy domestic burrow styling;
- no human clothing.

### Mole

- dark charcoal velvet-like fur;
- broad paws and small eyes;
- dignified, self-important posture rather than villainous appearance.

### Flower prince / flower people

- same miniature human scale as Thumbelina;
- delicate natural clothing made from petals, leaves and woven plant fibres;
- subtle translucent wings may appear on some flower people;
- prince can wear a small petal/seed crown in the final arrival scene;
- do not depict a wedding, coronation or name-change moment in the shared modern set.

## Environment continuity

### Human room

- warm wooden table by a window;
- flowerpot, bowl of water, books and household objects read as enormous architecture;
- walnut-shell bed and petals establish scale immediately.

### River

- broad lily/tulip leaf as raft;
- reeds tower like trees;
- dragonflies and butterflies feel like large flying creatures;
- bright summer water with strong reflections.

### Grassland / winter

- individual grass blades are tree-trunk scale;
- clover and daisies form canopy structures;
- autumn shifts to cold rain, then huge snowflakes and corn-stalk forests.

### Underground burrow / tunnel

- earth walls, roots and seed stores;
- warm mouse burrow contrasts with dark mole tunnels;
- the swallow should nearly fill the tunnel space to emphasise scale.

### Warm country

- marble fragments and columns read as ancient giant architecture;
- white flowers and broad leaves become rooms/platforms;
- rich warm air, oranges/grapes in the distance and bright clear sky.

## Miniature-scale continuity rules

This title should be judged as a scale sequence, not merely a character sequence.

- Thumbelina's apparent body size must remain stable relative to repeated natural cues.
- Ordinary objects must not randomly shrink to human scale around her.
- The swallow remains dramatically larger than Thumbelina in every appearance.
- Flowers in the final country should feel architectural without making Thumbelina fairy-sized in
  one image and ordinary child-sized in another.
- Keep the same dress palette and hair through all six images.
- No wings on Thumbelina before the final flower-country context, and preferably keep the shared
  arrival image pre-wing-gift so it remains neutral between editions.

## Independent text / illustration contract

### Story House Edition

- existing historical mapping becomes `classic`;
- generated six-image set becomes `modern`;
- default illustration set: `modern`.

### Full Classic Text

- existing historical mapping becomes `classic`;
- the same generated files are reused with matching Full Classic page/block anchors;
- default illustration set: `classic`.

The reader must continue to allow all four combinations:

1. Story House + Classic Illustrations;
2. Story House + Modern Illustrations;
3. Full Classic Text + Classic Illustrations;
4. Full Classic Text + Modern Illustrations.

Text and art choices remain independent; do not duplicate prose or generated image files.

## Human gate

Do not merge SH1.4.4 until all six generated images have been viewed together in the real reader,
Thumbelina's scale and appearance remain coherent across every environment, and Full Classic Text +
Modern Illustrations has also been checked.
