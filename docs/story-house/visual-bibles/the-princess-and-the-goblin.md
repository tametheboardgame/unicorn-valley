# The Princess and the Goblin — modern Story House visual bible

Work package: **R6.5-WP19SH1.5.3**

## Approved set
The approved modern set contains **15 illustrations** spanning Irene's discovery of the hidden tower, Curdie's investigations, the silver-thread rescue, the attack on the King's house, the underground flood and the quiet Story House closing scene.

Jessie Willcox Smith remains the **Classic Illustrations** option. The generated set is the independently selectable **Modern Illustrations** option. **What Irene Kept** is Story House-only because MacDonald's original ending does not directly stage that final tower visit.

## Visual language
- polished dimensional storybook / animated-film rendering;
- warm Victorian mountain interiors above ground;
- pale blue, silver, pearl and warm rose light in the hidden tower;
- blue/purple mineral caverns contrasted with amber goblin torchlight;
- restrained silver-thread magic;
- no collage layouts, panel numbers, baked-in captions, logos or UI.

## Character anchors
- **Irene:** eight; golden hair; blue eyes; pale-blue dress; cream shoes; sky-blue cloak when required.
- **Curdie:** about twelve; messy dark-brown hair; pale miner complexion; brown/charcoal miner clothes; cap; sturdy boots.
- **Great-great-grandmother:** smooth youthful face; wise eyes; immense snow-white hair; pale-blue/silver clothing; spinning-wheel room and white pigeons.
- **King-Papa:** dark hair; long dark beard streaked silver; navy/royal-blue and gold; white horse.
- **Goblin King:** broad and squat with an oversized hard-looking head.
- **Goblin Queen:** taller and sharper; pale skin; dark red/black royal clothing; heavy stone shoes.
- **Goblin creatures:** impossible domesticated hybrids with mixed familiar traits.

## Human gate
All 15 assets were reviewed for character continuity, costume continuity, goblin design, scene accuracy, scale and standalone landscape format. Final merge requires a complete Story House visual pass and a Full Classic + Modern sample.
