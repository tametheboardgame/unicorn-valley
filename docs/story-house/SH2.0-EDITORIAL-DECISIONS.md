# SH2.0 — Editorial decision and sign-off sheet

**Status:** proposed for David's review, 16 October 2026. **PR:** [#329](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/unicorn-valley/pull/329). This is a decision log, not a legal warranty or an image-acceptance form.

## Recommended defaults for SH2.0 human gate

| Gate | Recommended decision | Reason and follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Production sequence** | Accept approved order **Benjamin Bunny → Goldilocks → Velveteen Rabbit → Little Red Riding Hood → Emperor's New Clothes → Snow Queen → Wind in the Willows → Secret Garden → Missing Moonflower → Rainbow Meadow Mystery** | Short tales establish a repeatable production cadence before longer editions; originals conclude the series slate; keep independent per-title acceptance |
| **2. Text/edition** | Every title gets an **original, newly authored Story House Edition**. Produce optional complete Full Classic Text **only** where a complete, specifically attributed edition has separately cleared text/translation rights; do not force two editions | The user-facing source text must not mislabel abridgement as original classic; classic cover options are independent from text edition |
| **3. Goldilocks** | Prefer simple child-friendly modern adaptation in the established **Goldilocks-and-three-bears** tradition; select **Katharine Pyle's historical variant** as initial textual comparison, with Brooke's Goldenlocks as optional period contrast; no Full Classic now | Different versions and historical protagonists/wording require deliberate final source selection, not a made-up anonymous 'original' |
| **4. Little Red Riding Hood** | Prefer **Grimm-lineage rescue ending** in *newly written* child-friendly prose, with clear adaptation credit and historical source comparison; do not copy an unattributed 1922 translation | Perrault's different ending may be disturbing for the target child; later illustrated edition rights independent |
| **5. Visual style** | Adopt existing rich painterly Story House language, chapter-centred landscape illustrations, approved reader art as the 3:4 catalogue cover, 2–6 human-reviewed anchors per title | Preserves Alice/Peter/Lantern continuity, avoids gratuitous extra cover generation and visual drift |
| **6. Artwork budget** | Indicative image counts per title **5–7, 4–5, 6–9, 4–6, 4–6, 15–22, 20–30, 24–36, 10–15, 10–15**; generate in small approved batches, revising only on evidence | Flexible budget, not a promise to generate every possible scene; scope control and responsive browsing |
| **7. Original books** | *Missing Moonflower*: Rowan and Quill solve a **non-quest-flower** moonlight puzzle without changing Willow's collection. *Rainbow Meadow Mystery*: a windmill-bell/pond/wind pattern involving Breeze without granting Lookout progression | Maintains game and Book 1 continuity; scripts and new character anchor art require later explicit approval |
| **8. Rights and distribution** | UK-first evaluation. Treat Gutenberg/book archives as **candidate sources, not automatic worldwide licences**; protect mixed-text/image editions, especially later Wind in the Willows, Andersen translations and uncertain Goldilocks/Red translations | The exact underlying edition, later images, translation, new generated art, rights-holder and intended market require independent title-level clearance |
| **9. Approval approach** | Approve this **foundation and proposed direction** as a package, while keeping individual SH2.n manuscript, precise rights, visual anchors, preview and publication gates | Keeps acceptance meaningful and avoids converting a roadmap decision into an unreviewed release |
