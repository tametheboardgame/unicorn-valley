# SH2.0 — Source, translation and rights candidate register

**Checked:** 16 October 2026. **Scope:** eight classic/traditional titles, two original Valley stories. **Status:** researching — not a legal sign-off or publication licence.

## Rights decision method

The expected first distribution territory is **United Kingdom**; exact future distribution countries, platform licences and distribution terms must be confirmed before reuse. The UK government's copyright guidance describes the **usual** term for literary and artistic works as 70 years after the author's death, calculated from the end of the death year, with exceptions (including previously unpublished works). Separate typographical arrangements can carry 25 years' protection. Original underlying story, **specific translation/revision**, period illustrations, contemporary editions, book-layout files, newly generated illustrations and final shelf cover are **independent** rights objects. See [GOV.UK duration guidance](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/how-long-copyright-lasts) and [Copyright notice: duration](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/copyright-notice-duration-of-copyright-term/copyright-notice-duration-of-copyright-term).

Project Gutenberg labels describe US public-domain findings; these **do not certify UK or worldwide clearance**, permission for a later illustration set or modern adaptation, or the right to reproduce Gutenberg-branded distribution material. Use a verifiable historical source edition or legitimately free transcription, record its exact bibliographic credits/physical source and distribution terms, and have the title's source sign-off explicitly recorded. If the full text or translation has not been verified, a `Full Classic Text` edition is **prohibited** until the gap is resolved. No rights object below is currently signed off for publication.

**Status vocabulary:** `candidate` (identified bibliographic source; not finally vetted), `needs-edition-selection` (multiple materially different versions), `authored-in-future` (original work not yet written/approved), `hold` (legal ambiguity requiring evidence), `cleared` (only after documented per-territory review or licence). These research statuses are **not** runtime manifest rights values; do not convert them to `public-domain` or `original` before acceptance.

## Specific high-risk source decisions

1. **Gutenberg #27805 is not a general-use UK illustrated-art clearance.** It expressly credits Paul Bransom. If the Full Classic text for *The Wind in the Willows* is later imported, use the selected original text-only edition independently of protected drawings; verify the exact text and copy/marking terms.
2. **Andersen translations** are new expressive works by their translators. A story first published in the 1830s/1840s does not clear an unattributed translation. Resolve translator, publication and jurisdiction *before* using any historical English wording as an edition.
3. **Red Riding Hood/Goldilocks** require deliberate variant choice, exact prose source, consistent ending and matching human-readable metadata. The programme's 'Story House Edition' is a new retelling, not falsely labelled 'complete historic'.
4. **Full Classic is optional** for all eight classics and absent for the two original Valley stories. An unverified classic edition may be omitted entirely while a lawful original retelling is prepared, but not until rights/source and editorial approvals are actually recorded.
5. **Modern reader illustrations** are visually original, generated/staged through the existing controlled asset pipeline; record source and permissions per file. Never reproduce the composition of a later film illustration to 'match a classic'.
6. **Different territories:** UK is the provisional home jurisdiction, not world rights clearance. Assess markets and distribution modalities before any external/public release.
7. **Unrelated legacy uncertainty:** *The Duck Bread Baker* issue [#326](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/unicorn-valley/issues/326) remains open; do not imply SH2.0 resolves it.
